## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose of the Quality Manual

This Quality Manual constitutes the summary document of the Quality Management System (QMS) of Plus Sarl, in compliance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

It presents:
- the organization of the company and its context
- the quality policy and quality objectives
- the process map
- the provisions taken to satisfy each requirement of the standard

This document is intended to:
- serve as an internal reference for the Managing Director
- present the QMS to the certification body SQS
- communicate the quality organization to interested parties

### 1.2 Normative reference

- **ISO 9001:2015** — Quality management systems — Requirements
- **ISO 9000:2015** — Quality management systems — Fundamentals and vocabulary

### 1.3 Document convention

> **Legend:** 🔴 [TO BE COMPLETED] = mandatory, missing | 🟡 [RECOMMENDED] = recommended | 🟢 = already completed | 🔵 [TO BE VERIFIED] = to be confirmed

## Chapter 4 — Context of the Organization

### 4.1 Understanding the organization and its context

Plus Sarl has identified the internal and external issues relevant to its strategic direction and likely to influence its ability to achieve the intended results of its QMS.

#### External issues

- Dependence on Chinese industrial partners for tooling and production
- Deadlines and logistics constraints of international transport
- Customs and regulatory requirements for import/export
- Variations in production costs, transport costs and raw materials
- High customer expectations in terms of quality, technical conformity and deadline compliance

#### Internal issues

- One-person organization requiring strong priority management and planning
- Need for fluid and continuous communication with customers, suppliers and logistics partners
- Maintaining a high level of documentary, technical and organizational control
- Ability to guarantee responsiveness, confidentiality and lasting customer satisfaction

#### SWOT Analysis

The complete SWOT analysis (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats) is documented in **CTX-QUA-001 §2** and is reviewed at least once a year during the management review.

**Key points:**

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Technical expertise in injection mould development | One-person organization: limited human resources |
| Flexibility and responsiveness (micro-enterprise) | Dependence on two Chinese partners |
| Long-standing trust relationship with Yuyao Mould Factory (since 2007) | Geographical distance from production |
| In-depth knowledge of the European market | No in-house production capacity |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| ISO 9001 certification via SQS = access to new markets | Failure or unavailability of a Chinese partner |
| Continual improvement of customer satisfaction | Significant delays in international transport |
| Supplier diversification in Asia (Oukailuo in place) | Major quality defects on manufactured products |

### 6.2 Quality objectives and planning of actions

Five quality objectives are defined for the year 2026:

| # | Objective | Indicator | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivery deadline compliance | % of orders delivered on time | >= 95% |
| 2 | Conformity of delivered products | No. of NCs per customer per year | Max 3 NCs/customer/year |

Plus Sarl ensures that any change likely to impact conformity, deadlines, commercial conditions or the organization is analyzed, planned and controlled before implementation.

**Process:**
1. Impact analysis by relevant process (O1-O4)
2. Customer information and validation if necessary
3. Controlled implementation (trials or first implementation)
4. Update of documents and integration of risks
5. Review during the management review

*(cf. CTX-QUA-001 §6)*

## Chapter 8 — Operational Activities Realization

### 8.1 Operational planning and control

Operational activities are planned and controlled through the 4 operational processes (O1 to O4) and the main flow described in the process map.

**Main flow (12 steps):**

1. Reception of the customer request (O1)
2. Preliminary analysis and order review (O1/O2/O4) — including impact analysis if modification (clause 6.3)
3. Consultation of industrial partners (O1/O2/O3)
4. Validation and recording in FileMaker (O1/S1)
5. Technical coordination (O2)
6. Manufacturing launch (O2)
7. Pre-shipment quality control (O4)
8. Logistics preparation (O3)
9. Transport monitoring (O3)
10. Confirmation of reception by the customer (O3/O1)
11. Invoicing and follow-up (O1)
12. Traceability and improvement (S1)

*(cf. CRT-QUA-001 §3)*

### 8.2 Requirements for products and services

#### Determination of requirements (clause 8.2)

Plus Sarl determines **6 types of requirements** applicable to each order:
1. Technical specifications (drawings, materials, tolerances)
2. Quantities and deadlines
3. Legal and regulatory requirements (customs, standards)
4. Applicable standards
5. Delivery conditions
6. Implicit requirements (packaging, marking, certificates)

Plus Sarl reserves the **right to refuse** an order outside its area of competence (clause 8.1). 🟢

#### Order review

Each customer request (order, quotation, modification, complaint) is subject to a preliminary analysis:

| Aspect | Process |
|---|---|
| Understanding of needs | O1 — Commercial |
| Determination of 6 types of requirements | O1 — Commercial |
| Technical and logistics feasibility | O2 — Purchasing & subcontracting |
| Quality requirements | O4 — Quality control |

After validation: recording in FileMaker, acknowledgment of receipt to the customer.

Any modification is subject to analysis, partner validation if necessary, customer confirmation by email and FileMaker update.

*(cf. CTX-QUA-001 §1.3, M1-DIR-001 §6)*

### 8.3 Design and development — EXCLUDED

Plus Sarl does not design the products. The designs, specifications, patents and specifications belong to the customers or are defined by the industrial partners. This exclusion is justified in DOM-QUA-001 §3.

### 8.4 Control of externally provided processes, products and services

Production is entirely outsourced to Chinese partners. Control is based on:

- **Selection** based on **7 criteria**: technical capacity, quality, responsiveness, reliability, price, communication, flexibility 🟢

- **Evaluation** based on **4 indicators**: deadline compliance, parts conformity, relationship stability, responsiveness 🟢
- **Control**: pre-shipment quality control by the partner, validated samples
- **Tooling validation**: every new mould validated on sample before series launch 🟢
- **Confidentiality**: customer intellectual property protected by Quality Agreements 🟢

**Formalized Quality Agreements:**
- **FOR-AQF-001** — Quality Agreement with Hardeng Yuyao Mould Factory (10 sections) 🟢

Coordination of production and logistics service provision follows processes O2 and O3:

**Process O2 — Purchasing and Subcontracting:**
- Transmission of orders and technical requirements to partners

In addition: sending of samples to Plus Sarl in parallel with transport. Documentary follow-up maintained on FileMaker. Analysis of customer feedback after delivery. 🟢

*(cf. FOR-CTR-001)*

4. **Recording** in the NC register FileMaker 🔵 [TO BE VERIFIED — register to be set up]

### 10.1 General

### 10.2 Nonconformity and corrective action

**Identification of NCs:**
- during controls by the industrial partner
- upon reception of goods by the customer
- following a complaint

**Treatment process (clause 10.2):**
1. Root cause analysis
2. Identification of the type of cause (5 types: production defect, human error, labeling, logistics, clarification)
3. Appropriate corrective action (5 types: production correction, QC reinforcement, labeling rework, logistics adaptation, communication improvement)
4. Implementation of corrective actions
5. Verification of their effectiveness
6. Integration into quality monitoring, internal audit and management review

**Corrective actions register** by category (suppliers, carriers, customer) in FileMaker. If recurrence of an identical NC: in-depth analysis and reinforced actions. 🟢

**NC recorded in 2026:** NC_2026_1001 — labeling error on shipment SHIP_25058 / article CFM00057428 / reference 90.60.05710 (1000 parts with incorrect label). Action: reinforcement of labeling control. 🟢

*(cf. PRO-NCF-001, PRO-ACR-001, FOR-RCL-001)*

### 10.3 Continual improvement

Continual improvement (clause 10.3) targets 5 objectives: reduce NCs, optimize deadlines, strengthen quality, improve customer satisfaction, make the logistics chain more reliable.

**Improvement triggers:**
- Detected or recurring nonconformity
- Observed delivery delay
- Customer return or complaint
- Quality indicators below targets
- **Visits to China** — major improvement lever (formalization of production monitoring, on-site quality control, strengthening partner relationships) 🟢

The effectiveness of improvements is verified against 4 criteria: disappearance of the problem, improvement of indicators, satisfaction of parties, absence of recurrence.

Improvement decisions are consolidated during the annual management review and integrated into the planning of quality objectives.

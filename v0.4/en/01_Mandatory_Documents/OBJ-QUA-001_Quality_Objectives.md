## 1. Annual quality objectives

The quality objectives of Plus Sarl are defined in alignment with the quality policy and the industrial coordination activities between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners (Yuyao Mould Factory and **Whang**, Yuyao). These objectives have been confirmed and reflect the four strategic axes of the company. Certification is pursued through the certification body **SQS**. 🟢

## 3. Review of objectives

The quality objectives are reviewed:
- **Quarterly**: monitoring of indicators, identification of trends, corrective actions if necessary
- **Annually**: full review during the management review (M3), adjustment of targets for the following year

The results are presented during the management review and serve as the basis for planning continual improvement actions.

## 1. Annual quality objectives

The quality objectives of Plus Sarl are defined in consistency with the quality policy and the industrial coordination activities between European customers and Chinese manufacturing partners (Yuyao Mould Factory and **Whang**, Yuyao). These objectives have been confirmed and reflect the company's four strategic axes. Certification is aimed at with the **SQS** certification body. 🟢

### Objective 2 - Conformity of delivered products

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Limit the number of customer non-conformities and react quickly in case of NC |
| **Consistent with** | Quality policy - Commitment n.3 (effective coordination of industrial partners to ensure quality) and Commitment n.5 (non-conformity management) |
| **Indicator** | Number of customer non-conformities (NC) per year, per customer |
| **Complementary indicator** | Compliance correction or replacement delay |
| **Current value** | 1 NC recorded in Q1 2026: NC_2026_1001 - labeling error on shipment SHIP_25058 / item CFM00057428 / reference 90.60.05710 (1000 pieces with incorrect label) 🟢 |
| **Target** | Maximum 3 NC per customer per year 🟢 |
| **Replacement delay target** | Replacement carried out within a timeframe consistent with the need expressed by the customer 🟢 |
| **Responsible** | Roxane Wicky (Manager) |
| **Resources** | Non-conformity forms, NC register, coordination with Yuyao Mould Factory and Whang, tracking in FileMaker |
| **Deadline** | 31/12/2026 |
| **Tracking method** | Systematic recording of each NC in the register, monitoring of the number of NCs per customer, quarterly summary |

- Quality control ensured by the manufacturer before shipment

## 3. Objectives review

The quality objectives are reviewed:
- **Quarterly**: tracking of indicators, identification of trends, corrective actions if necessary
- **Annually**: complete review during the management review (M3), adjustment of targets for the following year

The results are presented during the management review and serve as a basis for planning continuous improvement actions.

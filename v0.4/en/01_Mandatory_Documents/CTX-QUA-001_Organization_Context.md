## 1. Company Presentation

### General Description

Plus Sarl is a Swiss company founded in 2007, with its headquarters located at Route de Montet 11, 1588 Cudrefin, Switzerland. The company, incorporated as a Sarl, has 1 employee (the managing director) and 3 shareholders: Roxane Wicky, Olav Wicky and Capucine Wicky.

Plus Sarl works with two Chinese manufacturing partners:
- **Yuyao Mould Factory**: main and historical partner 🟢
- **Whang** (Yuyao, China — specialized in screws): second production partner 🟢

The company has approximately **10 active customers**. 🟢

### 1.1 Activities and Organization of Outsourced Production

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.1 — Quality Manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

Plus Sarl acts as the interface between customers located primarily in Europe and industrial manufacturers located in China for the design, development and production of industrial parts.

Plus Sarl does not directly perform production, which is carried out by industrial partners located in China. The company coordinates this production to ensure that parts comply with the technical and quality requirements of customers.

Parts are designed according to designs belonging to customers and remain their intellectual property. From these designs, the tooling and molds necessary for manufacturing are developed, managed and stored in China at Plus Sarl's industrial partners.

In addition, Plus Sarl offers sourcing and procurement services for existing parts on the market from external suppliers.

Monitoring is managed to ensure continuous control of production progress through the various processes:

#### 01: Sales Process

Creation of an order in the FileMaker system including:
- validated customer order specifying quantities, references, deadlines, specific needs
- information transmitted to manufacturers by Plus Sarl, or directly by the customer in the context of tripartite exchanges (customer – Plus Sarl – supplier)
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (e-mail, WeChat, etc.)
- order traceability ensured in the FileMaker system

#### 02: Purchasing & Subcontracting

Creation of a production sheet in the FileMaker system including:
- expected product characteristics
- manufacturing deadline
- necessary production sheet updates
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (e-mail, WeChat, etc.)
- sending of samples to customers and Plus Sarl when necessary for production validation

#### 03: Logistics & Delivery

Creation of a delivery sheet in the FileMaker system including:
- shipping deadlines and conditions
- creation of customs documents compliant with customer orders and European and Swiss legislation
- shipment monitoring until final delivery

#### 04: Quality Control

Before shipping the goods, Plus Sarl ensures that the produced parts comply with:
- the technical requirements defined by the customer, production identical to the recorded order 🔵 [TO VERIFY — to be defined with China how]
- the recorded and validated order

This verification is based on:
- quality control performed by the manufacturer 🔵 [TO VERIFY — control sheet to be formalized with YAN]
- documentary monitoring of quality controls performed by the industrial partner and stored on FileMaker
- analysis of customer feedback after delivery by customer confirmation e-mail
- receipt and processing of any non-conformity information

*Associated document: PRO-NCF-001 — Non-conformity Management*

In case of identified non-conformity:
- creation of a non-conformity sheet
- decision to accept, replace or correct the goods according to the analysis performed
- cause analysis and investigation
- implementation of corrective actions to prevent recurrence

*Associated document: PRO-ACR-001 — Corrective Actions Procedure*

#### S1: Document Management — Document Control and Continual Improvement

Information related to production and conformity is stored:
- in the FileMaker system
- in e-mail and WeChat exchanges

These elements ensure traceability of operations and feed:
- non-conformity monitoring
- internal audit
- management review

enabling continual improvement of the quality management system.

### 1.2 Customer Relationship and Understanding of Needs

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.2 — Quality Manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

Plus Sarl selects its customers in accordance with its strategy aimed at responding in a comprehensive and personalized manner to all of their needs.

This approach ensures:
- strong responsiveness in case of emergency
- precise consideration of technical specifications and quality requirements
- sustainable support in the development, improvement and procurement of industrial parts

Plus Sarl's customers are primarily European companies, owners of the designs, patents or technical specifications of the manufactured products.

The established customer relationship is based on:
- direct and continuous communication
- detailed understanding of technical and logistical needs
- permanent adaptation to deadline, quality and product evolution constraints

This organization enables Plus Sarl to guarantee control of customer requirements from the expression of need to the delivery of industrial parts.

### 1.3 Analysis and Validation of Customer Requests

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.2.1 — Quality Manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

The company favors a lasting relationship with a limited number of customers based on trust, responsiveness and in-depth understanding of their technical and operational needs. This proximity enables:
- efficient management of urgent requests
- rapid adaptation to changes
- continuous support in development and optimization

This approach is part of a long-term partnership logic.

**Receipt of requests:** Any request — whether it is an order, a quote request, a planned modification or a complaint — is received primarily by e-mail to the sales department and exceptionally by telephone in case of emergency.

**Preliminary analysis:** Each request is subject to a preliminary analysis carried out according to the responsibilities defined according to the M1 Leadership organization chart (see M1-DIR-001), in order to verify:

| Element to verify | Responsible process |
|---|---|
| Understanding of needs expressed by the customer | Process 01 — Sales |
| Product references, quantities, deadlines | Process 01 — Sales |
| Technical and logistical feasibility | Process 02 — Purchasing & Subcontracting |
| Applicable quality requirements | Process 04 — Quality Control |

**Consultation with industrial partners:** When necessary, Plus Sarl consults industrial partners to confirm with the relevant managers:

| Element to confirm | Responsible process |
|---|---|
| Production feasibility and conditions | Process 01 — Sales |
| Manufacturing deadlines and conditions | Process 02 — Purchasing & Subcontracting |
| Delivery deadlines and conditions | Process 03 — Logistics |

> **Order review:** This step constitutes the order review ensuring that customer requirements are clearly defined, understood and achievable before any acceptance.

**After validation:**
- information is recorded in the FileMaker system, ensuring order traceability
- an order acknowledgment is transmitted to the customer, confirming quantities, price and estimated deadline

**Subsequent modifications:** Any modification requested by the customer is subject to:
- analysis of the request according to the assigned role (see M1-DIR-001 — Leadership)
- validation by the industrial partner if necessary
- formal confirmation by customer e-mail before implementation
- update in the FileMaker system

> This organization guarantees control of customer requirements, traceability of decisions and security of order acceptance.

- Dependence on Chinese industrial partners for tooling and production

## 6. Planning of Changes (clause 6.3)

> **Source:** UPDATE 6.3 — Planning of changes (20/02/2026) 🟢

Plus Sarl ensures that any change likely to have an impact on:
- product conformity
- production or delivery deadlines
- commercial conditions
- process organization

is analyzed, planned and controlled before its implementation.

### 6.1 Types of Changes Concerned

Changes may relate in particular to:
- introduction of a new industrial partner
- change of supplier or carrier
- product or tooling modification
- evolution of logistics arrangements
- internal organizational evolution

### 6.2 Impact Analysis by Process

Before implementation, the change is evaluated according to the processes concerned:

| Process | Impact analysis |
|---|---|
| **O1 — Sales** | Analysis of impact on customer requirements and customer validation if necessary |
| **O2 — Purchasing & industrial coordination** | Analysis of technical feasibility and production impacts |
| **O3 — Logistics** | Analysis of deadline impacts and shipping conditions |
| **O4 — Quality** | Analysis of risks on product conformity and definition of adapted controls if necessary |

### 6.3 Implementation and Follow-up

Depending on the nature of the planned change, Plus Sarl:
- evaluates the potential consequences on parts conformity, delivery deadlines and commercial conditions
- systematically informs the concerned customer
- requests customer validation when the modification concerns a supplier, a production or a specific part
- carries out, when relevant, trials or a first controlled implementation, in particular for transport-related changes
- updates the applicable documents and information
- integrates identified risks into the monitoring of the concerned processes

### 6.4 Review and Improvement

Changes are reviewed during the management review to evaluate their effectiveness and their impact on the quality management system.

The Managing Director retains responsibility for the final decision and ensures the integration of changes into the continual improvement of the organization in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 standard.

These provisions ensure that changes are implemented in a controlled manner, transparent for the customer and compliant with QMS requirements.

## 1. Company presentation

### General description

Plus Sarl is a Swiss company founded in 2007, with its registered office at Route de Montet 11, 1588 Cudrefin, Switzerland. The company, incorporated as a Sarl (limited liability company), has 1 employee (the Managing Director) and 3 partners: Roxane Wicky, Olav Wicky and Capucine Wicky.

Plus Sarl works with two Chinese manufacturing partners:
- **Yuyao Mould Factory**: main and longstanding partner 🟢
- **Whang** (Yuyao, China — specializing in screws): second production partner 🟢

The company has approximately **10 active clients**. 🟢

### 1.1 Activities and organization of outsourced production

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.1 — Quality manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

Plus Sarl provides the interface between clients located primarily in Europe and industrial manufacturers located in China for the design, development and production of industrial parts.

Plus Sarl does not carry out production directly; this is performed by industrial partners located in China. The company coordinates this production in order to guarantee the conformity of parts to the technical and quality requirements of its clients.

The parts are designed according to designs belonging to the clients and remain their intellectual property. Based on these designs, the tooling and molds required for manufacturing are developed, managed and stored in China at Plus Sarl's industrial partners' facilities.

In addition, Plus Sarl offers sourcing and procurement services for existing parts available on the market from external suppliers.

Monitoring is managed to ensure continuous control of production progress through the various processes:

#### 01: Commercial Process

Creation of an order in the FileMaker system comprising:
- validated customer order specifying quantities, references, lead times, specific requirements
- information transmitted to manufacturers by Plus Sarl, or directly by the client within the framework of tripartite exchanges (client – Plus Sarl – supplier)
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (email, WeChat, etc.)
- order traceability ensured in the FileMaker system

#### 02: Purchasing & Subcontracting

Creation of a production sheet in the FileMaker system comprising:
- expected product characteristics
- the manufacturing lead time
- necessary updates to the production sheet
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (email, WeChat, etc.)
- sending of samples to clients and to Plus Sarl when necessary for production validation

#### 03: Logistics & Delivery

Creation of a delivery sheet in the FileMaker system comprising:
- shipping lead times and conditions
- creation of customs documents compliant with customer orders and European and Swiss legislation
- shipment monitoring through to final delivery

#### 04: Quality Control

Before shipment of goods, Plus Sarl ensures that the produced parts are conforming:
- to the technical requirements defined by the client, production identical to the recorded order 🔵 [TO BE VERIFIED — to be defined with China as to how]
- to the recorded and validated order

This verification is based on:
- quality control carried out by the manufacturer 🔵 [TO BE VERIFIED — control sheet to be formalized with YAN]
- documentary monitoring of quality controls carried out by the industrial partner and retained in FileMaker
- analysis of customer returns after delivery by confirmation email from the client
- receipt and processing of any nonconformity information

*Associated document: PRO-NCF-001 — Nonconformity management*

In the event of an identified nonconformity:
- creation of a nonconformity report
- decision to accept, replace or correct the goods based on the analysis carried out
- root cause analysis and investigation
- implementation of corrective actions to prevent recurrence

*Associated document: PRO-ACR-001 — Corrective action procedure*

#### S1: Document Management — Document Control and Continual Improvement

Information related to production and conformity is retained:
- in the FileMaker system
- in email and WeChat exchanges

These elements ensure the traceability of operations and feed into:
- nonconformity monitoring

enabling continual improvement of the Quality Management System.

### 1.2 Customer relations and understanding of needs

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.2 — Quality manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

Plus Sarl selects its clients in accordance with its strategy aimed at comprehensively and personally meeting all their needs.

This approach ensures:
- a high level of responsiveness in case of emergency
- precise consideration of technical specifications and quality requirements
- lasting support in the development, improvement and supply of industrial parts

Plus Sarl's clients are primarily European companies, owners of the designs, patents or technical specifications of the manufactured products.

The established client relationship is based on:
- direct and continuous communication
- detailed understanding of technical and logistics needs
- continuous adaptation to constraints of lead time, quality and product evolution

This organization enables Plus Sarl to guarantee control of customer requirements from the expression of need through to the delivery of industrial parts.

### 1.3 Analysis and validation of customer requests

> **Source:** UPDATE 2.2.1 — Quality manual (18/02/2026) 🟢

The company favours a lasting relationship with a limited number of clients based on trust, responsiveness and a thorough understanding of their technical and operational needs. This closeness enables:
- effective management of urgent requests
- rapid adaptation to changes
- continuous support in development and optimization

This approach is part of a long-term partnership logic.

**Receipt of requests:** Any request — whether it concerns an order, a quotation request, a planned modification or a complaint — is received primarily by email at the sales department and exceptionally by telephone in case of emergency.

**Prior analysis:** Each request is subject to a prior analysis carried out according to the responsibilities defined in the M1 Leadership organization chart (cf. M1-DIR-001), in order to verify:

| Element to verify | Responsible process |
|---|---|
| Understanding of needs expressed by the customer | Process 01 — Commercial |
| Product references, quantities, lead times | Process 01 — Commercial |
| Technical and logistics feasibility | Process 02 — Purchasing & Subcontracting |
| Applicable quality requirements | Process 04 — Quality Control |

**Consultation with industrial partners:** When necessary, Plus Sarl consults the industrial partners in order to confirm with the responsible parties:

| Element to confirm | Responsible process |
|---|---|
| Feasibility and production conditions | Process 01 — Commercial |
| Manufacturing lead times and conditions | Process 02 — Purchasing & Subcontracting |
| Delivery lead times and conditions | Process 03 — Logistics |

> **Order review:** This step constitutes the order review ensuring that customer requirements are clearly defined, understood and achievable before any acceptance.

**After validation:**
- information is recorded in the FileMaker system, ensuring traceability of orders
- an order acknowledgement is sent to the client, confirming quantities, price and estimated lead time

**Subsequent modifications:** Any modification requested by the client is subject to:
- analysis of the request according to the assigned role (cf. M1-DIR-001 — Leadership)
- validation by the industrial partner if necessary
- formal confirmation by email from the client before implementation
- an update in the FileMaker system

> This organization guarantees control of customer requirements, traceability of decisions and securing of order acceptance.

## 2. Context analysis (SWOT)

### External issues (section 4.1 of context)

- Dependence on Chinese industrial partners for tooling and production
- International transport lead times and logistics constraints
- Customs and regulatory requirements for import/export
- Variations in production costs, transport costs and raw material prices
- High client expectations regarding quality, technical conformity and deadline compliance

### Internal issues (section 4.1 of context)

- One-person organization requiring strong priority management and planning
- Need for smooth and continuous communication with clients, suppliers and logistics partners
- Maintaining a high level of documentary, technical and organizational control
- Ability to guarantee responsiveness, confidentiality and lasting customer satisfaction

### 5.3 Evaluation grid

**Probability:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3)

**Impact:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3) / Very high (4)

- 1-2: Low (acceptable)
- 3-4: Medium (monitor)
- 6+: High (actions required)

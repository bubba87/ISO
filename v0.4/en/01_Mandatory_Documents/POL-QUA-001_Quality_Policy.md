## Plus Sarl - Quality Policy

### Our mission

Plus Sarl specializes in industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. Our activities cover the design, development, and production of industrial parts, as well as the sourcing of existing parts. Molds are developed and stored in China, at our main partner Yuyao Mould Factory. All operations are coordinated from our office in Cudrefin, Switzerland.

### Management commitment

The management of Plus Sarl commits to implementing, maintaining, and continuously improving a quality management system in accordance with ISO 9001 requirements, in order to fully meet the needs and expectations of its clients.

The company's activity is based on industrial coordination between clients located primarily in Europe and manufacturing partners located in China. In this context, the priorities are:

- sustainable customer satisfaction
- compliance of products supplied
- control of lead times
- reliability of logistics operations

Certification is sought with **SQS** (Swiss Association for Quality and Management Systems). 🟢

### Our commitments

Management commits to:

1. **Process customer requests** with responsiveness and rigor.

2. **Ensure consistency of commercial offers** and control of pricing conditions.

3. **Effectively coordinate industrial partners** to ensure product quality and compliance.

4. **Monitor production, transport, and delivery operations.**

5. **Manage non-conformities** and implement actions to prevent their recurrence.

6. **Protect the confidentiality** of technical information and intellectual property.

7. **Continuously improve** the organization, working methods, and overall performance of the quality management system.

### Our strategic quality priorities

- Ensure delivery deadlines are met at over 95%
- Limit non-conformities to a maximum of 3 NC per client per year
- Ensure an average response time to clients of less than 24h
- Strengthen the quality partnership with our Chinese suppliers (Yuyao Mould Factory and **Whang**, Yuyao) 🟢
- Ensure control of the international logistics chain (air, sea, rail)

### Framework

This quality policy constitutes the framework for:

- establishing quality objectives
- performance analysis
- implementation of improvement actions

It is communicated, understood, and applied throughout all company activities and is subject to regular review to ensure its relevance and alignment with changing context and customer expectations.

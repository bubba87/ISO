## Remaining Open Questions

### Question 1 — Internal auditor: final decision

**Context:** You indicated that the managing director performs the audit herself, otherwise an external auditor.

**ISO 9001 issue:** Clause 9.2.2 requires that "auditors shall not audit their own work". In a sole-proprietor company, this means an external auditor is **mandatory** for strict compliance.

**Options:**
1. **Appoint an external auditor** (recommended) — Budget CHF 500-2'000 for 1 day
2. **Audit exchange between SMEs** — Find another certified SME and exchange audits
3. **Managing director + justification** — Possible if you document why and SQS accepts it (risky)

**🔴 Decision required before the audit.**

## Detailed satisfaction measurement methods

> **[ATTENTION POINT]**: It is recommended to implement a formal annual questionnaire
> in addition to the indirect indicators currently used. The ISO 9001 auditor expects
> a structured method for measuring customer satisfaction, even though the indirect indicators
> described below are relevant and acceptable as a basis.

> 🟡 **[RECOMMENDED]**: Approximately 10 active clients will be informed of the initiative and invited to participate in the questionnaire. Sending the questionnaire to all active clients is a key step to demonstrate to the SQS auditor that customer satisfaction is measured proactively.

| Element | Description |
|---|---|

### Satisfaction indicators currently used (in accordance with QMS section 9.1)

| # | Indirect indicator | Description | Collection method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Absence of complaints** | The absence of complaints from the client is considered a positive indicator of satisfaction | 🟢 Continuous monitoring by email |
| 2 | **Thank-you messages** | Receipt of explicit satisfaction and thank-you messages from clients | 🟢 Archived emails |
| 3 | **Qualitative feedback in emails** | Positive or negative comments made by clients in routine exchanges | 🟢 Archived emails |
| 4 | **Continuity of business relationship** | Maintaining the business relationship and order renewals demonstrate satisfaction | 🟢 Order tracking (FileMaker) |

## Context and Adaptation

Within the framework of Plus Sarl's industrial coordination activity, products manufactured in China are shipped directly to European customers. Plus Sarl therefore does not perform physical reception control. "Reception control" is adapted as follows:

| Traditional Control | Plus Sarl Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical inspection upon reception | Verification of transport and shipping documents |
| Visual product inspection | Shipment tracking (online tools, carrier contact) |
| Dimensional control | Confirmation of reception by customer |
| Sample verification | :green_circle: **Pre-shipment QC by partner + sample sent to Plus Sarl in parallel with transport** |
| Accept/reject decision | Absence of complaint = implicit conformity (cf. section 8.6 of quality manual) |
| Opening NC if defect | If customer complaint: initiation of NC process (PRO-NCF-001) |

## 1. Purpose

This agreement defines the quality requirements applicable to the manufacturing of products produced for Plus Sàrl's clients.

It aims to ensure:

- product conformity to client specifications
- protection of intellectual property
- control of deadlines
- management of nonconformities
- transparency and cooperation between the parties

---

## 2. Scope

This agreement applies to:

- manufacturing of hardware and fastener parts
- development and modification of tooling
- pre-shipment quality controls
- nonconformity management

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

The supplier commits to:

- strictly comply with the drawings, specifications and requirements communicated
- not modify a product without prior written validation by Plus Sàrl
- confirm technical feasibility before production launch
- immediately inform Plus Sàrl in case of technical difficulty

---

## 4. Quality Control

The supplier commits to:

- perform an internal quality control before shipment
- verify dimensional, functional and visual conformity
- report any nonconformity detected before dispatch
- correct any non-conforming production before shipment

A quality control report may be requested by Plus Sàrl.

---

## 5. New Tooling Validation

For any new tooling or modification:

- a sample may be submitted for validation
- series production only begins after written confirmation from Plus Sàrl
- any subsequent modification requires a new validation

---

## 6. Nonconformity Management

In case of nonconformity identified after delivery:

- the supplier cooperates in the cause analysis
- provides the necessary information
- implements corrective actions
- bears the costs related to a production defect

---

## 7. Confidentiality

The supplier acknowledges that:

- designs, drawings and specifications belong to Plus Sàrl's clients
- this information is strictly confidential
- no reproduction, dissemination or external use is authorized
- tooling developed for clients remains identified and protected

---

## 8. Subcontracting

The supplier may not subcontract production without prior notification to Plus Sàrl.

---

## 9. Continual Improvement

The supplier commits to:

- cooperate in quality improvement efforts
- participate in incident analysis
- strengthen controls if necessary

---

## 10. Entry into Force

This agreement enters into force on the date of signature.

Made in two copies.

## Plus Sarl - Quality Policy

### Our mission

Plus Sarl specializes in industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The business covers the design, development and production of industrial parts, as well as the sourcing of existing parts. Molds are developed and stored in China, at our main partner Yuyao Mould Factory. All operations are coordinated from our office in Cudrefin, Switzerland.

### Management commitment

The Managing Director commits to implementing, maintaining and continuously improving a Quality Management System compliant with the ISO 9001:2015 standard. The company's business is based on industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The priorities are: customer satisfaction, product conformity, lead time control and logistics reliability.

Certification is sought from the **SQS** (Swiss Association for Quality and Management Systems) certification body. 🟢

### Our commitments

The Managing Director personally commits to:

1. **Processing all customer requests** with responsiveness and rigor.

2. **Ensuring consistency of commercial offers** and price control.

3. **Effectively coordinating industrial partners** to guarantee product quality and conformity.

4. **Monitoring production, transport and delivery operations** to ensure compliance with deadlines and requirements.

5. **Managing non-conformities** and implementing preventive actions.

6. **Protecting the confidentiality** of technical information and clients' intellectual property.

7. **Continuously improving** the organization, methods and QMS performance.

### Our strategic quality priorities

- Ensure delivery deadline compliance above 95%
- Limit non-conformities to a maximum of 3 NCs per client per year
- Ensure an average customer response time of less than 24 hours
- Strengthen the quality partnership with our Chinese suppliers (Yuyao Mould Factory and **Whang**, Yuyao) 🟢
- Ensure control of the international logistics chain (air, sea, rail)

## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for detection, recording, handling and follow-up of nonconformities (NCs) related to products (moulds, plastic parts, screws), processes and the QMS of Plus Sarl.

## 2. Scope

All nonconformities detected:
- By customers (~10 active customers) after delivery (complaints) -- main source of detection
- During shipment monitoring (transport damage, delays)
- During internal monitoring (labelling errors, documentation errors, quantity errors)
- During pre-shipment quality control by the Chinese partners
- During verification of samples received at Plus Sarl
- During internal audits
- During certification audits (SQS)
- During routine QMS operations

> **Note:** Plus Sarl does not physically receive the products. Goods are shipped directly from China to European customers. Nonconformities are therefore mainly detected by the customers themselves after receiving the products, or by the Managing Director during operational monitoring. A pre-shipment QC process with sending of samples to Plus Sarl in parallel with transport is in place (cf. PRO-ACH-001, section 8).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Nonconformity (NC)** | Non-satisfaction of a requirement (customer, standard, regulatory, internal) |
| **Major NC** | NC having a significant impact on product quality or customer satisfaction |
| **Minor NC** | One-off NC, limited impact |
| **Correction** | Immediate action to eliminate the detected NC (symptom treatment) |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of the NC and prevent its recurrence (see PRO-ACR-001) |
| **Concession** | Authorisation to use a nonconforming product under conditions (customer agreement) |

## 5. NC Detection Sources

| Source | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Customer complaint** | Customer reports a defect, a delay, a quantity error, an appearance issue |
| **Transport damage** | Products damaged during transport (air, sea, rail) |
| **Production defect** | Parts not conforming to specifications (dimensions, appearance, material) detected by the customer |
| **Labelling/packaging error** | Incorrect labels, reference mix-up, packaging error |
| **Delivery delay** | Production or transport deadline not met |
| **Pre-shipment QC** | :green_circle: NC detected during quality control before shipment by the Chinese partner |
| **Sample verification** | :green_circle: NC detected during verification of samples received at Plus Sarl in parallel with delivery |
| **Internal monitoring** | Detection by the Managing Director during operational monitoring (document verification, photos, exchanges) |
| **Internal audit** | Audit finding (major or minor NC) |
| **Certification audit** | Finding by the certification body (SQS) |
| **Chinese partner feedback** | Problem reported during manufacturing by Yuyao Mould Factory or Whang |
| **Routine operations** | Order error, omission, missing document |

> **Important:** All complaints are handled regardless of the date on which they are reported by the customer.

### 6.2 Step Details

**Step 1 - Record the NC**

Complete the nonconformity form with:
- Unique number: NC_[YYYY]_[NNNN] (e.g.: NC_2026_1001)
- Date of detection (date of the customer complaint or finding)
- Source of detection (customer complaint, transport damage, internal monitoring, pre-shipment QC, sample verification, etc.)
- Precise description of the NC
- Product/process concerned
- Order/project reference (FileMaker reference)

**Numbering format:** NC_YYYY_NNNN
- YYYY = year
- NNNN = 4-digit sequential number (1001, 1002, 1003, ...)
- Separator: underscore (_)

**Step 2 - Analyse the Situation**

The Managing Director analyses the situation by assessing:
- Severity: Major / Minor
- Impact on the customer and their satisfaction
- Probable origin of the problem (production defect, transport, order error, labelling)
- Whether other orders/customers are potentially affected

**Step 3 - Decide on Handling**

### 6.3 Nonconforming Product Handling Options

| Option | Description | When to use | Customer agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Replacement** | Relaunch production and ship new conforming products | Preferred option -- guarantee customer satisfaction | Not required |
| **Concession (customer acceptance)** | The customer accepts the product despite the NC | Minor NC, customer accepts as-is | **Required** |
| **Modification / rework** | Product modified if technically feasible | Correctable defect | To be assessed with the customer |
| **Product left with the customer** | Defective product left with the customer (scrap or secondary use) | Return not economical | Customer agreement |
| **Product recovery** | Defective product recovered from the customer | If necessary for analysis or return | Not required |

> **Guiding principle:** The primary objective is to guarantee customer satisfaction. As a general rule, nonconforming products are replaced.

**Step 4 - Implement**

- Apply the decision (launch replacement, organise modification, etc.)
- Document the actions taken
- Communicate with the customer on deadlines and proposed solutions

**Step 5 - Coordinate with the Supplier**

- Inform the Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or Whang) of the NC
- Discuss financial terms depending on the origin of the problem:
  - If production defect: borne by the Chinese partner
  - If transport damage: claim to the carrier
  - If labelling/packaging error: correction by the Chinese partner
  - Other cases: negotiation on a case-by-case basis
- Request that the partner retain the NC information for future production runs
- Request strengthened pre-shipment QC if the NC could have been detected before dispatch

**Step 6 - Verify**

- Confirm that the handling is effective (replacement delivered, customer satisfied)
- Ensure that the Chinese partner has taken the NC into account for future production runs
- Verify that the pre-shipment QC has been adjusted if necessary

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Record EVERY nonconformity reported by a customer or detected internally, regardless of the date of reporting
> 2. Use the numbering format NC_YYYY_NNNN (e.g.: NC_2026_1001)
> 3. Photograph or ask the customer to photograph defects when possible
> 4. Communicate each product NC to the relevant Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or Whang)
> 5. Document exchanges via email or WeChat with the Chinese partners
> 6. Analyse trends during the management review
> 7. When in doubt about the origin, prioritise customer satisfaction (replacement)
> 8. Check whether the NC could have been detected during pre-shipment QC and adjust the process if necessary

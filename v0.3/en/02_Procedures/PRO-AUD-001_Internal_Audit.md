## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for planning, conducting and following up on internal audits of the Quality Management System (QMS) of Plus Sarl, in accordance with clause 9.2 of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 2. Scope

All processes of the Plus Sarl QMS, with the exception of clause 8.3 (Design and development) which is excluded from the scope.

The audit covers in particular:
- Conformity of processes to defined requirements
- Management of nonconformities and complaints
- Achievement of quality objectives
- Document control and traceability

## 3. Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Who |

| Plan the audit programme | :green_circle: Roxane Wicky (Managing Director) |

> **IMPORTANT - INDEPENDENCE REQUIREMENT (clause 9.2 ISO 9001:2015):**
> The Managing Director should not audit her own processes. An independent external auditor
> is strongly recommended to guarantee the objectivity and impartiality of the audit.

> **:yellow_circle: [RECOMMENDED: EXTERNAL AUDITOR]**
> The Managing Director indicated that she could conduct the audit herself, or engage an external auditor.
> The ISO 9001:2015 standard (clause 9.2.2) requires that "auditors shall not audit their own work".
> For a sole proprietorship such as Plus Sarl, **it is strongly recommended to appoint an external auditor**
> (quality consultant, certified auditor, or audit exchange between SMEs) to satisfy this independence requirement.
> The certification body SQS will examine this point during the certification audit.

## 4. Frequency

- **Minimum 1 internal audit per year**, covering all QMS processes
- The audit must take place **before the management review**
- The audit must take place **before the SQS certification/surveillance audit**

### Topics Covered During Each Annual Audit

| Topic | Description |
|---|---|
| Process conformity | Verification that operational processes (coordination, purchasing, transport monitoring, delivery) are applied in accordance with documented procedures |
| NC and complaint management | Review of the handling of nonconformities and customer complaints, verification of the implementation of corrections |
| Quality objectives | Verification of the achievement of defined quality objectives, analysis of performance indicators |
| Document control and traceability | Check that QMS documents are kept up to date, archiving, traceability of orders in FileMaker |

## 6. Audit Process

### 6.1 Preparation (Before the Audit)

| # | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select the auditor (verify their competence and independence) | :green_circle: Roxane Wicky |
| 2 | Define the audit scope and criteria (the entire QMS) | Roxane Wicky + Auditor |
| 3 | Communicate the date and programme to the auditor | Roxane Wicky |
| 4 | Make QMS documentation available (procedures, records, FileMaker) | Roxane Wicky |
| 5 | Prepare records and evidence (orders, NCs, supplier evaluations, indicators) | Roxane Wicky |

### 6.2 Auditor Selection Criteria

The internal auditor must:
- [ ] Be independent of the audited processes (not be the Managing Director) :yellow_circle: [RECOMMENDED -- strongly recommended for clause 9.2]
- [ ] Have knowledge of the ISO 9001:2015 standard
- [ ] Ideally, have internal auditor training (ISO 19011)
- [ ] Be objective and impartial

**Options for Plus Sarl:**
- Appointed quality consultant
- Auditor from a partner company (audit exchange between SMEs)
- Independent certified auditor

### 6.3 Conducting the Audit (Audit Day)

| # | Stage | Estimated duration | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening meeting | 15 min | Recap of scope, method, schedule |
| 2 | Process audit | 4-5h | Review of documentation, records in FileMaker, email/WeChat exchanges, interview with the Managing Director |
| 3 | Auditor synthesis | 30 min | Preparation of findings |
| 4 | Closing meeting | 30 min | Presentation of findings, classification, next steps |

**Points of attention specific to Plus Sarl:**
- Verification of order traceability in FileMaker
- Review of exchanges with the Chinese partners (email, WeChat)
- Check of operational documents (customs invoices, customer invoices, delivery notes, order acknowledgements)
- Verification of FileMaker data backups (cloud, hosted by provider)
- Verification of the quality agreement with Yuyao Mould Factory and Whang
- Review of the pre-shipment QC and sampling process

### 6.4 After the Audit

| # | Action | Deadline | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The auditor drafts the audit report | 1-2 weeks | Auditor |
| 2 | The Managing Director reviews the report | 1 week | Roxane Wicky |
| 3 | For each NC: open a corrective action form (PRO-ACR-001) | 2 weeks | Roxane Wicky |
| 4 | Implement corrective actions | Per plan | Roxane Wicky |
| 5 | Verify the effectiveness of actions | 1-3 months after | Roxane Wicky / Auditor |
| 6 | Include results in the management review | Next review | Roxane Wicky |

## 7. Classification of Findings

| Type | Definition | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| **Major nonconformity** | Non-satisfaction of a requirement that could affect the QMS capability | Immediate corrective action required |
| **Minor nonconformity** | One-off non-satisfaction of a requirement, without system impact | Corrective action within an agreed timeframe |
| **Observation** | Potential weakness that could become an NC | To be considered for improvement |
| **Positive point** | Good practice identified | To be maintained and built upon |

## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for selection, evaluation and control of suppliers and subcontractors, in particular the Chinese partners who manufacture moulds and injection-moulded plastic parts for the European customers of Plus Sarl.

> **This procedure is CRITICAL for Plus Sarl.** As manufacturing is entirely
> outsourced to partners in China, controlling this process is essential
> and will be examined in detail by the certification auditor (SQS).

## 2. Scope

All purchases and outsourced services having an impact on the quality of products delivered to customers (~10 active customers):
- Mould manufacturing (Chinese partners)
- Plastic parts injection moulding (Chinese partners)
- Screw manufacturing (Whang, Yuyao)
- Subcontracting of specialities by the Chinese partners
- International transport (freight forwarder, air/sea/rail carrier)
- Any other supplier having a quality impact

## 6. Partner Selection and Retention

### 6.1 Selection and Retention Criteria

The selection and retention of Plus Sarl's partners is based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Importance | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust built over time** | Critical | Long-term relationship, demonstrated reliability |
| **Quality of delivered products** | Critical | Compliance with specifications, low NC rate |
| **On-time delivery** | High | Deliveries within agreed timeframes |
| **Technical capability** | High | Suitable equipment, expertise, feasibility |
| **Communication and responsiveness** | Medium | Smooth exchanges via email and WeChat |
| **Price and commercial terms** | Medium | Competitiveness and transparency |

> **Note:** Current partners have been in place since the creation of Plus Sarl in 2007 (Yuyao Mould Factory) and are retained based on mutual trust, product quality and on-time delivery. Performance monitoring is continuous.

### 6.2 Selection of a New Partner (if necessary)

|---|---|---|

| 2 | Initial evaluation | Assessment of technical capability, references, samples |
| 3 | Trial | Test order on a first project |
| 4 | Validation | Validation based on results (quality, delivery time, communication) |
| 5 | Integration | Integration into the ordering process |

## 10. Continuous Partner Evaluation

### 10.1 Continuous Monitoring (Class A Partners)

The performance of Chinese partners is continuously monitored by the Managing Director:

| Criterion | Monitoring method |
|---|---|
| Delivery quality | Tracking of customer complaints, NC rate per partner |
| On-time delivery | Comparison of announced delivery time vs. actual delivery time |
| Communication and responsiveness | Qualitative assessment of email/WeChat exchanges |
| NC management | Timeliness and effectiveness of reported NC handling |

> **First documented NC case:** NC_2026_1001 (labelling error on SHIP_25058/CFM00057428/90.60.05710) constitutes the first documented nonconformity case with the current supplier. The supplier was informed via WeChat on 10/02/2026. Processing is ongoing and will be reinforced during the trip to China planned for March 2026.

### 10.2 Formal Periodic Evaluation

| Class | Evaluation frequency |
|---|---|
| A - Critical (Chinese partners) | Annual (formal) + continuous monitoring |
| B - Important (carriers) | Annual (simplified) |
| C - Standard | No formal evaluation |

See form FOR-EVF-001 for the detail of the annual evaluation.

### 10.3 On-Site Production Quality Monitoring

> **Trip to China planned for March 2026:**
> A trip to the Chinese partners is planned for March 2026 with the following objectives:
> - Strengthen on-site production quality monitoring
> - Address ongoing corrective actions (AC_2026_001 - labelling error)
> - Discuss the implementation of a quality control checklist
> - Advance the formalisation of the quality agreement (cf. section 7)
> - Verify production, labelling and packaging conditions
> - Strengthen requirements for label verification before shipment
> - Formalise the pre-shipment QC and sampling process (cf. section 8)

### 10.4 Actions Based on Results

| Overall score | Assessment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| >= 80% | Performing partner | Maintain the relationship |
| 60-79% | Acceptable partner | Discussion of improvement areas |
| < 60% | Underperforming partner | Improvement plan or search for alternative |

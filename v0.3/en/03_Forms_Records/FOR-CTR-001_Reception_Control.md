## Context and adaptation

Within the framework of Plus Sarl's industrial coordination activities, products manufactured in China are shipped directly to European customers. Plus Sarl therefore does not perform physical incoming inspection. The "incoming inspection" is adapted as follows:

| Traditional inspection | Plus Sarl adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical inspection upon reception | Verification of transport and shipping documents |
| Visual product inspection | Shipment tracking (online tools, carrier contact) |
| Dimensional inspection | Customer reception confirmation |
| Sample inspection | 🟢 **Pre-shipment QC by the partner + sample sent to Plus Sarl in parallel with transport** |
| Accept/reject decision | Absence of complaint = implicit conformity (cf. section 8.6 of the quality manual) |
| NC opened if defect | If customer complaint: trigger of the NC process (PRO-NCF-001) |

## Complaint handling principles at Plus Sarl

| Item | Applicable rule |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledgement timeframe** | 🟢 Every complaint is handled regardless of when it is reported by the customer |
| **Handling process** | 🟢 Identify the cause, implement corrective actions, ensure customer satisfaction |
| **Product replacement** | 🟢 Products are replaced when the non-conformity is attributable to Plus Sarl or its suppliers |
| **Financial terms** | 🟢 Discussed with the supplier (Yuyao Mould Factory or Whang) depending on the origin of the issue |
| **Responsible** | 🟢 Roxane Wicky (Managing Director — sole employee) |

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Record EVERY complaint, even verbal ones (by phone or WeChat)
> 2. Acknowledge receipt to the customer within 24-48 hours
> 3. Handle critical complaints as a priority
> 4. Communicate to the relevant Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or Whang) if the complaint concerns manufacturing
> 5. Discuss financial terms with the supplier depending on the origin of the issue
> 6. Products are replaced when the NC is attributable to Plus Sarl or its suppliers
> 7. The absence of complaints is used as a customer satisfaction indicator (see FOR-SAT-001)
> 8. Present complaint indicators during the management review (FOR-RDR-001)
> 9. Every complaint is handled regardless of when it is reported — no statute of limitations
> 10. NCs can also be detected internally (cf. NC_2026_1001) — they do not depend exclusively on customer complaints
> 11. The SQS auditor will examine this register — keep it rigorously up to date

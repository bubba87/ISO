## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for planning, conducting, and monitoring internal audits of the Quality Management System (QMS) of Plus Sarl, in accordance with clause 9.2 of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 2. Scope

All processes of the Plus Sarl QMS, with the exception of clause 8.3 (Design and development), which is excluded from the scope.

The audit covers in particular:
- Conformity of processes to defined requirements
- Management of nonconformities and complaints
- Achievement of quality objectives
- Document control and traceability

## 3. Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Who |
|---|---|
| Plan the audit program | Roxane Wicky (managing director) |
| Select and appoint the internal auditor | Roxane Wicky (managing director) |
| Conduct the audit | **External internal auditor** (mandatory - independence) |
| Validate the audit report | Roxane Wicky (managing director) |
| Implement corrective actions | Roxane Wicky (managing director) |

> **IMPORTANT - INDEPENDENCE REQUIREMENT (clause 9.2 ISO 9001:2015):**
> The managing director cannot audit her own processes. An independent external auditor
> must be appointed to ensure the objectivity and impartiality of the audit.

> **[POINT OF ATTENTION: The PDF indicates that the managing director conducts the audit herself. The ISO 9001 standard requires auditor independence (clause 9.2). It is strongly recommended to engage an external auditor.]**
>
> The current operating model provides for a "structured annual review" conducted by the managing director herself. This approach does not comply with the independence requirement of the ISO 9001:2015 standard (clause 9.2.2: "auditors shall not audit their own work"). For a sole proprietorship such as Plus Sarl, the recommended solution is to appoint an external auditor (quality consultant, certified auditor, or audit exchange between SMEs).

## 4. Frequency

- **Minimum 1 internal audit per year**, covering all QMS processes
- The audit must take place **before the management review**
- The audit must take place **before the certification/surveillance audit**

### Topics Covered During Each Annual Audit

| Topic | Description |
|---|---|
| Process conformity | Verification that operational processes (coordination, purchasing, transport tracking, delivery) are applied in accordance with documented procedures |
| NC and complaints management | Examination of the handling of nonconformities and customer complaints, verification of the implementation of corrections |
| Quality objectives | Verification of the achievement of defined quality objectives, analysis of performance indicators |
| Document control and traceability | Review of QMS document maintenance, archiving, traceability of orders in FileMaker |

## 6. Audit Process

### 6.1 Preparation (Before the Audit)

| # | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select the external auditor (verify their competence and independence) | Roxane Wicky |
| 2 | Define the audit scope and criteria (entire QMS) | Roxane Wicky + Auditor |
| 3 | Communicate the date and program to the auditor | Roxane Wicky |
| 4 | Make the QMS documentation available (procedures, records, FileMaker) | Roxane Wicky |
| 5 | Prepare records and evidence (orders, NCs, supplier evaluations, indicators) | Roxane Wicky |

### 6.2 Auditor Selection Criteria

The internal auditor must:
- [ ] Be independent of the audited processes (must not be the managing director)
- [ ] Have knowledge of the ISO 9001:2015 standard
- [ ] Ideally, have internal auditor training (ISO 19011)
- [ ] Be objective and impartial

**Options for Plus Sarl:**
- Appointed quality consultant
- Auditor from a partner company (audit exchange between SMEs)
- Independent certified auditor

### 6.3 Conducting the Audit (Audit Day)

| # | Step | Estimated Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening meeting | 15 min | Review of scope, method, schedule |
| 2 | Process audit | 4-5h | Examination of documentation, records in FileMaker, email/WeChat exchanges, interview with the managing director |
| 3 | Auditor summary | 30 min | Preparation of findings |
| 4 | Closing meeting | 30 min | Presentation of findings, classification, next steps |

**Points of attention specific to Plus Sarl:**
- Verification of order traceability in FileMaker
- Examination of communications with Chinese partners (email, WeChat)
- Review of operational documents (customs invoices, customer invoices, delivery notes, order acknowledgements)
- Verification of FileMaker data backups

### 6.4 After the Audit

| # | Action | Deadline | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The auditor drafts the audit report | 1-2 weeks | External auditor |
| 2 | The managing director reviews the report | 1 week | Roxane Wicky |
| 3 | For each NC: open a corrective action form (PRO-ACR-001) | 2 weeks | Roxane Wicky |
| 4 | Implement corrective actions | Per plan | Roxane Wicky |
| 5 | Verify effectiveness of actions | 1-3 months after | Roxane Wicky / Auditor |
| 6 | Include results in the management review | Next review | Roxane Wicky |

## 7. Finding Classification

| Type | Definition | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Major nonconformity** | Failure to satisfy a requirement that could affect QMS capability | Immediate corrective action required |
| **Minor nonconformity** | Isolated failure to satisfy a requirement, no systemic impact | Corrective action within an agreed timeframe |
| **Observation** | Potential weakness that could become a NC | To be considered for improvement |
| **Positive point** | Good practice identified | To be maintained and capitalized upon |

## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for selection, evaluation, and control of suppliers and subcontractors, particularly the Chinese partners who manufacture moulds and injection-moulded plastic parts for the European customers of Plus Sarl.

> **This procedure is CRITICAL for Plus Sarl.** As manufacturing is entirely
> outsourced to partners in China, the control of this process is essential
> and will be examined in detail by the certification auditor.

## 2. Scope

All purchases and outsourced services having an impact on the quality of products delivered to customers:
- Mould manufacturing (Chinese partners)
- Plastic part injection moulding (Chinese partners)
- Specialty subcontracting by Chinese partners
- International transport (freight forwarder, air/sea/rail carrier)
- Any other supplier with a quality impact

## 6. Partner Selection and Maintenance

### 6.1 Selection and Maintenance Criteria

The selection and maintenance of Plus Sarl partners is based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Importance | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust built over time** | Critical | Long-term relationship, demonstrated reliability |
| **Quality of delivered products** | Critical | Conformity to specifications, low NC rate |
| **Adherence to deadlines** | High | Deliveries on time as agreed |
| **Technical capability** | High | Suitable equipment, expertise, feasibility |
| **Communication and responsiveness** | Medium | Smooth exchanges via email and WeChat |
| **Pricing and commercial terms** | Medium | Competitiveness and transparency |

> **Note:** The current partners have been in place since the creation of Plus Sarl in 2007 (Yuyao Mould Factory) and are maintained on the basis of mutual trust, product quality, and adherence to deadlines. Performance monitoring is continuous.

### 6.2 Selection of a New Partner (If Necessary)

| # | Step | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identification | Search for potential partners (network, recommendations, trade fairs) |
| 2 | Initial evaluation | Evaluation of technical capability, references, samples |
| 3 | Trial | Test order on an initial project |
| 4 | Validation | Validation based on results (quality, timeline, communication) |
| 5 | Integration | Integration into the ordering process |

## 7. Quality Agreement with Chinese Partners

> **[POINT OF ATTENTION]**: To date, the relationship with the Chinese partners is based on mutual
> trust built since 2007. No formal signed quality agreement exists.
>
> **Recommendation:** Formalizing a quality agreement with Class A partners is
> strongly recommended to meet the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard (clause 8.4).
> This agreement should cover at minimum the following points:

### Recommended Minimum Content of the Quality Agreement

| Clause | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Purpose | Description of the products/services concerned | [ ] To be formalized |
| 2. Quality requirements | General specifications (tolerances, materials, finishes) | [ ] To be formalized |
| 3. Technical documentation | The partner must receive drawings/specifications before manufacturing | [ ] Practice in place |
| 4. In-process inspection | The partner carries out inspections and provides reports | [ ] To be formalized |
| 5. Final inspection | The partner carries out a final inspection before shipment | [ ] To be formalized |
| 6. Inspection report | The partner provides an inspection report with each delivery | [ ] To be formalized |
| 7. Right to audit / visit | Plus Sarl may visit the factory | [ ] To be formalized (visit planned March 2026) |
| 8. NC management | NC handling process (timelines, responsibilities) | [ ] To be formalized |
| 9. Traceability | The partner ensures lot traceability | [ ] To be formalized |
| 10. Modifications | Any modification must be submitted to and approved by Plus Sarl | [ ] Practice in place |
| 11. Confidentiality | Protection of drawings, specifications, and customer data | [ ] To be formalized |

## 9. Continuous Partner Evaluation

### 9.1 Continuous Monitoring (Class A Partners)

The performance of Chinese partners is continuously monitored by the managing director:

| Criterion | Monitoring Method |
|---|---|
| Delivery quality | Monitoring of customer complaints, NC rate per partner |
| Adherence to deadlines | Comparison of announced timeline vs. actual delivery timeline |
| Communication and responsiveness | Qualitative assessment of email/WeChat exchanges |
| NC management | Timeline and effectiveness of handling reported NCs |

> **First documented NC case:** NC_2026_1001 (labelling error on SHIP_25058/CFM00057428/90.60.05710) constitutes the first documented case of nonconformity with the current supplier. The supplier was informed via WeChat on 10/02/2026. Handling is in progress and will be reinforced during the trip to China planned for March 2026.

### 9.2 Formal Periodic Evaluation

| Class | Evaluation Frequency |
|---|---|
| A - Critical (Chinese partners) | Annual (formal) + continuous monitoring |
| B - Important (carriers) | Annual (simplified) |
| C - Standard | No formal evaluation |

See form FOR-EVF-001 for the detail of the annual evaluation.

### 9.3 On-Site Production Quality Monitoring

> **Trip to China planned for March 2026:**
> A trip to the Chinese partners is planned for March 2026 with the following objectives:
> - Strengthen on-site production quality monitoring
> - Address ongoing corrective actions (AC_2026_001 - labelling error)
> - Discuss the implementation of a quality control checklist
> - Advance on the formalization of the quality agreement (cf. section 7)
> - Verify production, labelling, and packaging conditions
> - Strengthen requirements on label verification before shipment

### 9.4 Actions Based on Results

| Overall Score | Assessment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| >= 80% | Performing partner | Maintain the relationship |
| 60-79% | Acceptable partner | Discussion of areas for improvement |
| < 60% | Insufficient partner | Improvement plan or search for alternative |

## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for detection, recording, handling, and monitoring of nonconformities (NCs) related to products (moulds and plastic parts), processes, and the QMS of Plus Sarl.

## 2. Scope

All nonconformities detected:
- By customers after delivery (complaints) -- primary source of detection
- During shipment tracking (transport damage, delays)
- During internal monitoring (labelling errors, documentation errors, quantity errors)
- During internal audits
- During routine QMS operations

> **Note:** Plus Sarl does not physically receive products. Goods are shipped directly from China to European customers. Nonconformities are therefore primarily detected by the customers themselves after receipt of products, or by the managing director during operational monitoring.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Nonconformity (NC)** | Failure to satisfy a requirement (customer, standard, regulatory, internal) |
| **Major NC** | NC with a significant impact on product quality or customer satisfaction |
| **Minor NC** | Isolated NC, limited impact |
| **Correction** | Immediate action to eliminate the detected NC (treating the symptom) |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of the NC and prevent its recurrence (see PRO-ACR-001) |
| **Concession** | Authorization to use a nonconforming product under conditions (customer agreement) |

## 5. NC Detection Sources

| Source | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Customer complaint** | Customer reports a defect, a delay, a quantity error, an appearance issue |
| **Transport damage** | Products damaged during transport (air, sea, rail) |
| **Production defect** | Parts not conforming to specifications (dimensions, appearance, material) detected by the customer |
| **Labelling/packaging error** | Incorrect labels, reference mix-up, packaging error |
| **Delivery delay** | Production or transport deadline not met |
| **Internal monitoring** | Detection by the managing director during operational monitoring (document verification, photos, communications) |
| **Internal audit** | Audit finding (major or minor NC) |
| **Certification audit** | Finding by the certification body |
| **Chinese partner feedback** | Problem reported during manufacturing by Yuyao Mould Factory or the second partner [name TO BE CONFIRMED] |
| **Routine operations** | Order error, omission, missing document |

> **Important:** All complaints are handled regardless of the date on which they are reported by the customer.

### 6.2 Step Details

**Step 1 - Record the NC**

Complete the nonconformity form with:
- Unique number: NC_[YYYY]_[NNNN] (e.g.: NC_2026_1001)
- Detection date (date of customer complaint or finding)
- Detection source (customer complaint, transport damage, internal monitoring, etc.)
- Precise description of the NC
- Product/process concerned
- Order/project reference (FileMaker reference)

**Numbering format:** NC_YYYY_NNNN
- YYYY = year
- NNNN = 4-digit sequential number (1001, 1002, 1003, ...)
- Separator: underscore (_)

**Step 2 - Analyze the Situation**

The managing director analyzes the situation by assessing:
- Severity: Major / Minor
- Impact on the customer and their satisfaction
- Probable origin of the problem (production defect, transport, order error, labelling)
- Whether other orders/customers are potentially affected

**Step 3 - Decide on Handling**

### 6.3 Nonconforming Product Handling Options

| Option | Description | When to Use | Customer Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Replacement** | Relaunch production and ship new conforming products | Preferred option -- ensure customer satisfaction | Not required |
| **Concession (customer acceptance)** | The customer accepts the product despite the NC | Minor NC, customer accepts as-is | **Required** |
| **Modification / rework** | Product modified if technically feasible | Correctable defect | To be assessed with the customer |
| **Product left with the customer** | Defective product left with the customer (scrap or secondary use) | Return not economically viable | Customer agreement |
| **Product retrieval** | Defective product retrieved from the customer | If necessary for analysis or return | Not required |

> **Guiding principle:** The primary objective is to ensure customer satisfaction. As a general rule, nonconforming products are replaced.

**Step 4 - Implement**

- Apply the decision (launch replacement, organize modification, etc.)
- Document the actions taken
- Communicate with the customer regarding timelines and proposed solutions

**Step 5 - Coordinate with the Supplier**

- Inform the Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or the second partner [name TO BE CONFIRMED]) of the NC
- Discuss financial terms based on the origin of the problem:
  - If production defect: borne by the Chinese partner
  - If transport damage: claim with the carrier
  - If labelling/packaging error: correction by the Chinese partner
  - Other cases: negotiation on a case-by-case basis
- Request that the partner retain the NC information for future production runs

**Step 6 - Verify**

- Confirm that the handling is effective (replacement delivered, customer satisfied)
- Ensure that the Chinese partner has properly taken the NC into account for future production runs

> **Filling instructions:**
> 1. Record EVERY nonconformity reported by a customer or detected internally, regardless of the date of reporting
> 2. Use the numbering format NC_YYYY_NNNN (e.g.: NC_2026_1001)
> 3. Photograph or ask the customer to photograph defects when possible
> 4. Communicate each product NC to the relevant Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or second partner)
> 5. Document exchanges by email or WeChat with Chinese partners
> 6. Analyze trends during the management review
> 7. When in doubt about the origin, prioritize customer satisfaction (replacement)

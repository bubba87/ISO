## Detailed satisfaction measurement methods

> **[ATTENTION POINT]**: It is recommended to implement a formal annual questionnaire
> in addition to the indirect indicators currently used. The ISO 9001 auditor expects
> a structured method for measuring customer satisfaction, even though the indirect indicators
> described below are relevant and acceptable as a basis.

---

## Customer satisfaction measurement context at Plus Sàrl

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| **Current method** | Indirect evaluation based on qualitative and relational indicators |
| **Formal questionnaire** | Not used to date |
| **Justification** | Sole-proprietor company with a direct and personalized relationship with each European customer |

### Satisfaction indicators currently used (in accordance with QMS section 9.1)

| # | Indirect indicator | Description | Collection method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Absence of complaints** | The absence of complaints from the customer is considered a positive indicator of satisfaction | Continuous monitoring via email |
| 2 | **Thank-you messages** | Receipt of explicit satisfaction and thank-you messages from customers | Archived emails |
| 3 | **Qualitative feedback in emails** | Positive or negative comments made by customers in routine exchanges | Archived emails |
| 4 | **Continuity of the business relationship** | Maintaining the business relationship and order renewals reflect satisfaction | Order tracking (FileMaker) |

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Use the **simplified form** (Roxane Wicky method) after each significant project/order or at least once per year per customer
> 2. Send the detailed questionnaire (Method A) at least once per year to each active customer
> 3. In parallel, maintain continuous indicator monitoring (Method B) — this is Plus Sàrl's current strength
> 4. The auditor wants to see that you measure satisfaction and that you act on the results
> 5. Present the summary during the management review (FOR-RDR-001)
> 6. Keep returned questionnaires and thank-you emails as evidence
> 7. The absence of complaints is a positive indicator but is not sufficient on its own — the formal questionnaire strengthens the approach

## Context and adaptation

Within the scope of Plus Sàrl's industrial coordination activity, products manufactured in China are shipped directly to European customers. Plus Sàrl therefore does not perform physical receiving inspection. The "receiving inspection" is adapted as follows:

| Standard inspection | Plus Sàrl adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical inspection upon receipt | Verification of transport and shipping documents |
| Visual product inspection | Shipment tracking (online tools, carrier contact) |
| Dimensional inspection | Customer confirmation of receipt |
| Accept/reject decision | Absence of complaint = implicit conformity (cf. quality manual section 8.6) |
| NC opened if defect found | If customer complaint: trigger NC process (PRO-NCF-001) |

---

## Quality control checklist

> **Context:** This checklist was created following the corrective action resulting from NC_2026_1001
> (labelling error on order SHIP_25058 / CFM00057428 / 90.60.05710, 1000 parts
> with incorrect label). It aims to systematize key verifications before shipment
> or upon receipt of documents, in order to prevent recurrence of this type of nonconformity.
>
> **Note:** This checklist will be finalized and validated during the trip to China in March 2026,

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Complete a form for EACH delivery shipped from China to a customer
> 2. Use the **quality control checklist** for each shipment — it will be mandatory after validation during the trip to China (March 2026)
> 3. Verify transport documents as soon as the export documents are received
> 4. Track the shipment and follow up with the carrier in case of delay
> 5. Obtain the customer's confirmation of receipt (email, phone)
> 6. In the absence of a complaint within a reasonable time, conformity is implicit
> 7. If the customer reports a problem, immediately open an NC form (PRO-NCF-001)
> 8. These forms are key evidence for the auditor — they demonstrate tracking despite the absence of physical receiving
> 9. The planned trip to China in March 2026 will allow the quality control checklist to be finalized and upstream quality controls to be strengthened (cf. quality manual section 8.5)

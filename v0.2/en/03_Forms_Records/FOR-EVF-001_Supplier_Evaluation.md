> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Use the **simplified form** (Roxane Wicky method) for quick mid-year evaluations and for Class B or C suppliers/carriers
> 2. Use the **detailed evaluation** (weighted scoring out of 100) for the formal annual evaluation of critical partners (Class A)
> 3. Evaluate each Chinese partner at least once per year
> 4. Base evaluations on objective data (not solely on impressions)
> 5. Retain evidence (inspection reports, emails, NCs)
> 6. Communicate results to the supplier (transparency)
> 7. If the supplier is rated "insufficient", document the action plan
> 8. For Yuyao Mould Factory: the 19-year trust-based relationship is an asset, but formal evaluation is now required by the QMS
> 9. The first formal evaluation establishes the baseline for future evaluations

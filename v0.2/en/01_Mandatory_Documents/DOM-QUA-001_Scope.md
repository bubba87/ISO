## 2. Scope

The Quality Management System of Plus Sarl covers the following activities:

> **Industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. Design, development and production of industrial parts. Sourcing and procurement of existing parts. International logistics chain management.**

### Activities covered

| Activity | Description | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Customer relationship management | Prospecting, offers, order review, satisfaction monitoring of European clients | Plus Sarl office, Cudrefin |
| Requirements analysis | Needs analysis, technical specifications and quality requirements | Plus Sarl office, Cudrefin |
| Tooling development coordination | Coordination of tooling and mold development with Chinese partners | Plus Sarl office / Remote (email, WeChat) |
| Tooling management | Management, monitoring and improvement of tooling (molds stored in China) | Remote |
| Production monitoring | Production monitoring, parts conformity and customer satisfaction | Remote |
| Sourcing and procurement | Sourcing and procurement of existing parts from suppliers | Plus Sarl office / Remote |
| Confidentiality | Protection of the confidentiality of technical information and intellectual property | Plus Sarl office |
| International logistics | International transport management (air, sea, rail) | Remote |

### Products and services covered

- Injection-molded industrial parts (custom-developed molds)
- Injection molds (developed and stored at Yuyao Mould Factory in China)
- Existing parts sourced from suppliers
- Industrial coordination and logistics services

### Tools and systems

## 3. Exclusions

### Excluded requirements

| ISO 9001 clause | Requirement | Justification for exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 8.3 | Design and development | Plus Sarl does NOT design the products. Designs, specifications, patents and technical requirements belong to the clients or are defined by the industrial partners. Plus Sarl acts solely as an industrial coordinator: production coordination, manufacturing monitoring and logistics management. The exclusion of clause 8.3 does not affect the ability of Plus Sarl to provide products that conform to customer requirements. |

### Analysis of possible exclusions

| Clause | Requirement | Applicable? | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.3 Design and development | Product design | **NO - EXCLUDED** | Plus Sarl does not design. Clients provide the designs and specifications, or these are defined by the industrial partners. Plus Sarl coordinates, monitors production and manages logistics. |
| 8.5.1.f Process validation | Injection process validation | **NO (outsourced)** | The injection process is carried out by the Chinese partners. Plus Sarl controls this outsourced process through the requirements of clause 8.4. |

No other exclusion is applicable.

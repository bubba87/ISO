## 1. Company overview

### General description

Plus Sarl is a Swiss company founded in 2007, headquartered at Route de Montet 11, 1588 Cudrefin, Switzerland. The company, incorporated as a Sarl (limited liability company), has 1 employee (the managing director) and 3 partners: Roxane Wicky, Olav Wicky and Capucine Wicky.

The main activity of Plus Sarl is industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturers, specializing in the development of molds for plastic injection parts and parts manufacturing. The company acts as the link between the needs of its European clients and the production capacity of its partners in China.

Plus Sarl works with two Chinese manufacturing partners:

## 2. Context analysis (SWOT)

### External issues (section 4.1 of the context)

- Dependence on Chinese industrial partners for tooling and production
- International transport lead times and logistics constraints
- Customs and regulatory requirements for import/export
- Variations in production costs, transport costs and raw material prices
- High customer expectations regarding quality, technical conformity and on-time delivery

### Internal issues (section 4.1 of the context)

- Single-person organization requiring strong priority management and planning
- Need for smooth and continuous communication with clients, suppliers and logistics partners
- Maintenance of a high level of documentary, technical and organizational control
- Ability to guarantee responsiveness, confidentiality and lasting customer satisfaction

## 5. Risk and opportunity analysis

### 5.1 Identified risks (section 6.1)

#### Sales process (customer relations)

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Loss of a major customer | Risk | Low | High | **Medium** | Maintain a high level of trust and service to retain customers; diversification of the customer portfolio |
| O1 | Continuous improvement of customer satisfaction | Opportunity | High | High | **High** | Proactive monitoring, responsive communication, rapid processing of requests |
| O2 | ISO 9001 certification = access to new markets | Opportunity | High | High | **High** | Obtain and maintain certification, communicate to prospects |

#### Purchasing / Subcontracting process (Chinese partners)

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R2 | Failure or unavailability of a Chinese partner/supplier | Risk | Low | Very high | **High** | Close communication with partners, enhanced production monitoring; availability of a second partner |
| R3 | Major quality defects on already manufactured products | Risk | Medium | High | **High** | Rapid non-conformity handling, replacement of defective parts, enhanced controls for subsequent productions |
| O3 | Strengthening of production quality | Opportunity | High | High | **High** | Precise specifications, enhanced controls, systematic lessons learned |

#### Logistics / Delivery process

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R4 | Significant international transport delays | Risk | Medium | High | **High** | Selection and re-evaluation of carriers based on their reliability; safety margins in deadlines |
| R5 | Risks related to customs formalities | Risk | Low | Medium | **Low** | Level considered manageable; rigorous documentary compliance monitoring |
| O4 | Optimization of delivery lead times | Opportunity | Medium | High | **High** | Continuous improvement of logistics monitoring, regular carrier evaluation |

### 5.2 Summary of mitigation actions

| Risk | Key actions |
|---|---|
| Chinese partner failure | Close communication, enhanced production monitoring, second partner available |
| International transport delays | Selection and re-evaluation of carriers based on their reliability |
| Major quality defects | Rapid NC handling, replacement of defective parts, enhanced controls for subsequent productions |
| Loss of a major customer | Maintaining a high level of trust and service to retain customers |
| Customs formalities | Level considered manageable; rigorous documentary monitoring |

### 5.3 Evaluation grid

**Probability:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3)

**Impact:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3) / Very high (4)

**Risk level = Probability x Impact**
- 1-2: Low (acceptable)
- 3-4: Medium (monitor)
- 6+: High (actions required)

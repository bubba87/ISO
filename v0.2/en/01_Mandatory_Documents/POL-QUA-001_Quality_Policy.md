## Plus Sarl - Quality Policy

### Our mission

Plus Sarl specializes in industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The business covers the design, development and production of industrial parts, as well as the sourcing of existing parts. Molds are developed and stored in China, at our main partner Yuyao Mould Factory. All operations are coordinated from our office in Cudrefin, Switzerland.

### Management commitment

The managing director is committed to implementing, maintaining and continuously improving a Quality Management System (QMS) in compliance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard. The company's business is based on industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The priorities are: customer satisfaction, product conformity, lead time control and logistics reliability.

### Our commitments

The managing director personally commits to:

1. **Processing all customer requests** with responsiveness and rigor.

2. **Ensuring the consistency of commercial offers** and price control.

3. **Effectively coordinating industrial partners** to guarantee product quality and conformity.

4. **Monitoring production, transport and delivery operations** to ensure compliance with deadlines and requirements.

5. **Managing non-conformities** and implementing preventive actions.

6. **Protecting the confidentiality** of technical information and customers' intellectual property.

7. **Continuously improving** the organization, methods and performance of the QMS.

### Our strategic quality objectives

- Ensure on-time delivery compliance above 95%
- Limit non-conformities to a maximum of 3 NCs per customer per year
- Ensure an average customer response time of less than 24 hours
- Strengthen the quality partnership with our Chinese suppliers (Yuyao Mould Factory and second partner)
- Ensure control of the international logistics chain (air, sea, rail)

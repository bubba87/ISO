## 1. Annual quality objectives

The quality objectives of Plus Sarl are defined in alignment with the quality policy and the industrial coordination activities between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. These objectives have been confirmed and reflect the four strategic areas of the company.

---

### Objective 1 - On-time delivery compliance

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Meet the delivery deadlines communicated to customers |
| **Consistent with** | Quality policy - Commitment No. 4 (monitoring of production, transport and delivery operations) |
| **Indicator** | % of orders delivered within the deadlines communicated to the customer |
| **Current value** | To be measured - first year: establish baseline |
| **Overall target** | 95% of orders delivered on time |
| **Sea/rail transport target** | +/- 10 days from the target date |
| **Air transport target** | +/- 3 days from the target date |
| **Responsible** | Roxane Wicky (Managing Director) |
| **Resources** | Order tracking in FileMaker, shipment tracking, coordination with freight forwarders |

### Objective 2 - Delivered product conformity

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Limit the number of customer non-conformities and react quickly in case of NC |
| **Consistent with** | Quality policy - Commitment No. 3 (effective coordination of industrial partners to guarantee quality) and Commitment No. 5 (non-conformity management) |
| **Indicator** | Number of customer non-conformities (NCs) per year, per customer |
| **Current value** | 1 NC recorded in Q1 2026: NC_2026_1001 - labeling error on shipment SHIP_25058 / item CFM00057428 / reference 90.60.05710 (1000 parts with incorrect label) |
| **Target** | Maximum 3 NCs per customer per year |
| **Replacement lead time target** | Replacement carried out within a timeframe consistent with the customer's expressed need |
| **Responsible** | Roxane Wicky (Managing Director) |

## 3. Objectives review

The quality objectives are reviewed:
- **Quarterly**: indicator monitoring, trend identification, corrective actions if necessary
- **Annually**: full review during the management review (M3), target adjustment for the following year

The results are presented during the management review and serve as a basis for planning continuous improvement actions.

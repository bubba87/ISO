## 4.1 Understanding of the Organization and Its Context

Plus Sarl carries out international industrial coordination activities between clients located mainly in Europe and manufacturing partners located in China.

In this context, it identifies the external and internal issues likely to influence its ability to consistently provide products and services that conform to regulatory requirements.

### External Issues

The main external issues identified are:

- Dependence on industrial partners located in China for tooling development and parts production
- Delays and constraints related to international transport and logistics
- Regulatory and customs requirements applicable to import and export operations
- Variations in production costs, transport costs, or raw materials
- The high level of client requirements regarding quality, technical compliance, and adherence to deadlines

### Internal Issues

The main internal issues identified are:

- The organization relies on a single-person structure requiring strong control of priorities and planning
- The need to ensure fluid and continuous communication with clients, suppliers, and logistics partners
- Maintaining a high level of documentary, technical, and organizational control
- The ability to guarantee responsiveness, confidentiality, and lasting client satisfaction

Plus Sarl regularly monitors these issues in order to adapt its organization, control the associated risks, and ensure the effectiveness of its quality management system in accordance with the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 4.1 Understanding of the Company Context

Plus Sarl carries out international industrial coordination activities between clients located mainly in Europe and manufacturing partners located in China.

In this context, the company identifies the internal and external issues likely to influence its ability to consistently provide products and services that conform to applicable requirements, in particular regulatory and contractual requirements.

### Strengths

- Lasting relationship with a limited number of loyal clients
- Years of experience in international industrial coordination
- Long-standing relationship built on trust with industrial partners
- Strong responsiveness and availability in the face of urgent situations
- Direct and fast communication with industrial partners in China
- Good command of order tracking, transport, and deadlines
- Technical knowledge of products and client requirements
- Agile organization allowing rapid decision-making

### Weaknesses

- Dependence on a limited number of industrial partners
- Sizing of internal resources requiring rigorous management of priorities and planning
- Centralization of knowledge
- Sensitivity to international transport delays and customs constraints
- Dependence on digital tools and remote communications for coordinating activities

### Opportunities

- Development of new industrial or logistics partnerships to secure supply
- Strengthening of the client relationship over the long term
- Continuous improvement of quality and logistics processes
- Diversification of suppliers or transport modes

### Threats

- Risk of disruption or breakdown at an industrial partner
- Uncertainties related to international transport, customs constraints, or geopolitical context
- Risk of product non-conformities likely to affect client satisfaction
- Changes in international or customs regulations
- Variations in production or transport costs affecting the economic balance
- Economic dependence on a limited number of clients

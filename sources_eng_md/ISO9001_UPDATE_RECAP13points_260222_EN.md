## 2. Yuyao Quality Agreement

Prepare a quality agreement between Plus Sarl and Hardeng Yuyao Mould Factory and another one with Oukailuo.

### 1. Purpose

This agreement defines the quality requirements applicable to the manufacturing of products produced for Plus Sarl's customers.

It aims to guarantee:

- product conformity to customer specifications
- the protection of intellectual property
- deadline management
- nonconformity management
- transparency and cooperation between the parties

### 2. Scope

This agreement applies to:

- the manufacturing of industrial parts
- the development and modification of tooling
- quality controls before shipment
- nonconformity management

### 3. Technical Requirements

The supplier commits to:

- strictly comply with the plans, specifications, and requirements communicated
- not modify a product without prior written approval from Plus Sarl
- confirm technical feasibility before production launch
- immediately inform Plus Sarl in case of technical difficulty

### 4. Quality Control

The supplier commits to:

- perform an internal quality control before shipment
- verify dimensional, functional, and visual conformity
- report any nonconformity detected before dispatch
- correct any non-conforming production before shipment

A quality control report may be requested by Plus Sarl.

### 5. Validation of New Tooling

For any new mold or modification:

- a sample may be submitted for validation
- series production only begins after written confirmation from Plus Sarl
- any subsequent modification requires a new validation

### 6. Nonconformity Management

In case of nonconformity identified after delivery:

- the supplier cooperates in the root cause analysis
- provides the necessary information
- implements corrective actions
- bears the costs related to a production defect

### 7. Confidentiality

The supplier acknowledges that:

- the designs, plans, and specifications belong to Plus Sarl's customers
- this information is strictly confidential
- no reproduction, distribution, or external use is authorized
- molds developed for customers remain identified and protected

### 8. Subcontracting

The supplier may not subcontract production without prior notice to Plus Sarl.

### 9. Continual Improvement

The supplier commits to:

- cooperate in case of quality improvement
- participate in incident analysis
- strengthen controls if necessary

### 10. Entry into Force

This agreement enters into force on the date of signature.

Made in two copies:

For Plus Sarl: Name, date, signature

For the supplier: Name, date, signature

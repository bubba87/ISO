## 2.2 Customer Relationship and Understanding of Needs

Plus Sàrl selects its clients in accordance with its strategy aimed at responding in a comprehensive and personalised manner to all of their needs.

This approach ensures:

- strong responsiveness in case of emergency
- precise consideration of technical specifications and quality requirements
- lasting support in the development, improvement and procurement of industrial parts

Plus Sàrl's clients are mainly European companies, owners of the designs, patents or technical specifications of the manufactured products.

The established customer relationship is based on:

- direct and continuous communication
- detailed understanding of technical and logistical needs
- permanent adaptation to deadline, quality and product evolution constraints

This organisation enables Plus Sàrl to ensure control of client requirements from the expression of needs through to the delivery of industrial parts.

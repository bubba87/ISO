## 5.1 Management Commitment

The manager is directly involved in the implementation, maintenance, and improvement of the quality management system.

She personally handles all client requests to the extent of her capabilities, manages quotations, and analyzes the prices applied, in particular by relying on the history of previous quotations in order to guarantee the consistency and control of commercial conditions.

The manager also ensures the monitoring of transport and watches over compliance with announced deadlines.

In the event of a quality problem, she coordinates the exchanges between the client and the industrial partners in China in order to identify the causes, define suitable solutions, and ensure the resolution of non-conformities. The associated technical data is retained by the relevant industrial partners.

When a malfunction is identified, the manager implements actions aimed at preventing its recurrence. In the event of a transport delay, she reassesses the choice of carrier and freight forwarder and informs the relevant partners in order to avoid any further non-compliant use. In the event of a production delay, she strengthens the monitoring with industrial partners, takes the necessary actions to improve deadlines, and anticipates the deadlines communicated to clients. In the event of a production-related defect, she requests that the factory retain the information and implement reinforced inspections during subsequent productions.

Through this direct involvement, the manager ensures the quality orientation of the company, operational decision-making, risk control, and continuous improvement of performance in accordance with the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 5.1 Leadership and Commitment

The management of Plus Sarl is actively involved in the implementation, maintenance, and improvement of the quality management system.

It ensures the integration of quality system requirements into operational activities, in particular through:

- Supervision of client request management and commercial offers
- Analysis of pricing conditions to guarantee consistency and control of commitments
- Monitoring of transport and compliance with announced deadlines
- Coordination of exchanges between clients and industrial partners in the event of quality issues
- Implementation of corrective actions in the event of an identified malfunction

In the event of a logistics delay, management reassesses the relevant partners and adapts the transport arrangements if necessary.

In the event of a production delay, it strengthens the monitoring with industrial partners and adjusts the planning in order to limit the client impact.

In the event of product non-conformity, it ensures the analysis of causes, the implementation of appropriate corrective actions, and the follow-up of inspections during subsequent productions.

Through this commitment, management ensures:

- Client orientation
- Risk control
- Compliance with applicable requirements
- Continuous improvement of the quality management system

Management ensures the steering of the quality management system, in particular through the regular conduct of the management review.

# 6.1 Actions Addressing Risks and Opportunities

As part of the planning of its quality management system, Plus Sarl identifies and analyzes the risks and opportunities likely to influence its ability to provide products and services that conform to customer requirements and to improve their satisfaction.

The main identified risks are:

- The failure or unavailability of a supplier or industrial partner in China
- Significant delays related to international transport
- The occurrence of major quality defects on already manufactured products
- The loss of an important client for the company's business

The risk related to customs formalities is also taken into account, while being considered manageable through the search for appropriate solutions with the relevant parties.

In order to manage these risks, Plus Sarl implements the following actions in particular:

- Maintaining close communication with industrial partners and reinforced production monitoring
- Selection and re-evaluation of carriers based on their reliability and performance
- Rapid handling of non-conformities, replacement of defective parts, and implementation of reinforced controls during subsequent productions
- Maintaining a relationship of trust and a high level of service in order to retain clients

Plus Sarl also identifies improvement opportunities, notably the optimization of lead times, the reinforcement of production quality, and the continuous improvement of customer satisfaction.

These risks and opportunities are regularly monitored and integrated into the continuous improvement of the quality management system in accordance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 6.1 Actions Addressing Risks and Opportunities

Based on the context analysis and the SWOT, Plus Sarl identifies the risks and opportunities likely to influence:

- Product conformity
- Compliance with deadlines
- Customer satisfaction
- Performance of the quality management system

Proportionate actions are defined in order to:

- Prevent or reduce identified risks
- Exploit improvement opportunities
- Ensure process effectiveness and business continuity

These actions are integrated into operational processes, notably:

- Selection and monitoring of industrial partners
- Transport planning and logistics monitoring
- Management of non-conformities and corrective actions
- Definition of quality objectives and improvement actions

The effectiveness of implemented actions is evaluated during:

- Monitoring of indicators
- Internal audits
- Management review

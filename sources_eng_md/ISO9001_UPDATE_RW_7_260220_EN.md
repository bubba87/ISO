# 7.1 Resources

Plus Sarl determines and provides the resources necessary to establish, implement, maintain, and continuously improve its quality management system.

Given the size of the structure, the main resources rely on the manager as well as on the material and digital tools enabling the management of activities, communication with clients and partners, and the monitoring of operations.

The essential tools for the operation of the company include in particular:

- A computer equipped with the software necessary for administrative and technical management
- The FileMaker management system for tracking products, orders, prices, and transport
- Electronic mail for formal exchanges with industrial partners and clients
- Internet access enabling communication, logistics tracking, and access to necessary information
- The WeChat communication application used for operational exchanges with partners in China
- A mobile phone ensuring availability and responsiveness in communications

These resources are maintained in working order to ensure business continuity, quality of service rendered, and customer satisfaction in accordance with the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 7.1 Resources

### 7.1.1 General

Plus Sarl determines and provides the resources necessary to establish, implement, maintain, and continuously improve its quality management system.

These resources include:

- Human resources necessary for the functions defined in chapter 5.3
- Digital and material infrastructures
- External resources related to outsourced processes (industrial and logistics partners)

The resources are adapted to the international industrial coordination activity.

### 7.1.2 Human Resources

The competencies necessary for the functions of management, commercial, industrial coordination, logistics, and quality are defined in chapter 5.3.

The company ensures that activities are carried out by persons possessing:

- Technical knowledge related to the products
- Mastery of client requirements
- The ability to coordinate international partners
- Understanding of applicable quality and regulatory requirements

Competencies are maintained through professional experience, continuing education, and industry monitoring.

### 7.1.3 Infrastructures

The infrastructures necessary for operation include in particular:

- A computer equipped with the software necessary for administrative and technical management
- The FileMaker system for tracking products, orders, prices, and transport
- Electronic mail for formal exchanges
- Internet access enabling communication and logistics tracking
- The WeChat application for operational exchanges with partners in China
- A mobile phone ensuring availability

These tools are maintained in working order to ensure business continuity.

### 7.1.4 Environment for the Operation of Processes

The work environment ensures:

- Confidentiality of technical and commercial data
- Reliability of international communications
- Responsiveness in the management of orders and emergencies

### 7.1.5 External Resources -- Customized Process

As part of its activity, Plus Sarl relies on its industrial partners in China for the production of parts.

These partners constitute essential external resources for the operation of the quality system.

The company ensures to:

- Work with established companies that comply with applicable legal requirements
- Maintain a lasting relationship based on trust and knowledge of their organization
- Ensure regular monitoring of performance (deadlines -- product conformity)
- Integrate these partners into the control of outsourced processes (section 8.4)

## 4.2 Relevant Interested Parties

Within the framework of its quality management system, Plus Sarl identifies the interested parties likely to influence its ability to consistently provide products and services that conform to client requirements as well as applicable regulatory requirements.

The main relevant interested parties are as follows:

### 1. European Clients

They expect products that conform to technical specifications, adherence to deadlines, responsive communication, as well as control of confidentiality and intellectual property.

### 2. Manufacturers and Industrial Partners in China

They are involved in tooling development, mold manufacturing, and parts production. Their ability to meet technical requirements, expected quality, and agreed-upon deadlines is essential.

### 3. Carriers and International Logistics Service Providers

They ensure the shipment of goods between China and Europe. Adherence to deadlines, integrity of transported products, and documentary compliance are critical elements.

### 4. Customs Services

They oversee import and export operations according to regulatory requirements that vary depending on the countries and transport modalities. Compliance with customs regulations is essential to the smooth conduct of operations.

### 5. Fiduciary and Accounting Service Provider

They ensure the administrative, accounting, and fiscal compliance of the company, contributing to its sustainability and to the sound management of its activities.

Plus Sarl regularly monitors the needs and expectations of these interested parties in order to ensure control of its processes, the compliance of its services, and the continued effectiveness of its quality management system in accordance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 4.2 Relevant Interested Parties

Within the framework of its main activity of international industrial coordination between Europe and China, Plus Sarl identifies the relevant interested parties having a direct influence on product conformity, adherence to deadlines, and client satisfaction.

The interested parties considered relevant to the quality management system are:

**European Clients**
Expectation: technical conformity of parts, adherence to deadlines, responsive communication, confidentiality and protection of intellectual property

**Industrial Partners in China**
Expectation: clear transmission of requirements, effective coordination, compliance with technical specifications, expected quality, and deadlines

**Carriers**
Expectation: reliability of deadlines, integrity of goods, and compliance of shipping documents

**Customs Authorities**
Expectations: compliance with applicable import requirements

The needs and expectations of these interested parties are taken into account in process management, risk and opportunity analysis, as well as during the management review.

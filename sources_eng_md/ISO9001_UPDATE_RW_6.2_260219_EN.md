# 6.2 Quality Objectives and Planning to Achieve Them

In order to implement its quality policy and continuously improve customer satisfaction, Plus Sarl defines the following quality objectives:

- **Compliance with deadlines**
  Ensure rigorous monitoring of production and transport in order to meet the deadlines announced to clients. Indicator: Ship -- train: delivery within 10 days earlier or later than the desired delivery date. Air: delivery within 3 days earlier or later than the desired delivery date.

- **Conformity and quality of products**
  Ensure the conformity of delivered parts to the clients' technical specifications and promptly handle non-conformities. Indicator: Number of non-conformities and replacement time according to client needs.

- **Control of pricing conditions**
  Maintain coherent and competitive pricing conditions based on the history of offers and coordination with suppliers and carriers. Indicator: Price stability over several years and validation of offers by clients.

- **Monitoring and responsiveness towards clients**
  Respond as quickly as possible to client requests, questions, complaints, or emergencies and ensure continuous communication throughout projects. Indicator: Average response time to client requests and expressed level of satisfaction.

The manager ensures regular monitoring of these objectives, implements the necessary actions in case of deviation, and integrates the results into the continuous improvement approach of the quality management system in accordance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 6.2 Quality Objectives and Planning to Achieve Them

In order to implement its quality policy and manage the identified risks, Plus Sarl defines measurable quality objectives, regularly monitored and integrated into the continuous improvement approach of the quality management system.

### Compliance with Production Deadlines

**Objective:** Ensure compliance with the deadlines announced to clients.

**Indicators:**

- Maritime or rail transport: delivery within an interval of 1-2 working weeks compared to the planned date
- Air transport: delivery within an interval of 2-4 working days compared to the planned date

**Monitoring:** Visualization of actual lead times via logistics tracking and data recorded in the FileMaker system.

### Conformity and Quality of Products

**Objective:** Ensure the conformity of delivered parts to client technical specifications and effectively handle any non-conformity.

**Control:**

- Transmission of technical requirements to industrial partners
- Quality control performed by the manufacturer before shipment
- Sample validation when necessary
- Non-conformity management including root cause analysis, replacement, and corrective actions

**Indicators:**

- Number of non-conformities
- Time to achieve conformity or replacement

### Control of Pricing Conditions

**Objective:** Maintain coherent pricing conditions accepted by clients.

**Indicators:**

- Price stabilization over time
- Validation of offers by clients

**Monitoring:** Comparison with the history of offers and coordination with suppliers and carriers.

### Monitoring and Responsiveness Towards Clients

**Objective:** Respond quickly to requests and ensure continuous communication.

**Indicators:**

- Average response time to client requests: 24-48 hours
- Expressed level of satisfaction or absence of complaints

**Monitoring:** Analysis of client exchanges and the complaints register.

The monitoring of objectives is ensured by the relevant functions according to the roles defined in chapter 5.3. Results are analyzed during the management review and give rise, if necessary, to corrective or improvement actions.

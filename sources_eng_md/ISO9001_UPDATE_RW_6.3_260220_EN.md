# 6.3 Planning of Changes

The company ensures that any change likely to have an impact on product quality, deadlines, costs, or the organization of services is analyzed and controlled before its implementation.

Depending on the nature of the planned change (new supplier, new carrier, new product, or organizational evolution), Plus Sarl:

- Evaluates the potential consequences on part conformity, delivery deadlines, and commercial conditions
- Systematically informs the concerned client
- Requests client validation when the change concerns a supplier, a production, or a specific part
- Carries out, when relevant, trials or a first controlled implementation, particularly for changes related to transport

These provisions ensure that changes are implemented in a controlled manner, transparent for the client, and in compliance with the requirements of the quality management system.

The manager retains responsibility for the final decision and ensures the integration of changes into the continuous improvement of the organization in accordance with the ISO 9001 standard.

## 6.3 Planning of Changes

Plus Sarl ensures that any change likely to have an impact on:

- Product conformity
- Production or delivery deadlines
- Commercial conditions
- Process organization

is analyzed, planned, and controlled before its implementation.

Changes may concern in particular:

- The introduction of a new industrial partner
- A change of supplier or carrier
- A product or tooling modification
- An evolution of logistics arrangements
- An internal organizational evolution

### Impact Analysis

Before implementation, the change is evaluated according to the relevant processes:

- **Commercial process:** Analysis of the impact on client requirements and client validation if necessary
- **Purchasing & industrial coordination process:** Analysis of technical feasibility and production impacts
- **Logistics process:** Analysis of deadline impacts and shipping conditions
- **Quality process:** Analysis of risks to product conformity and definition of adapted controls if necessary

### Implementation and Monitoring

When required:

- The client is informed or asked for validation
- Trials, samples, or first controlled implementations are carried out
- Applicable documents and information are updated
- Identified risks are integrated into the monitoring of the relevant processes

Changes are reviewed during the management review in order to evaluate their effectiveness and their impact on the quality management system.

## 3. Quality Management System Support

The quality management system of Plus Sàrl defines the organisation, responsibilities, processes and working methods to:

- ensure customer satisfaction
- guarantee product and service conformity
- control risks related to activities
- implement continual improvement in accordance with ISO 9001

This system is based in particular on:

- management of client requirements and orders
- coordination of outsourced production
- logistics monitoring
- document monitoring
- nonconformity and corrective action management
- performance analysis during internal audits and management reviews

## 3.1 Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities

The roles and responsibilities within Plus Sàrl are defined according to the functional organisation of the company, in order to ensure the proper functioning of the quality management system and customer satisfaction.

### M1 Leadership
Ensures the overall management of the company and the quality management system.

**Guidance and Management**

- define the vision and strategy of the company
- establish, implement and maintain the quality policy
- define quality objectives and monitor their achievement
- drive continual improvement and ensure the availability of resources necessary for the functioning of the QMS

**Leadership**

- customer focus and consideration of their requirements
- allocation of necessary resources
- definition and assignment of roles and responsibilities

**System Control**

- ensure conformity to the ISO 9001 standard
- monitor QMS process performance
- conduct the management review
- address risks and opportunities

### O1 Sales
Manages the customer relationship, from receiving requests to monitoring orders and complaints.

**Customer Relationship Management**

- receive and analyse client requests (orders, quotations, complaints, modifications, etc.)
- understand the technical, logistical and quality needs expressed
- ensure clear and continuous communication with clients
- contribute to lasting customer satisfaction

**Needs Analysis and Quotation Preparation**

- analysis of technical specifications and applicable requirements
- verification of quantities, deadlines and conditions requested
- preparation of quotations and order confirmations
- coordination with other internal processes for validation

**Order Monitoring**

- recording of orders in the FileMaker system
- transmission of necessary information to industrial partners
- overall monitoring of order progress
- informing the client in case of changes, delays or difficulties

**Client Complaint Management**

- recording and analysis of the request
- relevant processes are consulted for investigation
- an appropriate response is provided to the client
- information is transmitted to the quality process for treatment if necessary

### O2 Purchasing & Subcontracting
Ensures the selection, coordination and monitoring of industrial partners as well as control of outsourced production, in accordance with the requirements of the quality management system.

**Supplier Selection and Management**

- ensures collaboration with industrial partners capable of meeting technical, quality and deadline requirements
- ensures continuous evaluation of suppliers based on delivery conformity, deadline compliance and communication quality
- identifies alternative solutions in case of supplier failure

**Production Coordination and Monitoring**

- ensures transmission of orders and technical requirements to industrial partners
- verifies production feasibility, deadlines and manufacturing conditions
- regular monitoring of production progress through documented exchanges (e-mail, WeChat, etc.)
- sending and validation of samples when necessary

**Control of Outsourced Production**

- ensures client technical specifications are taken into account
- ensures quality controls are carried out by industrial partners
- ensures traceability of production and validation information
- coordinates with the quality process in case of nonconformity

### O3 Logistics & Delivery
Ensures the organisation, coordination and monitoring of international shipments through to final delivery to the client.

**Transport Organisation**

- selection of appropriate transport mode (air, sea, rail, road) according to deadlines, constraints and costs
- coordination with carriers and freight forwarders
- planning of shipments in line with production progress

**Document and Customs Control**

- ensures the preparation of shipping documents and customs documents compliant with client orders
- consideration of applicable regulatory requirements in Europe and Switzerland
- ensures traceability of shipment-related information

**Delivery Monitoring**

- ensures transport monitoring through to final delivery
- transmits available tracking information to the client (tracking, progress status, etc.)
- informs the client in case of delay, incident or modification

**Order Closure**

- confirms with the client the proper logistics execution
- coordinates with the administrative service for invoicing
- informs the quality process regarding conformity or nonconformity

### O4 Quality Control and Nonconformities
Aims to ensure the conformity of products delivered to client requirements and to guarantee the continual improvement of the quality management system.

**Quality Control Management**

- ensures technical and quality requirements of the client are taken into account
- ensures quality controls are carried out by industrial partners before shipment
- retains control and validation information
- analyses client returns after delivery

**Nonconformity Identification**

- identification during controls by the industrial partner
- identification upon receipt of goods by the client
- identification following a complaint
- recording in a nonconformity report
- analysis of the situation and causes

**Nonconformity Treatment**

- acceptance as is after validation
- product correction
- goods replacement

**Corrective Actions and Prevention**

- implementation of corrective actions
- verification of their effectiveness
- integration of information into quality monitoring, internal audit and management review

**Continual Improvement**

- improvement of control practices
- error prevention
- strengthening of conformity of delivered products

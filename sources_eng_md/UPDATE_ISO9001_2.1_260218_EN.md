# Quality Manual Update

2.1 Plus Sàrl acts as the interface between clients located mainly in Europe and manufacturers based in China for the design, development and production of industrial parts.

The parts are designed according to designs belonging to the clients and remain the intellectual property of the clients. Based on these designs, the tooling and moulds necessary for manufacturing are developed in China at the Yuyao Mould Factory plant.

The moulds are stored and managed directly in China.

Plus Sàrl then organises the production of parts from the moulds, ensuring conformity to the technical and quality requirements of the clients.

In addition, Plus Sàrl offers tooling modification and improvement services according to the evolution of client needs, as well as sourcing and procurement activities for existing parts on the market from qualified external suppliers.

## 2.1 Activities and Organisation of Outsourced Production

Plus Sàrl acts as the interface between clients located mainly in Europe and industrial manufacturers based in China for the design, development and production of industrial parts.

Plus Sàrl does not carry out production directly, as this is performed by industrial partners located in China. The company coordinates this production to ensure the conformity of parts to the technical and quality requirements of clients.

The parts are designed according to designs belonging to the clients and remain their intellectual property.

Based on these designs, the tooling and moulds necessary for manufacturing are developed, managed and stored in China at Plus Sàrl's industrial partners.

In addition, Plus Sàrl offers sourcing and procurement services for existing parts on the market from external suppliers.

Monitoring is managed to ensure continuous control of production progress through the various processes:

### O1: Sales Process
Creation of an order in the FileMaker system including:

- validated client order specifying quantities, references, deadlines, specific needs
- information transmitted to manufacturers by Plus Sàrl, or directly by the client in tripartite exchanges (client – Plus Sàrl – supplier)
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (e-mail, WeChat, etc.)
- order traceability ensured in the FileMaker system

### O2: Purchasing & Subcontracting
Creation of a production record in the FileMaker system including:

- expected product characteristics
- manufacturing lead time
- necessary updates to the production record
- production monitoring carried out through regular exchanges with industrial partners (e-mail, WeChat, etc.)
- sending of samples to clients and to Plus Sàrl when necessary for production validation

### O3: Logistics & Delivery
Creation of a delivery record in the FileMaker system including:

- deadlines and shipping conditions
- creation of customs documents compliant with client orders and European and Swiss legislation
- shipment tracking through to final delivery

### O4: Quality Control
Before the shipment of goods, Plus Sàrl ensures that the produced parts conform to:

- the technical requirements defined by the client, production identical to the registered order (to be defined with China how)
- the registered and validated order

This verification is based on:

- quality control carried out by the manufacturer (control sheet to be formalised with YAN)
- documentary tracking of quality controls carried out by the industrial partner and stored in FileMaker
- analysis of client returns after delivery by client confirmation e-mail
- reception and treatment of any nonconformity information

*Associated document: Nonconformity Management*

In case of identified nonconformity:

- creation of a nonconformity report
- decision on acceptance, replacement or correction of the goods based on the analysis carried out
- cause analysis and investigation
- implementation of corrective actions to prevent recurrence

### S1 Document Management: Document Control and Continual Improvement

Information related to production and conformity is stored:

- in the FileMaker system
- in e-mail and WeChat exchanges

These elements ensure the traceability of operations and feed:

- nonconformity monitoring
- internal audit

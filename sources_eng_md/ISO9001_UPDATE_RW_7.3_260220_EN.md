# UPDATE -- 7.3 Awareness

Given the single-person structure of the company, the manager is directly involved in the understanding, application, and improvement of the quality management system.

She is familiar with the quality policy, the defined objectives, as well as the importance of client satisfaction and the conformity of the products and services provided.

In the event of a problem or malfunction, the manager adapts her organization, working methods, and decisions in order to avoid repetition of the situation and to contribute to the continuous improvement of the company's performance.

This direct involvement guarantees the permanent consideration of the requirements of the quality management system in accordance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 7.3 Awareness

Plus Sàrl ensures that every person involved in activities covered by the quality management system is made aware of:

- The company's quality policy
- The applicable quality objectives
- The importance of client satisfaction
- The requirements relating to the conformity of products and services
- The potential consequences of a deviation from the requirements of the quality system

Awareness is ensured through:

- The integration of quality requirements into daily activities
- Regular communication of priorities and objectives
- Analysis of non-conformities and client feedback
- Performance review during the management review

This organization guarantees the permanent consideration of the requirements of the quality management system and contributes to the continuous improvement of performance.

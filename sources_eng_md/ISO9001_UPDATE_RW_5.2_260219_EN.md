# UPDATE

## 5.2 Quality Policy

Management is committed to implementing, maintaining, and continuously improving a quality management system that conforms to the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard, in order to fully meet the needs and expectations of its clients.

The company's activity is based on industrial coordination between clients located mainly in Europe and manufacturing partners located in China. In this context, lasting client satisfaction, conformity of supplied products, control of deadlines, and reliability of logistics operations constitute essential priorities.

The manager is personally committed to:

- Handling all client requests with responsiveness and rigor
- Ensuring the consistency of commercial offers and the control of pricing conditions
- Effectively coordinating industrial partners in order to guarantee the quality and conformity of products
- Monitoring production, transport, and delivery operations
- Managing non-conformities and implementing actions aimed at preventing their recurrence
- Protecting the confidentiality of technical information and intellectual property
- Continuously improving the organization, working methods, and overall performance of the quality management system

This quality policy constitutes the reference framework for the establishment of quality objectives, performance analysis, and the implementation of improvement actions.

It is communicated, understood, and applied throughout all of the company's activities and is subject to regular review in order to guarantee its relevance and its adequacy with the evolution of the context and client expectations.

Done at Cudrefin, on February 10, 2026

Roxane Wicky -- Manager of Plus Sarl

## 5.3 Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities Within the Organization

Given the size of the company, all roles, responsibilities, and authorities related to the quality management system are held by the manager.

In this capacity, she:

- Makes decisions relating to client relations, commercial offers, transport organization, delivery conditions, and the handling of non-conformities
- Defines operational orientations and the actions necessary for client satisfaction and the conformity of products and services
- Ensures coordination with industrial partners, carriers, and other relevant interested parties
- Oversees the implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the quality management system

Prices related to production and transport are communicated by suppliers and carriers. The manager adapts the commercial conditions accordingly, while retaining control over final decisions with respect to clients and the possibility of exchanges or adjustments depending on the situations.

This organization guarantees clarity of responsibilities, decisional responsiveness, and overall control of activities in accordance with the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

## 5.3 Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities Within the Organization

Given the international industrial coordination activity of Plus Sarl, roles and responsibilities are structured according to the following functions:

### Direction

- Definition of the strategy and orientations of the company
- Establishment and maintenance of the quality policy
- Definition and monitoring of quality objectives
- Allocation of necessary resources
- Conduct of the management review
- Compliance with ISO 9001 requirements
- Steering of continuous improvement

### Commercial

- Analysis of client needs
- Preparation of commercial offers
- Validation of orders
- Communication with clients
- Handling of complaints

### Purchasing

- Selection and evaluation of industrial partners
- Transmission of technical requirements
- Coordination of production
- Compliance with manufacturing deadlines
- Control of applicable quality requirements

### Logistics

- Responsibility for organizing international transport
- Preparation and compliance of shipping documents
- Delivery tracking
- Informing clients in the event of a logistics issue

### Quality

- Management of the quality management system
- Document control
- Management of non-conformities
- Implementation of corrective actions
- Conduct of internal audits
- Monitoring of indicators and performance

This functional distribution guarantees clarity of responsibilities, control of processes, and the effectiveness of the quality management system.

## 9.1 Monitoring, Measurement, Analysis and Evaluations

Plus Sarl determines the elements to be monitored and measured in order to evaluate the performance of its processes and the effectiveness of its quality management system.

The analyzed data primarily comes from the FileMaker system, customer exchanges, and the monitoring of industrial partners.

### 1. Delivery Time Monitoring

The company monitors:

- the order date
- the expected delivery date
- the actual delivery date

This data allows:

- identifying any delays
- analyzing the cause (production, transport, or other factors)
- evaluating overall logistics performance

Periodic monitoring allows identifying annual or semi-annual trends.
To be implemented.

### 2. Product Conformity Monitoring

The number and nature of nonconformities are tracked.

Nonconformities may be related to:

- a production defect
- a labeling or packaging error
- an occasional manufacturing problem

The analysis allows identifying:

- the suppliers involved
- recurring causes
- the corrective actions to be implemented

### 3. Supplier Monitoring

The performance of industrial partners is evaluated based on:

- compliance with deadlines
- the level of product quality
- responsiveness in case of problems
- the stability of commercial conditions

This evaluation allows identifying the most reliable suppliers and those requiring enhanced monitoring.

### 4. Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction is evaluated through:

- feedback received by email
- the stability and repetition of orders
- the absence of customer loss related to quality problems
- any complaints received

A satisfaction summary can be produced periodically.

### 5. Analysis Frequency

The overall performance analysis is carried out at least once a year as part of the management review.

Intermediate analyses may be performed if necessary.

The results of the analyses allow:

- identifying areas for improvement
- adapting operational processes
- strengthening risk management

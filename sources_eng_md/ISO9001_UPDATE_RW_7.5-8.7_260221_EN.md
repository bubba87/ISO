# UPDATE -- 7.5 Documented Information

Plus Sàrl determines, creates, updates, and controls the documented information necessary for the effectiveness of its quality management system and the conformity of the products and services provided.

The main documented information is stored on the following media:

- The FileMaker management system, used for the product library, order tracking, pricing, and transport
- Email, enabling traceability of exchanges with clients, industrial partners, carriers, and other interested parties
- Technical files kept by industrial partners in China, particularly for certain technical and production data
- Files saved on the company's computer

Plus Sàrl ensures:

- Accessibility limited to authorized persons only
- Readability and identification of documents
- Appropriate retention of records
- Protection against any loss, deterioration, or uncontrolled use

Technical, commercial, and contractual data, including designs and specifications belonging to clients, are treated as strictly confidential.

Confidentiality is ensured notably through:

- Secure access to computer systems
- Limitation of exchanges to the relevant contacts
- Structured organization of digital media
- Protection of access to databases and email systems
- Controlled transmission of information to industrial partners

These provisions guarantee the traceability of operations, the protection of clients' intellectual property, compliance with regulatory requirements, and the continuity of activities.

## 8.1 Planning and Control of Operational Activities

Plus Sàrl plans and controls the processes necessary for the realization of products and services in order to meet client requirements.

Upon receipt of an order or a request:

- The request is analyzed to verify its technical, logistical, or commercial feasibility
- If necessary, technical elements are transmitted to the industrial partner for a feasibility study and deadline confirmation
- Production deadlines are confirmed before final validation with the client
- The order is recorded in the FileMaker system, ensuring its traceability
- An acknowledgment of receipt is sent to the client, confirming quantities, prices, and deadlines

In the event that the company is unable to respond to a request (for example, for a new product outside the scope of competency), the request may be refused in order to guarantee conformity and control of commitments.

This organization ensures that only feasible and controlled orders are accepted.

---

## 8.2 Requirements Related to Products and Services

Plus Sàrl determines, analyzes, and validates the requirements related to products and services before any order acceptance.

Client requests are received in the form of purchase orders or by email. They may include:

- Ordered quantities
- Desired deadlines
- Product references or specific versions
- Technical specifications (dimensions, colors, specific modifications)
- Packaging requirements
- Logistics requirements or delivery location

In the case of a new product or technical modification, a drawing or specifications may be provided.

Before validation, Plus Sàrl conducts a review of requirements in order to verify:

- Complete understanding of the needs
- Technical feasibility with the industrial partner
- Production capacity
- Logistics conditions
- Consistency of commercial conditions

Any incomplete or ambiguous requirement is subject to clarification with the client.

Technical elements are transmitted to the industrial partners for confirmation when necessary.

The validated order is recorded in the FileMaker system to ensure traceability. An acknowledgment of receipt is sent to the client confirming quantities, prices, and deadlines.

Any subsequent modification is subject to:

- A new analysis
- Supplier validation if necessary
- Written confirmation to the client
- An update in the management system

Only orders deemed feasible and controlled are accepted.

## 8.4 Control of Industrial Partners and Suppliers

Plus Sàrl relies on industrial partners and, when necessary, on subcontractors.

The selection of partners is based on:

- Industrial experience and technical competence
- The ability to produce according to client specifications
- The quality of facilities and observed working conditions
- Reliability and stability of deadlines
- The ability to meet deadlines
- Responsiveness and flexibility
- Consistency of pricing conditions

The main industrial partner, in a longstanding and durable relationship with the company, participates in the identification and selection of new subcontractors when necessary.

On-site visits may be carried out to assess technical and organizational capabilities.

### Supplier Evaluation and Monitoring

The performance of industrial partners is continuously evaluated based on:

- Compliance with production deadlines
- The quality level of delivered products
- The non-conformity rate and responsiveness in case of problems
- The stability of commercial conditions

In the event of repeated malfunctions, an analysis is carried out and a decision may be made to seek an alternative partner.

Given the specific nature of production (small technical series), a change of supplier is subject to a prior evaluation of feasibility and availability.

A formal reinforcement of supplier monitoring is being structured to improve the traceability of evaluations.

### Validation of New Tooling

During the development of a new mold or a tooling modification:

- Technical specifications are validated with the client
- A sample or initial production run may be submitted for validation
- Series production only begins after client confirmation

Validations are kept in the form of written email exchanges.

### Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Designs, drawings, and technical specifications belong to the client and are treated as confidential.

Molds developed for clients are stored at industrial partners in China.

Technical information is transmitted only to the relevant partners and is not disclosed without authorization.

## 8.5 Control of Externalized Production and Release

The production of parts is carried out by industrial partners in China.

Product conformity is ensured through:

- The transmission of technical specifications to industrial partners
- Feasibility validation before production launch
- The performance of an internal quality control by the manufacturer before shipment
- The issuance of a quality control report (to be formalized and standardized during the trip to China)

In the event of a non-conformity detected before shipment, a new production run or correction is carried out before any client delivery.

---

## 8.6 Release of Products and Services

The release of products is carried out after confirmation by the industrial partner that the parts conform to the defined requirements.

The release is based on:

- The quality control performed by the manufacturer
- The confirmation of conformity
- When necessary, photos or supporting evidence transmitted

An improvement in the formalism of the release process will be implemented to strengthen the documentary traceability of controls performed.

---

## 8.7 Control of Non-Conforming Products

In the event of a non-conformity reported after delivery:

- The problem is analyzed based on the information and photos transmitted by the client
- A discussion is organized with the industrial partner to identify the cause
- A decision is made regarding replacement, correction, or exceptional acceptance
- Corrective actions are defined to avoid repetition

A formalized register of non-conformities will be implemented in the FileMaker system to ensure:

- Traceability of incidents
- Root cause analysis
- Monitoring of corrective actions

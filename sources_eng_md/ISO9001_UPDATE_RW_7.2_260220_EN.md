# UPDATE -- 7.2 Competencies

Plus Sàrl determines the competencies necessary for the implementation of its activities and the maintenance of conformity of the products and services provided.

Given the single-person structure, the manager possesses and implements the organizational, interpersonal, technical, and decision-making competencies essential to the proper functioning of the company, notably:

- A high level of availability ensuring service continuity and responsiveness towards clients and partners
- The ability to define priorities in the overall management of the company and to focus on essential day-to-day actions
- The aptitude to assess the limits of the company's competencies and to refuse or halt a product when a conforming solution cannot be guaranteed
- Interpersonal competencies and a human sense enabling the management of client complaints, the maintenance of a relationship of trust, and the preservation of the quality of relationships with suppliers
- The ability to implement quick solutions to resolve problems, including through commercial adjustments when necessary for client satisfaction
- The ability to communicate in English with international partners
- The availability to travel if necessary to industrial partners in China, to clients, or to customs authorities in order to resolve a critical situation

The manager ensures the maintenance and development of these competencies through experience, daily practice of the activity, continuous communication with partners, and the consideration of client feedback, in accordance with the requirements of the standard.

## 7.2 Competencies

Plus Sàrl determines the competencies necessary to ensure product conformity and the control of the processes defined in chapter 5.3.

The required competencies cover notably:

- International industrial coordination
- Analysis of client requirements
- International logistics management
- Control of non-conformities
- Professional communication in English
- Decision-making and priority management

Competencies are maintained through:

- Professional experience
- Continuous practice of the activity
- Technical and sector monitoring
- Client feedback and performance analysis

## Extension to Suppliers

**Competencies of Industrial Partners**

Plus Sàrl ensures that industrial partners have the technical competencies necessary for manufacturing parts that conform to client specifications.

This assurance is based on:

- The selection of experienced partners
- The analysis of their technical capability
- A lasting relationship and performance monitoring
- The evaluation of deadlines and product conformity
- The management of non-conformities and corrective actions if necessary

Partner competencies are continuously evaluated through:

- Compliance with deadlines
- Conformity of delivered parts
- Stability of the industrial relationship

# UPDATE -- 7.4 Communication

Plus Sàrl determines the internal and external communications necessary for the proper functioning of its quality management system, taking into account the interested parties, the information to be transmitted, and the appropriate means of communication.

Communication with clients is carried out primarily by email. The telephone may be used on an occasional basis, particularly in cases of urgency or situations requiring a rapid response.

Communication with industrial partners in China is primarily ensured via the WeChat application, supplemented when necessary by email exchanges.

Communication with carriers is carried out mainly by email and, for the main carrier, also via WeChat. Exchanges with the freight forwarder are carried out primarily by email.

## 7.4 Communication

Plus Sàrl determines the internal and external communications necessary for the proper functioning of its quality management system, taking into account:

- The relevant interested parties
- The information to be transmitted
- The associated responsibilities
- Confidentiality and data protection requirements

### External Communication

Communication with:

- **Clients** is carried out primarily by email. The telephone may be used on an occasional basis in case of urgency.
- **Industrial partners in China** is primarily ensured via the WeChat application, supplemented if necessary by email exchanges.
- **Carriers and freight forwarders** is carried out mainly by email, and for some via WeChat.

### Confidentiality Control

Plus Sàrl ensures the protection of technical, commercial, and contractual information transmitted within the scope of its activities.

To this end:

- Technical data belonging to clients is treated as confidential
- Access to computer systems and email is secured
- Information is transmitted only to the relevant parties
- Documents are stored in an organized manner in the internal management system
- Industrial partners are informed of the confidential nature of the transmitted technical data

This organization guarantees the confidentiality of information and the protection of intellectual property.

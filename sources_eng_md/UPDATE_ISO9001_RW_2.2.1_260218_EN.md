## 2.2.1 Analysis and Validation of Client Requests

The company favours a lasting relationship with a limited number of clients based on trust, responsiveness and a thorough understanding of their technical and operational needs. This proximity enables:

- effective management of urgent requests
- rapid adaptation to changes
- continuous support in development and optimisation

This approach is part of a long-term partnership logic.

Any request, whether it is an order, a quotation request, a planned modification or a complaint, is received mainly by e-mail at the sales department and exceptionally by telephone in case of emergency.

Each request is subject to a preliminary analysis carried out according to the responsibilities defined in the M1 Leadership organisation chart: assignment of responsibilities, in order to verify:

- understanding of needs expressed by the client: Process O1 Sales
- product references, quantities, deadlines: Process O1 Sales
- technical and logistical feasibility: Process O2 Purchasing & Subcontracting
- applicable quality requirements: Process O4 Quality Control

When necessary, Plus Sàrl consults the industrial partners to confirm with the relevant managers:

- feasibility and production conditions: Process O1 Sales
- manufacturing deadlines and conditions: Process O2 Purchasing & Subcontracting
- delivery deadlines and conditions: Process O3 Logistics

This step constitutes the order review, ensuring that client requirements are clearly defined, understood and achievable before any acceptance.

After validation:

- information is recorded in the FileMaker system, ensuring order traceability
- an order acknowledgement is sent to the client, confirming quantities, price and estimated deadline

Any subsequent modification requested by the client is subject to:

- analysis of the request according to assigned role: M1 - Leadership
- validation by the industrial partner if necessary
- formal confirmation by e-mail from the client before implementation
- update in the FileMaker system

This organisation ensures control of client requirements, traceability of decisions and securing of order acceptance.

## 1.1 Purpose of the Quality Manual

This Quality Manual describes the Quality Management System (QMS) of **Plus Sarl**, in accordance with the requirements of **ISO 9001:2015**.

It constitutes the reference document of the QMS and aims to:

- Define the scope of the QMS
- Present the organization, the processes and their interactions
- Describe the arrangements made to satisfy the requirements of ISO 9001:2015
- Serve as a reference for internal and external audits
- Communicate Management's commitment to quality

---

## 1.2 Scope

The QMS of Plus Sarl covers all activities related to **industrial monitoring and global sourcing**, including:

- Sales management and customer relations
- Selection, qualification and evaluation of international suppliers
- Monitoring and quality control of outsourced production
- Logistics coordination and international shipping
- Document management and support processes

### Exclusions

In accordance with clause 4.3 of ISO 9001:2015, clause **8.3 (Design and development)** is excluded from the scope. Plus Sarl does not carry out product design; the company provides sourcing and quality control of products designed by its customers or suppliers.

## 2.1 Presentation of Plus Sarl

Plus Sarl is a company specializing in **industrial monitoring and global sourcing**. The company acts as a qualified intermediary between its customers and an international network of industrial suppliers, ensuring the conformity, quality and traceability of sourced products.

### Areas of activity

| Area                           | Description                                                                 |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Global industrial sourcing     | Identification and qualification of international suppliers                 |
| Production monitoring          | Monitoring and quality control of outsourced production                     |
| Inspection and quality control | Performing inspections at various stages of production                      |
| Consulting and support         | Technical and logistics assistance for procurement operations               |

---

## 2.2 Outsourced production model

Plus Sarl operates under a **fully outsourced production model**. The company does not own its own manufacturing facilities. Its role consists of:

1. **Selecting** the most suitable suppliers for customer requirements
2. **Qualifying** the production capabilities of industrial partners
3. **Supervising** critical manufacturing stages
4. **Controlling** product quality before shipment
5. **Coordinating** international logistics

This model requires rigorous control of outsourced processes in accordance with ISO 9001:2015, clause 8.4.

## 2.6 Demand analysis workflow

The customer demand analysis process follows these steps:

### Step 1: Receipt of the request
- The **Sales Role** receives the customer request
- Registration in the management system
- Assignment of a unique reference number

### Step 2: Feasibility analysis
- Evaluation of technical specifications
- Verification of qualified supplier availability
- Analysis of logistics and timeline constraints
- Consultation with the **Purchasing Role** and the **Quality Role** if necessary

### Step 3: Requirements review

| Review element                 | Responsible        | Validation criterion                      |
|--------------------------------|--------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Technical specifications       | Quality Role       | Conformity with applicable standards       |
| Supplier capability            | Purchasing Role    | Qualified supplier available               |
| Lead times                     | Logistics Role     | Consistency with customer expectations     |
| Commercial conditions          | Sales Role         | Profitability and acceptable conditions    |
| Regulatory requirements        | Quality Role       | Conformity with applicable regulations     |

### Step 4: Offer and validation
- Issuance of the commercial offer by the **Sales Role**
- Possible negotiation with the customer
- Final validation and order acceptance

### Step 5: Operational launch
- Transmission of the file to the **Purchasing Role** to launch the supplier order
- Inspection planning by the **Quality Role**
- Logistics organization by the **Logistics Role**

## 2.7 Customer commitments

Plus Sarl commits to its customers on the following points:

1. **Transparency**: clear and regular communication on order progress
2. **Quality**: rigorous conformity control of products to specifications
3. **Responsiveness**: rapid handling of requests and complaints
4. **Traceability**: complete documentation of each process step
5. **Continual improvement**: systematic consideration of customer feedback

## 5.2 Quality policy

### 5.2.1 Establishing the quality policy

The quality policy of Plus Sarl is founded on **five pillars** that guide all company activities and decisions:

---

#### Pillar 1: Excellence in customer service

> Plus Sarl commits to understanding, anticipating and meeting the requirements of its customers worldwide, by providing monitoring and sourcing services of an impeccable quality level.

**Operational translation:**
- In-depth analysis of each customer request
- Transparent and regular communication
- Systematic measurement of customer satisfaction

## 6.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities

### 6.1.1 Risk identification methodology

Plus Sarl applies a structured approach to identifying and addressing risks and opportunities:

1. **Identification**: inventory of risks and opportunities related to the context (cf. MQ_04)
2. **Analysis**: assessment of likelihood of occurrence and severity of impact
3. **Prioritization**: ranking by criticality level
4. **Treatment**: definition of actions to control risks or exploit opportunities
5. **Monitoring**: surveillance of the effectiveness of actions implemented

### 6.1.2 Assessment scale

**Likelihood of occurrence:**

| Level | Label        | Description                               |
|--------|-------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1      | Rare         | Less than once per year                   |
| 2      | Occasional   | One to two times per year                 |
| 3      | Probable     | Several times per year                    |
| 4      | Frequent     | Several times per quarter                 |

**Severity of impact:**

| Level | Label        | Description                               |
|--------|-------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1      | Minor        | Negligible impact on operations            |
| 2      | Moderate     | Limited impact, manageable without difficulty |
| 3      | Major        | Significant impact on quality or timelines |
| 4      | Critical     | Severe impact on customer satisfaction or sustainability |

**Criticality = Likelihood x Severity**

| Criticality level | Range | Required action                              |
|---------------------|-------|---------------------------------------------|
| Low                 | 1-4   | Monitoring, no immediate action required     |
| Medium              | 5-8   | Reduction actions to be planned              |
| High                | 9-12  | Priority actions to be implemented           |
| Critical            | 13-16 | Mandatory immediate actions                  |

| Action                               | Description                                                      | Responsible        | Timeline    |

| Analysis method                      | Description                                                      | Application                     |

| Element to analyze                   | Key questions                                                    |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|

| Method                               | Was the procedure followed? Was it adequate?                     |

# PM01 - Leadership and Steering Process

| **Process**        | PM01 - Leadership and Steering                         |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **Type**            | Management                                            |

| **Version**         | v0.7                                                  |

| **Reference**       | PR-PM01-LEAD                                          |
| **ISO 9001 Standard** | Chapters 5, 6, 9.3, 10                             |

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process defines the strategic steering activities of the Quality Management System (QMS) of **Plus Sarl**, an international industrial monitoring and sourcing company. It covers the definition of the quality policy, planning of objectives, resource allocation, conduct of management reviews, risk management, indicator monitoring and continual improvement steering.

---

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 9001:2015, Chapters 5 (Leadership), 6 (Planning), 9.3 (Management review), 10 (Improvement)
- Quality Manual Plus Sarl (MQ-001)
- Quality Policy Plus Sarl

---

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role                        | Main responsibilities                                                |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Management**              | Strategic steering, quality policy, resource allocation              |
| **Quality Manager**         | QMS facilitation, indicator monitoring, audit coordination           |
| **Process owners**          | Operational implementation, reporting, improvement proposals         |

---

## 4. Inputs and outputs

### Inputs
- Internal and external audit results
- Customer and interested party feedback
- Process performance and indicators
- Status of corrective and preventive actions
- Risk and opportunity analysis
- Regulatory and standards evolutions
- Supplier evaluation results

### Outputs
- Validated quality policy
- Measurable quality objectives
- Resource plan
- Management review decisions
- Continual improvement plan
- Updated risk map

---

## 5. Activity descriptions

### A1 - Definition and communication of the quality policy

Management defines the quality policy aligned with the strategy of Plus Sarl. It ensures its communication to all employees and its adequacy with the company's international context.

### A2 - Planning of quality objectives

Management establishes measurable quality objectives, consistent with the quality policy. These objectives are deployed by process and monitored quarterly (cf. FM-PM01-OBJ).

### A3 - Resource allocation and management

Management identifies and allocates the necessary resources (human, material, financial, informational) for the effective operation of the QMS and international operations.

### A4 - Conduct of management reviews

Management conducts planned management reviews (minimum 2 per year) following the FM-PM01-RD format, integrating all input data required by ISO 9001:2015.

### A5 - Risk and opportunity management

Management steers the identification, evaluation and treatment of risks and opportunities related to the context of the organization, including risks related to international sourcing and monitoring operations.

### A6 - Performance indicator monitoring

Management reviews the key performance indicator (KPI) dashboards for each process, analyzes trends and initiates necessary actions.

### A7 - Continual improvement steering

Management identifies improvement opportunities, validates action plans, monitors their implementation and evaluates their effectiveness.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process describes the sales activities of **Plus Sarl** from receipt of a customer request to satisfaction measurement. It covers the entire sales cycle for industrial monitoring and global sourcing activities.

---

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 9001:2015, Chapters 8.2 (Requirements for products and services), 8.5 (Production and service provision), 9.1.2 (Customer satisfaction)
- Quality Manual Plus Sarl (MQ-001)

---

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role                        | Main responsibilities                                                |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Sales**                   | Request management, offers, contracts, customer follow-up            |
| **Management**              | Validation of strategic offers, approval of major contracts          |
| **Quality**                 | Support for quality requirements, complaint handling                 |
| **Customer**                | Expression of need, offer validation, satisfaction feedback          |

---

## 4. Inputs and outputs

### Inputs
- Customer request (specifications, RFQ)
- Applicable regulatory and normative requirements
- Customer history and commercial conditions
- Plus Sarl service catalog

### Outputs
- Validated commercial offer
- Signed contract / purchase order
- Internal execution order
- Customer satisfaction report
- Commercial performance indicators

---

## 5. Activity descriptions

### A1 - Reception and registration of the customer request

The Sales Role receives the customer request (email, phone, online platform), registers it in the tracking system and assigns a unique reference number.

### A2 - Analysis and review of customer requirements

The Sales Role analyzes the request to verify technical, logistical and commercial feasibility. Requirements are reviewed with processes P02 (Purchasing), P03 (Logistics) and P04 (Quality Control) if necessary.

### A3 - Commercial offer preparation

The Sales Role prepares the offer including: service description, pricing conditions, timelines, delivery conditions (Incoterms), payment terms and quality requirements.

### A4 - Validation and sending of the offer

The offer is validated according to defined approval thresholds (Management for strategic offers) then transmitted to the customer.

### A5 - Negotiation and adjustment

The Sales Role conducts negotiations with the customer, adjusts the offer if necessary and documents the modifications made.

### A6 - Contracting

After agreement, the Sales Role formalizes the contract or purchase order, ensures both parties sign and triggers the internal execution order.

### A7 - Transmission to operational processes

The Sales Role transmits the necessary information to processes P02 (Purchasing/Sourcing), P03 (Logistics) and P04 (Quality Control) for order execution.

### A8 - Execution follow-up and customer communication

The Sales Role monitors progress with operational processes and communicates regularly with the customer on the status of their order.

### A9 - Customer satisfaction measurement

The Sales Role steers customer satisfaction measurement via surveys (FM-P01-SAT), feedback analysis and complaint handling.

| **ISO 9001 Standard** | Chapters 8.4, 8.6                                  |

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process describes the purchasing and subcontracting activities of **Plus Sarl** for its international industrial monitoring and sourcing operations. It covers the selection, qualification and evaluation of suppliers worldwide, as well as order management and product/service reception.

---

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 9001:2015, Chapters 8.4 (Control of externally provided processes, products and services), 8.6 (Release of products and services)
- Quality Manual Plus Sarl (MQ-001)

---

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role                        | Main responsibilities                                                |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Purchasing**              | Sourcing, negotiation, orders, supplier follow-up                    |
| **Quality**                 | Supplier qualification, audits, nonconformity follow-up              |
| **Management**              | Validation of strategic suppliers, budgets                           |
| **Logistics**               | Coordination of international reception and transport                |
| **Supplier**                | Conforming delivery, SQA compliance, communication                   |

---

## 4. Inputs and outputs

### Inputs
- Purchasing needs from process P01 (Sales)
- Specifications / technical requirements
- Existing supplier panel
- Supplier performance history
- Applicable regulatory requirements by country

### Outputs
- Validated purchase orders
- Signed Supplier Quality Agreement (SQA)
- Supplier evaluation (FM-P02-EVAL)
- Updated supplier panel
- Conforming products/services received

---

## 5. Activity descriptions

### A1 - Purchasing need identification

The Purchasing Role receives the purchasing need from process P01 or an internal need. It verifies specification completeness and identifies potential international supply sources.

### A2 - Supplier search and pre-selection

The Purchasing Role identifies potential suppliers on international markets, analyzes their capabilities and proceeds to pre-selection based on defined criteria (capacity, certifications, location, references).

### A3 - Supplier qualification

The Purchasing Role, in coordination with the Quality Role, proceeds to qualify pre-selected suppliers: on-site or remote audit, certification verification, production capacity evaluation, Supplier Quality Agreement signing (FM-P02-AQF).

### A4 - Consultation and negotiation

The Purchasing Role launches consultations with qualified suppliers, compares offers (price, timelines, conditions, Incoterms) and conducts negotiations.

### A5 - Order placement

The Purchasing Role issues the purchase order with complete specifications, quality requirements, delivery timelines and logistics conditions (Incoterms, transport mode).

### A6 - Order follow-up and reminders

The Purchasing Role monitors order execution with the supplier, verifies timeline compliance and triggers reminders if necessary.

### A7 - Reception and control

The Purchasing Role coordinates with the Logistics Role and Quality Role for product/service reception. Controls defined by process P04 are triggered (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check as applicable).

### A8 - Periodic supplier evaluation

The Purchasing Role, in coordination with the Quality Role, conducts periodic supplier evaluations according to weighted criteria defined in FM-P02-EVAL. Suppliers are classified A/B/C and the panel is updated.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process describes the quality control activities of **Plus Sarl** for its international industrial monitoring and sourcing operations. It covers the four types of inspection (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check), nonconformity management, supplier audits and quality data analysis.

---

## 2. Normative references

- Quality Manual Plus Sarl (MQ-001)

---

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role                        | Main responsibilities                                          |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Quality**                 | Inspection planning, control execution, NC management, reporting |
| **Purchasing**              | Communication of supplier requirements, corrective action follow-up |
| **Sales**                   | Transmission of customer requirements, criteria validation           |
| **Management**              | Arbitration on critical NCs, validation of major actions      |
| **Supplier**                | Making available for inspection, NC processing                |

---

## 4. Types of inspection

| Type            | Full name                            | Timing                           | Objective                                    |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **IPC**         | Initial Production Check             | Start of production              | Verify raw materials, components and production parameters |
| **DUPRO**       | During Production Check              | During production (30-50%)       | Monitor in-process quality, detect deviations |
| **PSI**         | Pre-Shipment Inspection              | Production completed (100%)      | Final verification before shipment        |
| **Loading Check** | Loading check                      | At the time of loading           | Verify packaging, labeling and loading |

---

## 5. Input and output data

### Input data
- Specifications and customer requirements (P01)
- Purchase order and quality requirements (P02)
- Supplier production schedule
- Applicable standards and regulations
- Defined acceptance criteria (AQL, tolerances)

### Output data
- Inspection reports (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check)
- Nonconformity sheets (FM-P03-NC)
- Release / rejection / sorting decision
- Supplier audit reports (FM-P03-AUD)
- Quality statistics and trends

---

## 6. Description of activities

### A1 - Inspection planning

The Quality Role defines the inspection plan based on the order, the supplier (history, classification) and customer requirements. It determines the type, timing and inspection criteria.

### A2 - Inspection preparation

The Quality Role prepares the inspection documents: checklist based on specifications, sampling plan (AQL), required measurement tools, acceptance/rejection criteria.

### A3 - Inspection execution (IPC)

The Quality Role performs the Initial Production Check at the start of production to verify raw materials, components, production parameters and the supplier's understanding of specifications.

### A4 - Inspection execution (DUPRO)

The Quality Role performs the During Production Check at 30-50% completion to monitor in-process product quality, detect deviations and take early corrective measures.

### A5 - Inspection execution (PSI)

The Quality Role performs the Pre-Shipment Inspection at 100% of completed production. Final inspection according to the AQL sampling plan. Release, rejection or sorting decision.

### A6 - Inspection execution (Loading Check)

The Quality Role performs the Loading Check at the time of loading to verify packaging, marking, labeling, quantity and loading conditions.

### A7 - Nonconformity management

The Quality Role identifies, records and classifies nonconformities according to 4 levels (cf. FM-P03-NC). It conducts root cause analysis, defines corrective actions and ensures their follow-up.

### A8 - Supplier audits

The Quality Role plans and performs supplier audits (on-site or remote) according to the annual program. The results feed the supplier evaluation (FM-P02-EVAL).

### A9 - Data analysis and quality reporting

The Quality Role compiles and analyzes quality data (conformity rate, NC trends, supplier performance), produces dashboards and presents the results in management review.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process describes the logistics activities of **Plus Sarl** covering shipment planning, international transport coordination, delivery tracking and confirmation of proper receipt. It applies to all logistics flows related to worldwide industrial monitoring and sourcing operations.

---

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 9001:2015, Chapters 8.5.4 (Preservation), 8.6 (Release of products and services)
- Quality Manual Plus Sarl (MQ-001)
- Applicable customs and international transport regulations

---

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role                        | Main responsibilities                                          |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Logistics**              | Planning, transport coordination, tracking, documentation          |
| **Purchasing**              | Transmission of supplier information, Incoterms conditions      |
| **Sales**              | Customer communication on timelines and tracking                      |
| **Quality**                 | Release of goods after inspection                         |
| **Freight Forwarder / Carrier** | Transport execution, customs clearance                              |

---

## 4. Input and output data

### Input data
- Validated and confirmed purchase order (P01/P02)
- Quality release confirmation (P04)
- Supplier information (address, contact, conditions)
- Customer requirements (timeline, location, Incoterms)
- Applicable customs regulations

### Output data
- Shipment plan
- Transport documents (BL, AWB, CMR, packing list)
- Customs declarations
- Proof of delivery (POD)
- Customer receipt confirmation

---

## 5. Description of activities

### A1 - Shipment planning

The Logistics Role receives the validated order information and plans the shipment: transport mode (sea, air, road, rail), timelines, volumes, special packaging and handling requirements.

### A2 - Freight forwarder / carrier selection

The Logistics Role selects the appropriate freight forwarder or carrier from qualified providers, taking into account cost, timelines, reliability and destination.

### A3 - Documentation preparation

The Logistics Role prepares or coordinates the preparation of necessary documents: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, customs documents, bill of lading (BL) or air waybill (AWB).

### A4 - Collection and loading coordination

The Logistics Role coordinates the collection of goods from the supplier, ensures proper execution of the Loading Check (P04) where applicable, and confirms loading.

### A5 - In-transit monitoring

The Logistics Role ensures real-time transport tracking, manages contingencies (delays, damages, customs holds) and proactively communicates updates to the Sales Role for customer information.

### A6 - Customs clearance and final delivery

The Logistics Role coordinates customs clearance operations at destination, organizes final delivery to the customer and ensures compliance with agreed conditions.

### A7 - Delivery confirmation and closure

The Logistics Role obtains the proof of delivery (POD), confirms proper receipt to the Sales Role and the customer, archives documents and closes the logistics file.

## 4. Coding system

### 4.1 Document types

| Code Prefix | Document type          | Description                                          |
|-------------|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **MQ**      | Quality Manual             | Strategic document describing the QMS                |
| **PR**      | Procedure                  | Detailed description of a process                 |
| **IT**      | Work instruction     | Detailed operating procedure for a specific task   |
| **FM**      | Form                 | Template to be completed to record data        |
| **EN**      | Record             | Evidence of the execution of an activity              |

### 4.2 Coding convention

The document code follows the structure:

```
[TYPE]-[PROCESS]-[IDENTIFIER]
```

**Examples:**
- `MQ-001`: Quality Manual, main document
- `PR-P01-COM`: Procedure for process P01 Sales
- `IT-P04-ECH`: AQL sampling work instruction (P04)
- `FM-P02-AQF`: Supplier Quality Agreement form (P02)
- `EN-P03-POD`: Proofs of delivery records (P03)

### 4.3 Version convention

Versions follow the format **vX.Y**:
- **X** (major): Significant structural or substantive modification
- **Y** (minor): Correction, adjustment, minor update

Examples: v0.1, v0.7, v1.0, v1.1, v2.0

## 5. Input and output data

- Normative and regulatory requirements
- Audit feedback (internal or external)
- Requests from process owners
- Obsolete documents to be revised

### Output data
- Approved and distributed documents
- Updated document register (FM-PS01-GD)
- Obsolete documents archived or destroyed
- Version history

---

## 6. Description of activities

### A1 - Documentation need identification

The Document Management Role or the Process owner identifies the need to create, modify or delete a document. The need is formalized and justified.

### A2 - Document drafting or revision

The designated author (generally the relevant Process owner) drafts or revises the document in accordance with defined templates and coding. The document is identified with its code, version, date and status.

### A3 - Verification

The Document Management Role verifies the document conformity: compliance with coding, consistency with the QMS, absence of contradictions, completeness.

### A4 - Approval

The document is approved by the authorized responsible person according to the approval matrix:
- Quality Manual, Policy: Management
- Procedures: Quality Manager
- Work instructions, Forms: Process owner

### A5 - Distribution and availability

The Document Management Role distributes the approved document to the relevant recipients, updates the document register (FM-PS01-GD) and ensures the withdrawal of obsolete versions.

### A6 - Record management and archiving

The Document Management Role ensures the conservation of records according to defined retention periods, under conditions guaranteeing their readability, integrity and accessibility.

### A7 - Periodic review and destruction

The Document Management Role conducts a periodic (annual) review of the documentation to identify documents to be updated or destroyed. Documents beyond their retention period are destroyed in a controlled manner.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process defines the activities related to the steering of continual improvement within the QMS of **Plus Sarl**. It covers the collection and analysis of improvement data, nonconformity and corrective action management, PDCA cycle steering, and the annual improvement program.

This process interacts with all QMS processes and directly feeds the management review (PM01).

---

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 9001:2015, Chapter 10.1 (General), 10.2 (Nonconformity and corrective action), 10.3 (Continual improvement)

|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|

### Output data

## 5. Description of activities

### A1 - Collection of improvement data

| Source                              | Data collected                               | Frequency         | Responsible       |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Internal audits                     | Findings, NCs, observations, strengths         | Per program    | Quality Role      |
| Customer complaints                | Complaint sheets, analyses                  | Continuous           | Sales Role   |

| Supplier evaluations            | Scores, trends, alerts                       | Semi-annual       | Purchasing Role       |
| Performance indicators          | KPI dashboards per process               | Monthly          | Quality Role      |
| Management reviews                 | Decisions, actions to be taken                       | Semi-annual       | Management         |
| Collaborator suggestions          | Improvement ideas, field feedback            | Continuous           | All roles    |
| Normative and competitive intelligence | Standards changes, market practices              | Quarterly      | Quality Role      |

### A2 - Nonconformity processing

The nonconformity processing follows the 6-step process defined in MQ_10:

1. **Detection and recording** -- Opening NC sheet (NC-YYYY-NNN)
2. **Immediate action** -- Isolation, notification, securing
3. **Root cause analysis** -- Five Whys, Ishikawa, cause tree
4. **Corrective actions** -- Definition, validation, implementation
5. **Effectiveness verification** -- Control, evaluation, non-recurrence
6. **Capitalization** -- Document update, lessons learned, management review

### Nonconformity classification

| Level | Category        | Processing time | Approval required |
|--------|------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| 1      | **Minor**      | 30 days            | Quality Role        |
| 2      | **Significant** | 15 days           | Quality Role        |
| 3      | **Major**      | 5 days             | Management           |
| 4      | **Critical**     | Immediate            | Management           |

### A3 - PDCA cycle steering

The Quality Role facilitates the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle for each process:

| Phase   | Actions                                          | Responsible              | Frequency      |
|---------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| PLAN    | Analyze data, define improvement objectives, plan actions | Quality Role + Owners | Quarterly |
| DO      | Implement planned actions, collect data | Process owners   | Continuous       |
| CHECK   | Measure results, compare to objectives, analyze gaps | Quality Role          | Quarterly |
| ACT     | Standardize if successful, correct if gap, start new cycle | Management + Quality Role | Quarterly |

### A4 - Annual improvement program

| Step                               | Description                                              | Responsible       | Period    |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|------------|
| Previous year review            | Analysis of results, trends, action effectiveness | Quality Role       | Q4         |
| Priority areas identification | Selection of priorities based on data           | Management          | Q4         |
| Program definition             | Actions, responsible persons, resources, deadlines             | Quality Role       | Q4/Q1      |
| Validation                          | Approval by Management in management review        | Management          | Q1         |
| Quarterly follow-up                   | Progress review, adjustments                          | Quality Role       | Q1-Q4      |
| Annual review                        | Evaluation of program effectiveness                  | Management          | Q4         |

### A5 - Continual improvement tools

| Tool                    | Application                                              | Frequency              |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Trend analysis     | Tracking KPI evolution over time               | Monthly              |
| Pareto analysis           | Identification of main causes (80/20 rule)      | Quarterly          |
| Brainstorming            | Team generation of improvement ideas              | As needed           |
| Benchmarking             | Comparison with industry best practices     | Annual               |
| Lessons learned | Capitalization after each significant project          | At each closure       |
| Five Whys               | Root cause investigation                              | At each NC            |
| Ishikawa diagram     | Multi-cause analysis                                     | Complex NCs           |

# Process Chain - CHAIN-01: Existing Product Order

| **Document**       | CHAIN-01_Existing_Product_Order              |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Version**        | v0.7                                         |
| **Date**           | 2026-03-04                                   |
| **Classification** | Internal                                     |
| **Process**        | Process Chain - Operations                   |
| **Drafted by**     | Quality Role                                 |
| **Approved by**    | Management                                   |

---

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the complete process chain for the processing of an **existing product order** at Plus Sarl. It covers the standard flow applicable when a customer orders a product already referenced, with an already evaluated supplier and existing tooling.

---

## 2. Scope

This chain applies to any customer order for an existing product in the Plus Sarl catalog, regardless of the geographic location of the customer or the supplier.

---

## 3. Specifics of the "Existing Product" chain

| Characteristic                   | Description                                                        |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Development phase                | None — product already developed and validated                     |
| Supplier evaluation              | Already completed — supplier qualified and referenced              |
| Tooling                          | Existing — no tooling creation or modification required            |
| Lead time                        | The shortest among the 4 order types                               |
| Inspection                       | Standard quality control according to existing specifications      |

---

## 4. Process lines

The chain is structured around **4 process lines**:

| Code | Line              | Lead role            | Main function                                    |
|------|-------------------|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 01   | SALES             | Sales Role           | Sales management, customer relations, invoicing  |
| 02   | MANUFACTURE       | Purchasing Role      | Supplier coordination, production monitoring     |
| 03   | DELIVERY          | Logistics Role       | Transport, delivery, customs                     |
| 04   | QUALITY           | Quality Role         | Conformity control, quality validation           |

---

## 5. Overview of the 8 blocks

```
BLOCK 1         BLOCK 2         BLOCK 3         BLOCK 4
Receipt of      Order           Transport       Technical
order           sheet           sheet           study
[01 SALES]      [01 SALES]      [03 DELIVERY]   [02 MANUFACTURE]
    |               |               |               |
    v               v               v               v
BLOCK 5         BLOCK 6         BLOCK 7         BLOCK 8
Order           Production      Delivery        Goods
validation      & Quality       & Customs       acceptance
[01 SALES]      [02 MANUF.      [03 DELIVERY    [04 QUALITY
                 04 QUALITY]     01 SALES]        01 SALES]
```

---

| Supplier qualification         | Referencing decision, update of supplier panel                         | Purchasing Role    |
| Management approval            | Final approval of the new supplier by management                       | Management         |

---

## 6. Supplier selection criteria and audit process

### 6.1 Selection criteria grid

| Criterion                | Weighting   | Description                                                                 |
|--------------------------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Quality                  | 25%         | Quality system, certifications (ISO 9001, IATF, etc.), quality history      |
| Cost                     | 20%         | Price competitiveness, cost structure transparency                          |
| Deadline                 | 20%         | Ability to meet requested deadlines, responsiveness                         |
| Production capacity      | 15%         | Production resources, ability to absorb requested volumes                   |
| Certifications           | 10%         | Sector-specific certifications, environmental standards                     |
| CSR                      | 10%         | Corporate social responsibility, working conditions, environmental impact   |

### 6.2 Qualification

- **Minimum score for qualification**: 70/100
- The score is calculated based on the criteria grid above, each criterion being scored out of 100 then weighted.
- A supplier not reaching the minimum score cannot be referenced except by derogation approved by Management.

### 6.3 Audit process

- **On-site audit mandatory** for any order exceeding the threshold defined by Management.
- The audit covers: quality system, production resources, nonconformity management, traceability, working conditions.
- An audit report is formalized and archived. Identified gaps are subject to a corrective action plan with follow-up.
- For orders below the threshold, a remote document audit may be accepted subject to Quality Role validation.

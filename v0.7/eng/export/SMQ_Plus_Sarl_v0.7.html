1.1 Purpose of the Quality Manual

This Quality Manual describes the Quality Management System (QMS) of Plus Sàrl, in accordance with the requirements of ISO 9001:2015.

It constitutes the reference document of the QMS and its objectives are to:

  • Define the scope and field of application of the QMS
  • Present the organization, processes and their interactions
  • Describe the provisions taken to satisfy the requirements of ISO 9001:2015
  • Serve as a reference for internal and external audits
  • Communicate Management's commitments regarding quality

1.2 Scope

The QMS of Plus Sàrl covers all activities related to industrial monitoring and worldwide sourcing, including:

  • Sales management and customer relations
  • Selection, qualification and evaluation of international suppliers
  • Monitoring and quality control of outsourced production
  • Logistics coordination and international shipping
  • Document management and support processes

Exclusions

In accordance with clause 4.3 of ISO 9001:2015, clause 8.3 (Design and development) is excluded from the scope. Plus Sàrl does not perform product design; the company provides sourcing and quality control of products designed by its customers or suppliers.

2.6 Request analysis workflow

The customer request analysis process follows these steps:

Step 1: Receipt of the request

  • The Sales Role receives the customer request
  • Registration in the management system
  • Assignment of a unique reference number

Step 2: Feasibility analysis

  • Evaluation of technical specifications
  • Verification of qualified supplier availability
  • Analysis of logistics and lead time constraints
  • Consultation with the Purchasing Role and Quality Role if necessary

Step 3: Review of requirements

Review element Responsible Validation criterion
Technical specifications Quality Role Conformity with applicable standards
Supplier capacity Purchasing Role Qualified supplier available
Lead times Logistics Role Consistency with customer expectations
Commercial conditions Sales Role Profitability and acceptable conditions
Regulatory requirements Quality Role Conformity with applicable regulations

Step 4: Quotation and validation

  • Issuance of the commercial offer by the Sales Role
  • Possible negotiation with the customer
  • Final validation and order acceptance

Step 5: Operational launch

  • Transmission of the file to the Purchasing Role for supplier order placement
  • Inspection planning by the Quality Role
  • Logistics organization by the Logistics Role

5.2 Quality policy

5.2.1 Establishing the quality policy

The quality policy of Plus Sàrl is founded on five pillars that guide all activities and decisions of the company:

Pillar 1: Excellence in customer service

Plus Sàrl commits to understanding, anticipating and meeting the requirements of its customers worldwide, by providing monitoring and sourcing services of an impeccable quality level.

Operational translation: - In-depth analysis of each customer request - Transparent and regular communication - Systematic measurement of customer satisfaction

Pillar 2: Rigor in supplier control

Plus Sàrl commits to selecting, qualifying and evaluating its international suppliers according to strict and objective criteria, ensuring the conformity and reliability of the supply chain.

Operational translation: - Structured qualification process (AQF) - Periodic supplier evaluations - Classification and performance monitoring

Pillar 3: Product conformity and traceability

Plus Sàrl commits to implementing rigorous inspections at each critical stage of production, ensuring product conformity to specifications and their complete traceability.

Operational translation: - Structured inspection program (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check) - Exhaustive inspection documentation - Effective nonconformity management

Pillar 4: Continual improvement

Plus Sàrl commits to continually improving the effectiveness of its Quality Management System through data analysis, lessons learned and the implementation of corrective and preventive actions.

Operational translation: - Performance indicator monitoring (KPIs) - Periodic management reviews - PDCA cycle applied to all processes

Pillar 5: Competency development

Plus Sàrl commits to developing and maintaining the competencies of its team, ensuring the adequacy between the aptitudes required by each role and the competencies held.

Operational translation: - Competency matrix per role (see process PS02 — FM-PS02-MCO) - Adapted training plans - Periodic competency evaluations

6.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities

6.1.1 Risk identification methodology

Plus Sàrl applies a structured approach to identifying and addressing risks and opportunities:

  1. Identification: inventory of risks and opportunities related to the context (see MQ_04)
  2. Analysis: evaluation of the probability of occurrence and severity of impact
  3. Prioritization: ranking by criticality level
  4. Treatment: definition of actions to control risks or exploit opportunities
  5. Monitoring: surveillance of the effectiveness of implemented actions

6.1.2 Evaluation scale

Probability of occurrence:

Level Label Description
1 Rare Less than once per year
2 Occasional Once to twice per year
3 Probable Several times per year
4 Frequent Several times per quarter

Severity of impact:

Level Label Description
1 Minor Negligible impact on operations
2 Moderate Limited impact, manageable without difficulty
3 Major Significant impact on quality or delivery times
4 Critical Serious impact on customer satisfaction or business continuity

Criticality = Probability x Severity

Criticality level Range Required action
Low 1-4 Monitoring, no immediate action required
Medium 5-8 Reduction actions to be planned
High 9-12 Priority actions to be implemented
Critical 13-16 Mandatory immediate actions

The Logistics Role receives the validated order information and plans the shipment: transport mode (sea, air, road, rail), lead times, volumes, special packaging and handling requirements.

A2 - Freight forwarder / carrier selection

The Logistics Role selects the appropriate freight forwarder or carrier from qualified providers, considering cost, lead times, reliability and destination.

A3 - Documentation preparation

The Logistics Role prepares or coordinates the preparation of necessary documents: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, customs documents, bill of lading (BL) or air waybill (AWB).

A4 - Collection and loading coordination

The Logistics Role coordinates the collection of goods from the supplier, ensures proper execution of the Loading Check (P04) where applicable, and confirms loading.

A5 - In-transit tracking

The Logistics Role provides real-time transport tracking, manages contingencies (delays, damages, customs holds) and proactively communicates updates to the Sales Role for customer information.

A6 - Customs clearance and final delivery

The Logistics Role coordinates customs clearance operations at destination, organizes final delivery to the customer and ensures compliance with agreed conditions.

A7 - Delivery confirmation and closure

The Logistics Role obtains the proof of delivery (POD), confirms proper receipt to the Sales Role and the customer, archives the documents and closes the logistics file.

6. Criterions de sélection fournisseur et processus d'audit

6.1 Grille de critères de sélection

Criterion Pondération Description
Qualité 25 % Système qualité, certifications (ISO 9001, IATF, etc.), historique qualité
Coût 20 % Compétitivité des prix, transparence de la structure de coûts
Délai 20 % Capacité à respecter les délais demandés, réactivité
Capacité de production 15 % Moyens de production, capacité à absorber les volumes demandés
Certifications 10 % Certifications spécifiques au secteur, normes environnementales
RSE 10 % Responsibility sociétale, conditions de travail, impact environnemental

6.2 Qualification

  • Score minimum pour qualification : 70/100
  • Le score est calculé sur la base de la grille de critères ci-dessus, chaque critère étant noté sur 100 puis pondéré.
  • Un fournisseur n'atteignant pas le score minimum ne peut pas être référencé sauf dérogation validée par la Management.

6.3 Processus d'audit

  • Audit sur site obligatoire pour toute commande dont le montant dépasse le seuil défini par la Management.
  • L'audit couvre : système qualité, moyens de production, gestion des non-conformités, traçabilité, conditions de travail.
  • Un rapport d'audit est formalisé et archivé. Les écarts identifiés font l'objet d'un plan d'actions correctives avec suivi.
  • Pour les commandes en dessous du seuil, un audit documentaire à distance peut être accepté sous validation du Quality Role.

## 1.2 Core Business Activities

Plus Sarl acts as a qualified intermediary between European B2B customers and international industrial suppliers. The company's activities include:

- **Sourcing**: Identification, selection and qualification of international industrial suppliers
- **Monitoring**: On-site monitoring and quality control of outsourced production
- **Quality control**: Structured inspection program (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check)
- **Logistics coordination**: International shipping, customs clearance, delivery management
- **Customer relations**: Order management, technical liaison, after-sales support

## 1.3 Organizational Note — Micro-Enterprise Structure

As a micro-enterprise with a single employee, Plus Sarl's QMS is designed to be **proportionate** to the organization's size and complexity. The managing director assumes all functional roles defined in the QMS. Roles are defined independently of individuals to allow for future growth. The competency matrix (FM-PS02-MCO) documents how each role is covered.

# 2. QMS Scope and Exclusions

## 2.1 Scope Statement

The Quality Management System of Plus Sarl covers all activities related to **industrial monitoring and worldwide sourcing**, including:

- Sales management and customer relations
- Selection, qualification and evaluation of international suppliers
- Monitoring and quality control of outsourced production
- Logistics coordination and international shipping
- Document management and support processes

## 2.2 Exclusions

| Excluded Clause | Justification |
|-----------------|---------------|
| **8.3 — Design and Development** | Plus Sarl does not carry out product design. The company provides sourcing and quality control of products designed by its customers or suppliers. All technical specifications and drawings are customer property. |

No other clauses are excluded.

# 6. Process Chain Architecture

## 6.1 Four Order Types

Plus Sarl handles four distinct order types, each following a specific process chain:

| Chain | Order Type | Description | Key Difference |
|-------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| CHAIN-01 | Existing Product | Repeat order with existing tooling | No technical study needed |
| CHAIN-02 | Tooling Modification | Order requiring modification to existing tools | Technical study for modifications |
| CHAIN-03 | New Tooling | Order requiring new mold/tool creation | Full technical study and validation |
| CHAIN-04 | Sourcing | New supplier search and qualification | Supplier selection process added |

## 6.2 Eight Process Blocks (BLOCs)

All order types are composed of combinations of the following 8 standardized blocks:

| BLOC | Name | Operational Line | Key Activities |
|------|------|-----------------|----------------|
| 001 | Order Reception | Sales (P01) | Receive request, assess feasibility, assign order number |
| 002 | Order Sheet Creation | Sales (P01) | Create detailed order sheet with specifications |
| 003 | Transport Sheet Creation | Delivery (P04) | Plan logistics, shipping mode, customs requirements |
| 004 | Supplier Technical Study | Manufacturing (P02) | Technical feasibility, supplier quotation, timeline |
| 005 | Order Validation | Sales (P01) | Customer confirmation, final pricing, production go-ahead |
| 006 | Production & Quality | Manufacturing (P02) + Quality (P03) | Production monitoring, IPC, DUPRO, PSI inspections |
| 007 | Delivery & Customs | Delivery (P04) + Sales (P01) | Shipping, customs clearance, delivery tracking |
| 008 | Goods Acceptance | Quality (P03) + Sales (P01) | Final inspection, customer delivery, feedback |

## 6.3 Chain Compositions

| # | Pillar | Commitment |

# 10. Key Audit Focus Areas

## 10.1 Critical Audit Points

The following areas are identified as critical given the nature of Plus Sarl's operations:

### 10.1.1 Control of Externally Provided Processes (Clause 8.4)

**Why critical**: 100% of manufacturing is outsourced to international suppliers. This is the core control mechanism of the QMS.

**Evidence to examine**:

| Evidence | Document/Record |
|----------|----------------|
| Supplier selection and evaluation criteria | DOC_Evaluation_Fournisseur, FM-P02-EVA |
| Supplier Quality Agreement | DOC_Accord_Qualite_Fournisseur, FM-P02-SQA |
| Defined requirements communicated to suppliers | FM-P01-CMD (Order Sheets with specifications) |
| Incoming inspection records | FM-P03-IPC, FM-P03-DUPRO, FM-P03-PSI, FM-P03-LC |
| Supplier performance monitoring | FM-P02-EVA (periodic evaluations) |
| Supplier audit program | IT-P03-AUD |

### 10.1.2 Product/Service Release (Clause 8.6)

**Why critical**: Plus Sarl must demonstrate conformity verification before delivery to customers.

**Evidence to examine**:

| Evidence | Document/Record |
|----------|----------------|
| PSI inspection criteria and results | IT-P03-PSI, FM-P03-PSI |
| AQL sampling application | IT-P03-PSI (ISO 2859-1 reference) |
| Release authorization records | FM-P03-PSI (PASS/FAIL decision) |
| Nonconforming product handling | DOC_Non_Conformite, FM-P03-NC |

### 10.1.3 Traceability (Clause 8.5.2)

**Why critical**: Each customer order must be traceable from request through delivery.

**Evidence to examine**:

| Evidence | Document/Record |
|----------|----------------|
| Order numbering and tracking | FM-P01-CMD |
| Production linkage to order | BLOC 006 workflow |
| Inspection linkage to order | FM-P03-IPC/DUPRO/PSI (order reference) |
| Delivery linkage to order | FM-P04-TRN, POD (Proof of Delivery) |

### 10.1.4 Micro-Enterprise Considerations

| Aspect | How Addressed |
|--------|---------------|
| Single-person managing all roles | Roles defined independently of individuals; competency matrix (FM-PS02-MCO) documents coverage |
| Internal audit independence | External internal auditor required (consultant or quality network member) |
| Management review formality | Documented review meeting (even if conducted alone); minutes recorded in FM-PM01-RD |
| Awareness (7.3) | Not separately documented for a single-person organization; awareness is inherent |

## 10.2 Inspection Program Overview

Plus Sarl implements a **four-stage inspection program** aligned with international trade practices:

| Stage | Timing | Purpose | Work Instruction |
|-------|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **IPC** — Initial Production Check | Start of production | Verify raw materials, initial samples, setup | IT-P03-IPC |
| **DUPRO** — During Production | 20-80% completion | Monitor quality during production run | IT-P03-DUPRO |
| **PSI** — Pre-Shipment Inspection | Production complete, before packing | Final quality verification per AQL | IT-P03-PSI |
| **LC** — Loading Check | Container loading | Verify correct loading, quantities, packing | IT-P03-LC |

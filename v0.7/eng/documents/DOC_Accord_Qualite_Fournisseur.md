## Preamble

This Supplier Quality Agreement (SQA) establishes the quality requirements that **Plus Sarl** imposes on its suppliers within the scope of its international industrial monitoring and sourcing activities. It constitutes a mutual commitment between Plus Sarl and the supplier to guarantee the conformity of the products and services provided.

## 3. Quality Requirements

### 3.1 Conformity to Specifications

The Supplier commits to:
- [ ] Deliver products conforming to the technical specifications provided by Plus Sarl
- [ ] Comply with the standards and regulations applicable to the product and destination country
- [ ] Provide the required certificates of conformity and test reports
- [ ] Inform Plus Sarl of any change in materials, processes, or subcontractors

### 3.2 Quality Management System

The Supplier commits to:
- [ ] Maintain a documented quality control system
- [ ] Authorize Plus Sarl inspections (IPC, DUPRO, PSI, Loading Check)
- [ ] Make premises and documentation available for audits
- [ ] Retain quality records for a minimum period of 3 years

### 3.3 Packaging and Marking

The Supplier commits to:
- [ ] Package products in accordance with specified requirements
- [ ] Ensure marking complies with the regulations of the destination country
- [ ] Protect products against damage during transport
- [ ] Provide the required accompanying documents (packing list, certificates)

### 3.4 Traceability

The Supplier commits to:
- [ ] Ensure product traceability from raw materials
- [ ] Uniquely identify each production batch
- [ ] Retain traceability records for a minimum of 5 years

## 4. Nonconformity Management

### 4.1 Detection and Notification

- The Supplier must notify Plus Sarl of any detected nonconformity within **24 hours**
- Plus Sarl will notify the Supplier of any nonconformity identified during inspections

### 4.2 Nonconformity Handling

| Action                              | Responsible    | Deadline       |
|-------------------------------------|----------------|----------------|
| Acknowledgment of receipt of NC     | Supplier       | 24 hours       |
| Root cause analysis (8D / 5 Whys)   | Supplier       | 5 working days |
| Corrective action plan              | Supplier       | 10 working days|
| Implementation of corrections       | Supplier       | Per plan       |
| Effectiveness verification          | Plus Sarl      | Next inspection|

### 4.3 Consequences of Repeated Nonconformities

In case of recurring nonconformities, Plus Sarl reserves the right to:
- Increase the inspection level
- Downgrade the supplier (A/B/C classification)
- Suspend or terminate the business relationship

---

## 5. Inspections and Audits

### 5.1 Right of Inspection

Plus Sarl reserves the right to carry out the following inspections:

| Inspection type   | Description                             | Required notice |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------|----------------|
| IPC               | Initial Production Check                | 48 hours       |
| DUPRO             | During Production Check                 | 48 hours       |
| PSI               | Pre-Shipment Inspection                 | 48 hours       |
| Loading Check     | Loading control                         | 24 hours       |
| Supplier audit    | Quality system audit                    | 15 days        |

### 5.2 Supplier Obligations During Inspections

The Supplier commits to:
- Grant access to production and storage premises
- Make a qualified representative available
- Provide requested documents and records
- Not ship goods before release by Plus Sarl (if applicable)

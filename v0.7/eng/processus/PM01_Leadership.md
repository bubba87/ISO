## 1. Purpose and scope

This process defines the strategic steering activities of the Quality Management System (QMS) of **Plus Sarl**, an international industrial monitoring and sourcing company. It covers the definition of the quality policy, planning of objectives, allocation of resources, conduct of management reviews, risk management, monitoring of indicators and steering of continual improvement.

## 5. Description of activities

### A1 - Definition and communication of the quality policy

Management defines the quality policy aligned with the strategy of Plus Sarl. It ensures its communication to all staff and its adequacy with the international context of the company.

### A2 - Planning of quality objectives

Management establishes measurable quality objectives, consistent with the quality policy. These objectives are broken down by process and monitored quarterly (see FM-PM01-OBJ).

### A3 - Allocation and management of resources

Management identifies and allocates the necessary resources (human, material, financial, informational) for the effective operation of the QMS and international operations.

### A4 - Conduct of management reviews

Management conducts planned management reviews (minimum 2 per year) according to the FM-PM01-RD format, incorporating all input data required by the ISO 9001:2015 standard.

### A5 - Risk and opportunity management

Management leads the identification, assessment and treatment of risks and opportunities related to the context of the organisation, including risks related to international sourcing and monitoring operations.

### A6 - Performance indicator monitoring

Management reviews the key performance indicator (KPI) dashboards for each process, analyses trends and triggers necessary actions.

### A7 - Steering of continual improvement

Management identifies improvement opportunities, approves action plans, monitors their implementation and evaluates their effectiveness.

## 8. Key Performance Indicators (KPI)

| Indicator                                 | Formula / Method                                | Target         | Frequency    |
|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Quality objectives achievement rate       | Objectives achieved / Objectives set x 100     | >= 80 %        | Quarterly    |
| Management review completion              | Reviews conducted / Reviews planned            | 100 %          | Annual       |
| Critical risk treatment rate              | Risks treated / Risks identified x 100         | >= 90 %        | Semi-annual  |
| Corrective action closure time            | Average closure time                           | < 30 days      | Quarterly    |
| Overall interested party satisfaction     | Average satisfaction score                     | >= 4/5         | Annual       |
| Continual improvement rate                | Improvement actions closed / launched x 100    | >= 75 %        | Semi-annual  |

## 10. Continual improvement

Improvement opportunities are identified through:
- Management reviews
- Internal and external audits
- Analysis of performance indicators
- Feedback from customers and interested parties
- Staff suggestions

Each improvement action is recorded, planned, monitored and evaluated for effectiveness.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This process defines the activities related to competence management within **Plus Sarl**. It covers the identification of competence needs, assessment of existing competences, planning and delivery of training, and evaluation of training effectiveness.

This process applies to all roles defined in the Plus Sarl QMS.

## 5. Description of activities

### A1 - Identification of competence needs

Management, in collaboration with process owners, identifies the competences required for each QMS role. Needs are determined based on:

| Source                          | Type of need                                                |
|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Process sheets                  | Technical competences required per process                   |
| Normative developments          | New competence requirements (ISO, regulatory)               |
| Audit results                   | Competence gaps identified                                  |
| Customer feedback               | Additional competences required                             |
| Projects and developments       | New competences linked to business evolution                |

### A2 - Assessment of existing competences

Competence assessment is carried out using the competence matrix (FM-PS02-MCO). Each competence is assessed at 3 levels:

| Level      | Code | Description                                                          |
|------------|------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Expert     | E    | Full mastery, ability to train others, complete autonomy             |
| Sufficient | S   | Operational mastery, autonomy in routine situations                  |
| Basic      | B    | Fundamental knowledge, requires guidance                             |

### A3 - Gap analysis and planning

| Action                          | Description                                              | Responsible        |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Gap analysis                    | Comparison of required vs. current competences           | Quality Role       |
| Prioritisation                  | Ranking of gaps by criticality for the QMS               | Management         |
| Training plan                   | Definition of training actions with deadlines            | Management         |
| Resource allocation             | Budget and time allocated to training                    | Management         |

### A4 - Delivery of competence development actions

| Type of action                  | Description                                              | Examples                           |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| External training               | Training delivered by an external organisation           | ISO 9001, internal audit, AQL     |
| Internal training               | Knowledge transfer between staff members                 | Mentoring, coaching               |
| Self-study                      | Supervised autonomous learning                           | E-learning, technical documentation|
| On-the-job training             | Practical guidance at supplier site                      | Accompanied inspection            |
| Event participation             | Trade fairs, seminars, professional conferences          | Industrial fairs, webinars        |

### A5 - Evaluation of training effectiveness

| Evaluation level               | Method                                                   | Timeframe           |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Reaction (satisfaction)         | Post-training satisfaction questionnaire                 | Immediate           |
| Learning (knowledge)            | Knowledge test or quiz                                   | End of training     |
| Transfer (application)          | Observation in work situation                            | 1 to 3 months      |
| Results (impact)                | Measurement of improvement in relevant indicators        | 3 to 6 months      |

### A6 - Competence matrix update

The competence matrix is updated:
- After each training completed
- During the annual assessment
- In case of role change or scope evolution

## 10. Key Performance Indicators (KPI)

| Indicator                                 | Formula / Method                                    | Target         | Frequency    |
|-------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Training plan completion rate             | Training completed / Training planned x 100         | >= 80 %        | Annual       |
| Critical competence coverage rate         | Competences >= S / Critical competences required x 100 | >= 90 %     | Annual       |
| Training effectiveness rate               | Effective training / Training evaluated x 100       | >= 85 %        | Annual       |
| Training hours per person                 | Total training hours / Number of staff              | >= 16h/year    | Annual       |
| Matrix completion rate                    | Cells assessed / Total cells x 100                  | 100 %          | Semi-annual  |
| Critical gap closure time                 | Average gap treatment duration                      | <= 6 months    | Annual       |

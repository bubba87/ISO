## 2.1 Presentation of Plus Sarl

Plus Sarl is a company specializing in **industrial monitoring and worldwide sourcing**. The company acts as a qualified intermediary between its customers and an international network of industrial suppliers, ensuring the conformity, quality and traceability of sourced products.

### Areas of Activity

| Area                           | Description                                                                 |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Worldwide industrial sourcing  | Identification and qualification of international suppliers                 |
| Production monitoring          | Tracking and quality control of outsourced production                       |
| Inspection and quality control | Performing inspections at various stages of production                      |
| Consulting and support         | Technical and logistical assistance for procurement operations              |

---

## 2.2 Outsourced Production Model

Plus Sarl operates according to a **fully outsourced production model**. The company does not own its own manufacturing facilities. Its role consists of:

1. **Selecting** the most suitable suppliers for customer requirements
2. **Qualifying** the production capabilities of industrial partners
3. **Supervising** critical manufacturing stages
4. **Controlling** product quality before shipment
5. **Coordinating** international logistics

This model requires rigorous control of outsourced processes in accordance with the requirements of the ISO 9001:2015 standard, clause 8.4.

## 2.6 Request Analysis Workflow

The customer request analysis process follows these steps:

### Step 1: Request Reception
- The **Commercial Role** receives the customer request
- Registration in the management system
- Assignment of a unique reference number

### Step 2: Feasibility Analysis
- Evaluation of technical specifications
- Verification of qualified supplier availability
- Analysis of logistics and lead time constraints
- Consultation with the **Purchasing Role** and **Quality Role** as needed

### Step 3: Requirements Review

| Review Element                 | Responsible        | Validation Criterion                      |
|--------------------------------|--------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Technical specifications       | Quality Role       | Compliance with applicable standards       |
| Supplier capability            | Purchasing Role    | Qualified supplier available               |
| Lead times                     | Logistics Role     | Consistency with customer expectations     |
| Commercial conditions          | Commercial Role    | Profitability and acceptable conditions    |
| Regulatory requirements        | Quality Role       | Compliance with applicable regulations     |

### Step 4: Quotation and Validation
- Issuance of the commercial offer by the **Commercial Role**
- Possible negotiation with the customer
- Final validation and order acceptance

### Step 5: Operational Launch
- Transfer of the file to the **Purchasing Role** for supplier order placement
- Inspection planning by the **Quality Role**
- Logistics organization by the **Logistics Role**

## 2.7 Commitments to Customers

Plus Sarl commits to its customers on the following points:

1. **Transparency**: clear and regular communication on order progress
2. **Quality**: rigorous control of product conformity to specifications
3. **Responsiveness**: prompt handling of requests and complaints
4. **Traceability**: complete documentation of each process step
5. **Continual improvement**: systematic consideration of customer feedback

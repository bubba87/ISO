## 1. Purpose

This work instruction describes the procedure for planning and conducting **supplier audits** (on-site or remote) to evaluate the supplier's quality management system, production capabilities, and compliance with Plus Sàrl requirements. Audit results feed into the supplier evaluation process (FM-P02-EVAL).

---

## 2. Scope

This instruction applies to:
- Initial qualification audits for new suppliers
- Periodic surveillance audits for active suppliers
- Special audits triggered by quality issues or customer complaints

---

## 3. Audit Types

| Type                     | Trigger                                              | Frequency               |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Qualification audit**  | New supplier before first order                      | Once (before qualification) |
| **Surveillance audit**   | Active supplier (per annual program)                 | Annual for Class A/B suppliers |
| **Special audit**        | Recurring NCs, customer complaint, supplier change   | As needed               |
| **Remote audit**         | Distance / cost constraints, preliminary assessment  | As needed               |

---

## 4. Preparation

| Step | Action                                   | Responsible   | Documents                     |
|------|------------------------------------------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| 1    | Define audit scope and objectives        | Quality Role  | Annual audit program          |
| 2    | Notify supplier (minimum 2 weeks)        | Quality Role  | Audit notification letter     |
| 3    | Review supplier file                     | Quality Role  | FM-P02-EVAL, NC history, SQA  |
| 4    | Prepare audit checklist                  | Quality Role  | FM-P03-AUD template           |
| 5    | Plan logistics (travel, access)          | Quality Role  | Travel arrangements           |
| 6    | Confirm agenda with supplier             | Quality Role  | Audit plan                    |

---

## 5. Audit Procedure

### 5.1 Opening Meeting

| Action                               | Description                                          |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Introduce audit team                 | Names, roles, responsibilities                       |
| Confirm audit scope                  | Processes, areas, and products to be audited         |
| Confirm schedule                     | Timing, access requirements, key contacts            |
| Explain methodology                  | Interviews, observations, document reviews           |
| Address confidentiality              | NDA provisions, photo permissions                    |

### 5.2 Audit Execution

#### A. Quality Management System Assessment

| Check Point                          | Method                                               | Reference               |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Quality policy and objectives        | Document review, interviews                          | ISO 9001 §5.2, §6.2     |
| Document and record control          | Document review, traceability check                  | ISO 9001 §7.5           |
| Management commitment                | Interview with management                            | ISO 9001 §5.1           |
| Internal audit program               | Document review                                      | ISO 9001 §9.2           |
| NC and corrective action management  | Record review, examples                              | ISO 9001 §10.2          |

#### B. Production Capability Assessment

| Check Point                          | Method                                               | Acceptance Criteria              |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Production equipment                 | Visual inspection, maintenance records               | Adequate, well-maintained        |
| Production capacity                  | Interview, records                                   | Sufficient for order volume      |
| Process control                      | Observation, records                                 | Parameters monitored             |
| Personnel competence                 | Training records, interviews                         | Trained and qualified            |
| Work environment                     | Visual inspection (5S, cleanliness, safety)          | Clean, organized, safe           |

#### C. Quality Control Assessment

| Check Point                          | Method                                               | Acceptance Criteria              |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Incoming inspection                  | Observation, records                                 | Performed systematically         |
| In-process inspection                | Observation, records                                 | Defined checkpoints              |
| Final inspection                     | Observation, records                                 | Per specifications               |
| Inspection equipment                 | Calibration records                                  | Calibrated and valid             |
| NC product segregation               | Visual check                                         | Clearly identified / isolated    |
| Traceability system                  | Verification exercise                                | Effective batch traceability     |

#### D. Logistics and Packaging Assessment

| Check Point                          | Method                                               | Acceptance Criteria              |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Storage conditions                   | Visual inspection                                    | Clean, organized, FIFO           |
| Packaging process                    | Observation                                          | Adequate product protection      |
| Labeling accuracy                    | Verification                                         | Correct and complete             |
| Shipping documentation               | Document review                                      | Complete and accurate            |

### 5.3 Closing Meeting

| Action                               | Description                                          |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Present preliminary findings         | Strengths, NCs, observations, opportunities          |
| Discuss major findings               | Ensure supplier understanding                        |
| Agree on corrective action timeline  | Deadlines for corrective actions                     |
| Thank supplier                       | Professional closure                                 |

---

## 6. Finding Classification

| Finding Type         | Definition                                           | Required Action                  |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Major NC**         | Systemic failure or absence of a required process    | Corrective action within 30 days |
| **Minor NC**         | Isolated deviation from a requirement                | Corrective action within 60 days |
| **Observation**      | Improvement opportunity without deviation            | Recommendation                   |
| **Strength**         | Good practice worth highlighting                     | Capitalization                   |

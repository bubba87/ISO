## Complaint handling principles at Plus Sàrl

| Item | Applicable rule |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledgement deadline** | Every complaint is handled regardless of when it was reported by the customer |
| **Handling process** | Identify the cause, implement corrective actions, ensure customer satisfaction |
| **Product replacement** | Products are replaced when the non-conformity is attributable to Plus Sàrl or its suppliers |
| **Financial terms** | Discussed with the supplier (Yuyao Mould Factory or second partner) depending on the origin of the problem |
| **Responsible** | Roxane Wicky (manager — sole employee) |

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Record EVERY complaint, even verbal ones (by phone or WeChat)
> 2. Acknowledge receipt to the customer within 24-48 hours
> 3. Handle critical complaints as a priority
> 4. Notify the relevant Chinese partner if the complaint relates to manufacturing
> 5. Discuss financial terms with the supplier depending on the origin of the problem
> 6. Products are replaced when the NC is attributable to Plus Sàrl or its suppliers
> 7. The absence of complaints is used as a customer satisfaction indicator (see FOR-SAT-001)
> 8. Present complaint indicators during the management review (FOR-RDR-001)
> 9. Every complaint is handled regardless of when it was reported — no statute of limitations

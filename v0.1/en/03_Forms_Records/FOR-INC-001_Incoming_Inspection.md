## Context and adaptation

Within the scope of Plus Sàrl's industrial coordination activities, products manufactured in China are shipped directly to European customers. Plus Sàrl therefore does not carry out physical incoming inspection. "Incoming inspection" is adapted as follows:

| Conventional inspection | Plus Sàrl adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical inspection upon receipt | Verification of transport and shipping documents |
| Visual inspection of products | Shipment tracking (online tools, carrier contact) |
| Dimensional inspection | Confirmation of receipt by the customer |
| Acceptance/rejection decision | Absence of complaint = implicit conformity (cf. quality manual section 8.6) |
| NC raised if defect found | If customer complaint: NC process triggered (PRO-NCF-001) |

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Complete a form for EVERY delivery shipped from China to a customer
> 2. Verify the transport documents as soon as the export documents are received
> 3. Track the shipment and follow up with the carrier in case of delay
> 4. Obtain the customer's confirmation of receipt (email, phone)
> 5. In the absence of a complaint within a reasonable period, conformity is implicit
> 6. If the customer reports a problem, immediately raise an NC form (PRO-NCF-001)
> 7. These forms are key evidence for the auditor — they demonstrate monitoring despite the absence of physical receipt
> 8. The planned visit to China will strengthen upstream quality control (cf. quality manual section 8.5)

## 1. Annual quality objectives

The quality objectives of Plus Sarl are defined in alignment with the quality policy and the industrial coordination activities between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. These objectives reflect the four strategic priorities of the company.

---

### Objective 1 - On-time delivery performance

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Meet delivery deadlines according to the mode of transport used |
| **Consistent with** | Quality policy - Commitment no. 4 (monitoring of production, transport and delivery operations) |
| **Indicator** | Deviation between the actual delivery date and the target date communicated to the client |
| **Current value** | To be measured - first year: establish baseline |
| **Sea/rail transport target** | +/- 10 days from target date |
| **Air transport target** | +/- 3 days from target date |
| **Responsible** | Roxane Wicky (Managing Director) |
| **Resources** | Order tracking in FileMaker, shipment tracking, coordination with freight forwarders |
| **Deadline** | 31/12/2026 |

### Objective 2 - Product conformity and quality

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Ensure conformity of delivered products and react quickly in case of non-conformity |

| **Indicator 1** | Number of non-conformities (NCs) recorded per period |
| **Indicator 2** | Replacement lead time for non-conforming products according to the client's need |

## 3. Objectives review

Quality objectives are reviewed:
- **Quarterly**: indicator monitoring, trend identification, corrective actions if needed
- **Annually**: full review during the management review (M3), target adjustment for the following year

Results are presented during the management review and serve as a basis for planning continuous improvement actions.

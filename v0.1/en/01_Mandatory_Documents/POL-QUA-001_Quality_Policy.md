## Plus Sarl - Quality Policy

### Our mission

Plus Sarl specializes in industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The business covers the design, development and production of industrial parts, as well as sourcing of existing parts. Moulds are developed and stored in China, at our main partner Yuyao Mould Factory. All operations are coordinated from our office in Cudrefin, Switzerland.

### Management commitment

The director is committed to implementing, maintaining and continuously improving a Quality Management System in compliance with the ISO 9001:2015 standard. The company's business is based on industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturing partners. The priorities are: customer satisfaction, product conformity, lead time management and logistics reliability.

### Our commitments

The director personally commits to:

1. **Handle all customer requests** with responsiveness and rigour.

2. **Ensure the consistency of commercial offers** and maintain price control.

3. **Effectively coordinate industrial partners** to guarantee product quality and conformity.

4. **Monitor production, transport and delivery operations** to ensure compliance with deadlines and requirements.

5. **Manage non-conformities** and implement preventive actions.

6. **Protect the confidentiality** of technical information and customers' intellectual property.

7. **Continuously improve** the organization, methods and performance of the QMS.

### Our strategic quality priorities

- Achieve a conformity rate of delivered parts exceeding 95%
- Guarantee on-time delivery performance above 90%
- Reduce non-conformities and customer complaints
- Strengthen the quality partnership with our Chinese suppliers (Yuyao Mould Factory and second partner)
- Ensure control of the international logistics chain (air, sea, rail)

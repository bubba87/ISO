## 1. Company overview

### General description

Plus Sarl is a Swiss company founded in 2007, with its registered office at Route de Montet 11, 1588 Cudrefin, Switzerland. The company, incorporated as a Sarl, has 1 employee (the managing director) and 3 partners: Roxane Wicky, Olav Wicky and Capucine Wicky.

The main activity of Plus Sarl is industrial coordination between European clients and Chinese manufacturers, specializing in the development of moulds for plastic parts injection and parts manufacturing. The company provides the link between the needs of its European clients and the production capacity of its partners in China.

Plus Sarl works with two Chinese manufacturing partners:

## 2. Context analysis (SWOT)

### External issues (section 4.1 of the context)

- Dependence on Chinese industrial partners for tooling and production
- International transport lead times and logistics constraints
- Customs and regulatory requirements for import/export
- Variations in production costs, transport costs and raw material prices
- High customer expectations regarding quality, technical conformity and on-time delivery

### Internal issues (section 4.1 of the context)

- One-person organization requiring strong priority management and planning
- Need for fluid and continuous communication with clients, suppliers and logistics partners
- Maintaining a high level of documentary, technical and organizational control
- Ability to guarantee responsiveness, confidentiality and lasting customer satisfaction

## 5. Risk and opportunity analysis

### 5.1 Identified risks (section 6.1)

#### Sales process (customer relationship)

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| O2 | ISO 9001 certification = access to new markets | Opportunity | High | High | **High** | Obtain and maintain certification, communicate to prospects |

#### Purchasing / Subcontracting process (Chinese partners)

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| # | Risk / Opportunity | Type | Probability | Impact | Level | Planned action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R4 | Significant international transport delays | Risk | Medium | High | **High** | Selection and re-evaluation of carriers based on their reliability; safety margins in lead times |
| R5 | Risks related to customs formalities | Risk | Low | Medium | **Low** | Level considered manageable; rigorous monitoring of documentary compliance |
| O4 | Optimization of delivery lead times | Opportunity | Medium | High | **High** | Continuous improvement of logistics monitoring, regular evaluation of carriers |

### 5.2 Summary of mitigation actions

| Risk | Key actions |
|---|---|
| Chinese partner failure | Close communication, strengthened production monitoring, second partner available |
| International transport delays | Selection and re-evaluation of carriers based on their reliability |
| Major quality defects | Rapid handling of NCs, replacement of defective parts, strengthened inspections for subsequent production runs |
| Loss of a major client | Maintaining a high level of trust and service to retain clients |
| Customs formalities | Level considered manageable; rigorous documentary monitoring |

### 5.3 Evaluation grid

**Probability:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3)

**Impact:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3) / Very high (4)

**Risk level = Probability x Impact**
- 1-2: Low (acceptable)
- 3-4: Medium (monitor)
- 6+: High (actions required)

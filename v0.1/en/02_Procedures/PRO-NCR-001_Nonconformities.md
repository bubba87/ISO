## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for detection, recording, treatment and follow-up of nonconformities (NCs) related to products (moulds and plastic parts), processes and the QMS of Plus Sarl.

## 2. Scope

All nonconformities detected:
- By customers after delivery (complaints) -- main source of detection
- During shipment monitoring (transport damage, delays)
- During internal audits
- During routine QMS operations

> **Note:** Plus Sarl does not physically receive the products. Goods are shipped directly from China to European customers. Nonconformities are therefore mainly detected by the customers themselves after receiving the products.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Nonconformity (NC)** | Non-fulfilment of a requirement (customer, standard, regulatory, internal) |
| **Major NC** | NC having a significant impact on product quality or customer satisfaction |
| **Minor NC** | Isolated NC, limited impact |
| **Correction** | Immediate action to eliminate the detected NC (symptom treatment) |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of the NC and prevent its recurrence (see PRO-ACR-001) |
| **Concession** | Authorisation to use a nonconforming product under conditions (customer agreement) |

## 5. NC Detection Sources

| Source | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Customer complaint** | Customer reports a defect, a delay, a quantity error, an appearance issue |
| **Transport damage** | Products damaged during transport (air, sea, rail) |
| **Production defect** | Parts not conforming to specifications (dimensions, appearance, material) detected by the customer |
| **Delivery delay** | Production or transport deadline not met |
| **Internal audit** | Audit finding (major or minor NC) |
| **Certification audit** | Finding by the certification body |
| **Chinese partner feedback** | Issue reported during manufacturing by Yuyao Mould Factory or the second partner [name TO BE CONFIRMED] |
| **Routine operations** | Order error, oversight, missing document |

> **Important:** All complaints are handled regardless of the date on which they are reported by the customer.

### 6.2 Step Details

**Step 1 - Record the NC**

Complete the nonconformity sheet with:
- Unique number (NC-[YEAR]-[NNN], e.g.: NC-2026-001)
- Detection date (date of customer complaint or finding)
- Detection source (customer complaint, transport damage, etc.)
- Precise description of the NC
- Product/process concerned
- Order/project reference (FileMaker reference)

**Step 2 - Analyse the Situation**

The managing director analyses the situation by assessing:
- Severity: Major / Minor
- Impact on the customer and their satisfaction
- Probable origin of the problem (production defect, transport, order error)
- Whether other orders/customers are potentially affected

**Step 3 - Decide on Treatment**

### 6.3 Treatment Options for Nonconforming Products

| Option | Description | When to use | Customer agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Replacement** | Relaunch production and ship new conforming products | Preferred option -- ensure customer satisfaction | Not required |
| **Concession (acceptance by the customer)** | The customer accepts the product despite the NC | Minor NC, customer accepts as-is | **Mandatory** |
| **Modification / rework** | Product modified if technically feasible | Correctable defect | To be assessed with the customer |
| **Product left with the customer** | Defective product left with the customer (scrap or secondary use) | Return not economical | Customer agreement |
| **Product retrieval** | Defective product retrieved from the customer | If necessary for analysis or return | Not required |

> **Guiding principle:** The primary objective is to ensure customer satisfaction. As a general rule, nonconforming products are replaced.

**Step 4 - Implement**

- Apply the decision (launch replacement, organise modification, etc.)
- Document the actions taken
- Communicate with the customer on timelines and proposed solutions

**Step 5 - Coordinate with the Supplier**

- Inform the Chinese partner (Yuyao Mould Factory or the second partner [name TO BE CONFIRMED]) of the NC
- Discuss financial terms based on the origin of the problem:
  - If production defect: borne by the Chinese partner
  - If transport damage: claim against the carrier
  - Other cases: case-by-case negotiation
- Ask the partner to retain NC information for future production runs

**Step 6 - Verify**

- Confirm that the treatment is effective (replacement delivered, customer satisfied)
- Ensure that the Chinese partner has duly taken the NC into account for upcoming production runs

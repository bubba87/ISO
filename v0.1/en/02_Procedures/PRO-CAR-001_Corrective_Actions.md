## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for root cause analysis, implementation and verification of the effectiveness of corrective actions in order to eliminate the causes of nonconformities and prevent their recurrence within the Plus Sarl QMS.

## 2. Scope

Any nonconformity requiring a corrective action:
- Major NCs (systematically)
- Recurring minor NCs
- Internal or external audit NCs
- Significant or repetitive customer complaints
- Any situation where the managing director deems it necessary to act on the causes

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Correction** | Action to eliminate a detected NC (immediate symptom treatment) |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the **cause** of a NC and prevent its recurrence |
| **Root cause** | The fundamental cause at the origin of the NC |
| **Effectiveness verification** | Check that the corrective action has indeed prevented the recurrence of the NC |

> **Key difference:**
> - Correction = "I replaced the defective parts for the customer" (symptom)
> - Corrective action = "I asked the factory to implement reinforced inspection on this reference" (cause)

### 5.2 Step Details

**Step 1 - Open the CA Sheet**

- Assign a number: CA-[YYYY]-[NNN]
- Link to the originating NC (NC-[YYYY]-[NNN])
- Describe the problem

**Step 2 - Analyse the Causes**

Cause analysis is carried out by the managing director within her competence, in coordination with the Chinese partners when the NC is related to manufacturing.

Use the 5 Whys method (recommended for Plus Sarl):

| # | Why? | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why did the NC occur? | [Answer 1] |
| 2 | Why [Answer 1]? | [Answer 2] |
| 3 | Why [Answer 2]? | [Answer 3] |
| 4 | Why [Answer 3]? | [Answer 4] |
| 5 | Why [Answer 4]? | [Answer 5 = probable root cause] |

### 5.3 Typical Corrective Actions for Plus Sarl

The objective of corrective actions is to prevent the recurrence of NCs and to reduce the impact on customers. Below are the most frequent scenarios and associated corrective actions:

#### Scenario 1: Transport Delay

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Delivery delay related to the carrier or freight forwarder |
| **Typical root cause** | Unreliable carrier/freight forwarder, logistics issues |
| **Corrective actions** | - Re-evaluate the carrier/freight forwarder concerned |
| | - Inform the Chinese partners to avoid this service provider in future |
| | - Adapt the logistics choice (change of mode: air/sea/rail) |
| | - Anticipate lead times in communications to customers |

#### Scenario 2: Production Delay

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Manufacturing delay at the Chinese partner |
| **Typical root cause** | Insufficient planning, factory overload, technical issue |
| **Corrective actions** | - Reinforced follow-up with the Chinese partners (more frequent progress reviews) |
| | - Push for better lead times and improved planning |
| | - Anticipate communications to customers in case of delay risk |
| | - Commercial adjustments if necessary (goodwill gesture, staggered delivery) |

#### Scenario 3: Production Defect

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Parts not conforming to specifications (dimensions, appearance, material) |
| **Typical root cause** | Machine setting issue, mould wear, material error, insufficient inspection |
| **Corrective actions** | - Request the factory to retain information on the NC |
| | - Request the implementation of reinforced inspections on future production runs of this reference |
| | - Replacement of nonconforming parts for the customer |
| | - Logistics adaptation if necessary (urgent re-shipment) |
| | - Reinforced partner monitoring (continuous quality monitoring) |
| | - Commercial adjustments if necessary |

> **Guiding principle:** The corrective actions of Plus Sarl aim to prevent recurrence and reduce the impact on the customer. The managing director acts within her competence and relies on her Chinese partners for the technical aspects of manufacturing.

**Step 3 - Identify the Root Cause**

- Select the most probable cause
- Verify through data/evidence if possible (photos, reports, email/WeChat exchanges)

**Step 4 - Define the Corrective Action**

The corrective action must:
- Act on the root cause (not the symptom)
- Be realistic and proportionate to the means of Plus Sarl
- Have an owner and a deadline
- Be communicated to the relevant Chinese partner if necessary

**Step 5 - Implement**

- Carry out the action or coordinate its implementation by the Chinese partner
- Document what has been done
- Communicate actions to the parties concerned (Chinese partner, customer, carrier)

**Step 6 - Verify Effectiveness**

- Allow sufficient time (generally 1 to 3 months or the next orders of the same type)
- Verify that the NC has not recurred during subsequent production runs/deliveries
- If the NC recurs: resume cause analysis, consider stronger measures

> **Completion instructions:**
> 1. Only open a CA if justified (major NC, recurring NC, or audit NC)
> 2. Take the time to properly analyse the root cause -- this is the key step
> 3. The 5 Whys method is simple and effective for a micro-enterprise like Plus Sarl
> 4. Involve the Chinese partners (Yuyao Mould Factory or the second partner) in the analysis when the NC originates from manufacturing
> 5. Document exchanges (emails, WeChat) as evidence of corrective actions
> 6. The auditor will verify that your CAs are effective (not just on paper)
> 7. Cause analysis is carried out within the managing director's competence -- it is a continual improvement process

## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for selection, evaluation and control of suppliers and subcontractors, in particular the Chinese partners who manufacture moulds and injection-moulded plastic parts for the European customers of Plus Sarl.

> **This procedure is CRITICAL for Plus Sarl.** Since manufacturing is entirely
> outsourced to partners in China, the control of this process is essential
> and will be examined in detail by the certification auditor.

## 2. Scope

All purchases and outsourced services having an impact on the quality of products delivered to customers:
- Mould manufacturing (Chinese partners)
- Plastic parts injection moulding (Chinese partners)
- Subcontracting of specialities by the Chinese partners
- International transport (freight forwarder, air/sea/rail carrier)
- Any other supplier having a quality impact

## 6. Partner Selection and Retention

### 6.1 Selection and Retention Criteria

The selection and retention of Plus Sarl partners is based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Importance | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust built over time** | Critical | Long-term relationship, demonstrated reliability |
| **Quality of products delivered** | Critical | Conformity to specifications, low NC rate |
| **On-time delivery** | High | Deliveries within agreed timelines |
| **Technical capability** | High | Suitable equipment, expertise, feasibility |
| **Communication and responsiveness** | Medium | Smooth exchanges by email and WeChat |
| **Price and commercial terms** | Medium | Competitiveness and transparency |

> **Note:** The current partners have been in place since the creation of Plus Sarl in 2007 (Yuyao Mould Factory) and are retained based on mutual trust, product quality and on-time delivery. Performance monitoring is continuous.

### 6.2 Selection of a New Partner (If Necessary)

| # | Step | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identification | Search for potential partners (network, recommendations, trade fairs) |
| 2 | Initial evaluation | Evaluation of technical capability, references, samples |
| 3 | Trial | Test order on a first project |
| 4 | Validation | Validation based on results (quality, lead time, communication) |
| 5 | Integration | Integration into the ordering process |

## 9. Ongoing Partner Evaluation

### 9.1 Continuous Monitoring (Class A Partners)

The performance of Chinese partners is monitored continuously by the managing director:

| Criterion | Monitoring method |
|---|---|
| Delivery quality | Monitoring of customer complaints, NC rate per partner |
| On-time delivery | Comparison of announced lead time vs. actual delivery lead time |
| Communication and responsiveness | Qualitative assessment of email/WeChat exchanges |
| NC management | Timeliness and effectiveness of handling reported NCs |

### 9.2 Formal Periodic Evaluation

| Class | Evaluation frequency |
|---|---|
| A - Critical (Chinese partners) | Annual (formal) + continuous monitoring |
| B - Important (carriers) | Annual (simplified) |
| C - Standard | No formal evaluation |

See form FOR-EVF-001 for details of the annual evaluation.

### 9.3 Actions Based on Results

| Overall score | Assessment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| >= 80% | Performing partner | Maintain the relationship |
| 60-79% | Acceptable partner | Discuss areas for improvement |
| < 60% | Underperforming partner | Improvement plan or search for alternative |

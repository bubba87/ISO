## 1. Introduction

### Why This Certification?

One of your customers requires that their suppliers be ISO 9001 certified. This certification demonstrates that DUMMY Sarl has an organized system to:

- Deliver products that conform to customer requirements
- Control quality despite outsourced manufacturing in China
- Continuously improve its performance

### Is This Feasible for a Micro-Enterprise?

**Yes.** ISO 9001:2015 is designed to be applicable to any size of organization. For a one-person company, the system will naturally be lighter. The standard requires a system that is **proportionate** to the complexity of the organization.

### What the Standard Does NOT Require

- A dedicated quality manager (the managing director assumes this role)
- Hundreds of pages of documentation
- Expensive quality management software
- Procedures for every task

## 2. Understanding ISO 9001:2015

### Structure of the Standard (10 Chapters)

| Chapter | Title | Applicable? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope | Informative |
| 2 | Normative references | Informative |
| 3 | Terms and definitions | Informative |
| **4** | **Context of the organization** | **YES** |
| **5** | **Leadership** | **YES** |
| **6** | **Planning** | **YES** |
| **7** | **Support** | **YES** |
| **8** | **Operation** | **YES** |
| **9** | **Performance evaluation** | **YES** |
| **10** | **Improvement** | **YES** |

### The 7 Quality Management Principles

1. **Customer focus** - Understand and meet customer requirements
2. **Leadership** - Management (you) drives quality
3. **Engagement of people** - Even alone, document how you work
4. **Process approach** - See the company as a set of interrelated processes
5. **Improvement** - Constantly seek to do better
6. **Evidence-based decision making** - Rely on data
7. **Relationship management** - Manage Chinese partner, customers, etc.

## 3. Prerequisites Before Starting

### 3.1 Purchase the Standard

- Purchase ISO 9001:2015 from SNV (Swiss Association for Standardization): www.snv.ch
- Price approximately CHF 180.-
- **Essential**: you must know the exact requirements

### 3.2 Get Trained

Recommended options for Switzerland:
- **SQS** (Swiss Association for Quality and Management Systems): short courses
- **SAQ** (Swiss Association for Quality): "Understanding ISO 9001" courses
- Online training (1-2 days is sufficient for a micro-enterprise)

### 3.3 Management Commitment

Since you are alone, this means:
- Making the formal decision to implement the QMS
- Allocating time (approximately 2-4 hours per week for 6-12 months)
- Budgeting (see section 8)

### 3.4 Conduct an Initial Assessment

Before creating documents, assess where you stand:
- What quality practices already exist (even informal ones)?
- How do you communicate with your Chinese partner?
- How do you control quality upon receipt?
- How do you handle customer complaints?
- How do you track your orders and deliveries?

## 4. The 7 Requirement Chapters

### Chapter 4 - Context of the Organization

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 Context | Identify internal/external issues (SWOT) | CTX-QUA-001 |
| 4.2 Interested parties | List: customers, Chinese partner, shareholders, authorities | CTX-QUA-001 |
| 4.3 Scope | Define the QMS scope | SCP-QUA-001 |
| 4.4 QMS and processes | Create the process map | MAP-QUA-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics:**
- The context must mention the dependency on the Chinese partner
- The scope must cover: mold design, subcontracting management, quality control, delivery
- No chapter can be excluded except 8.3 (design) if you do not design the molds yourself. **Warning**: if you develop the molds (even if manufactured in China), chapter 8.3 applies.

### Chapter 5 - Leadership

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 Leadership and commitment | Draft and communicate the quality policy | POL-QUA-001 |
| 5.2 Quality policy | Formal signed document | POL-QUA-001 |
| 5.3 Roles and responsibilities | Functional organization chart (even for 1 person) | CTX-QUA-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics:**
- You combine roles: management, quality manager, sales, purchasing, inspector
- The organization chart will show functions (not people) and indicate the director assumes all of them
- The quality policy should be short (1 page) and authentic

### Chapter 6 - Planning

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 Risks and opportunities | Risk analysis per process | CTX-QUA-001 |
| 6.2 Quality objectives | Define measurable objectives | OBJ-QUA-001 |
| 6.3 Planning of changes | Simple procedure to manage changes | Integrated in PRO-DOC-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics:**
- Main risks: manufacturing defects in China, delivery delays, loss of Chinese partner, CHF/CNY exchange rate fluctuation
- Measurable objective examples: incoming conformity rate > 95%, delivery on time > 90%, 0 major customer complaints

### Chapter 7 - Support

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 Resources | Identify necessary resources | Integrated in CTX-QUA-001 |
| 7.2 Competence | Document your skills and training | FOR-CMP-001 |
| 7.3 Awareness | Not applicable for 1 person (you are already aware) | - |
| 7.4 Communication | Define how you communicate (internal/external) | Integrated in process sheets |
| 7.5 Documented information | Document control procedure | PRO-DOC-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics:**
- No specific work environment needed (administrative office)
- Monitoring and measuring equipment: if you inspect parts upon receipt, your instruments must be calibrated/verified
- If you only do visual inspection, document it

### Chapter 8 - Operation

This is the core of the system for DUMMY Sarl.

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 8.1 Operational planning | Plan each order/project | Process sheets |
| 8.2 Product/service requirements | Order review process | SHT-PRO-001 |
| 8.3 Design and development | Mold design process | SHT-PRO-001 |
| 8.4 Control of externally provided processes | **CRITICAL**: Chinese partner management | PRO-PUR-001 + FOR-SUE-001 |
| 8.5 Production and service provision | Manufacturing control (through the partner) | SHT-PRO-001 |
| 8.6 Release of products | Incoming inspection before customer delivery | FOR-INC-001 |
| 8.7 Control of nonconforming outputs | Nonconforming mold/part management | PRO-NCR-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics (CRITICAL):**

> **Control of externally provided processes (8.4) is THE most important point of your certification.**

You must demonstrate that:
1. You have selection and evaluation criteria for the Chinese partner
2. You clearly define technical requirements (specifications, drawings)
3. You verify product conformity upon receipt (incoming inspection)
4. You have a quality agreement/contract with your partner
5. You monitor the partner's performance (defect rate, delivery times)

### Chapter 9 - Performance Evaluation

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 9.1 Monitoring and measurement | Quality indicators (KPIs) | OBJ-QUA-001 |
| 9.1.2 Customer satisfaction | Satisfaction survey or measurement | FOR-CSA-001 |
| 9.2 Internal audit | Minimum 1 internal audit per year | PRO-AUD-001 + CHK-AUD-001 |
| 9.3 Management review | Minimum 1 management review per year | FOR-MRV-001 |

**DUMMY Sarl specifics:**
- The internal audit can be performed by an external consultant (you cannot audit yourself)
- The management review is a formal meeting (even alone, document your analysis)
- Customer satisfaction: a simple annual questionnaire or complaint tracking is sufficient

### Chapter 10 - Improvement

**What needs to be done:**

| Requirement | Action for DUMMY Sarl | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 10.1 General | Identify improvement opportunities | Management review |
| 10.2 Nonconformity and corrective action | Handle NCs and prevent recurrence | PRO-NCR-001 + PRO-CAR-001 |
| 10.3 Continual improvement | Demonstrate improvement (KPI trends) | Management review |

## 5. Critical Points for DUMMY Sarl

### 5.1 Control of Subcontracting in China

This is your number one challenge. The auditor will pay particular attention to it.

**Essential actions:**

1. **Formal quality agreement** with the Chinese partner including:
   - Quality requirements (tolerances, materials, finishes)
   - Right to audit or inspect
   - Nonconformity management process
   - Contractual delivery times
   - Confidentiality clause

2. **Specifications** for each mold/part including:
   - Dimensioned technical drawings
   - Material specifications (plastic type, color, etc.)
   - Acceptance criteria
   - Quantities and deadlines

3. **Systematic incoming inspection**:
   - Define the inspections to be carried out (dimensional, visual, functional)
   - Record the results
   - Define acceptance/rejection criteria

4. **Periodic evaluation** of the partner:
   - Delivery conformity rate
   - On-time delivery rate
   - Responsiveness in case of issues
   - Formal annual evaluation

### 5.2 Traceability

- Each customer order must be traceable end-to-end
- From customer request -> specifications -> manufacturing in China -> incoming inspection -> delivery
- A simple Excel spreadsheet or tracking software is sufficient

### 5.3 Design and Development (if applicable)

If you design the molds (even the specifications/drawings):
- Document design inputs (customer requirements)
- Design review (verification of drawings before sending to China)
- Design validation (first sample/prototype)
- Design change management

### 5.4 Communication with China

- Document communication channels (email, WeChat, etc.)
- Keep records of important exchanges
- In case of nonconformity, document corrective exchanges

## 6. Choosing a Certification Body

### Recognized Bodies in Switzerland

| Organization | Website | Note |
|---|---|---|
| SQS | www.sqs.ch | Leader in Switzerland, internationally recognized |
| SGS | www.sgs.com | International group, present in Switzerland |
| Bureau Veritas | www.bureauveritas.ch | International group |
| TUV SUD | www.tuvsud.com | Very well recognized in Europe |
| Kiwa | www.kiwa.com | Present in Switzerland |

### Selection Criteria

- **Price**: request at least 3 quotes
- **International recognition**: important if your customers are European
- **Sector experience**: experience in plastic injection is a plus
- **Proximity**: an auditor in Switzerland reduces costs
- **Accreditation**: verify SAS (Swiss Accreditation Service) accreditation

### Typical Process

1. Request for quote (send your scope and profile)
2. Receive the offer (detailing audit days)
3. Sign the contract
4. Stage 1 audit (document review)
5. Stage 2 audit (on-site audit, 1-2 days for your size)
6. Audit report and certification decision
7. Certificate valid for 3 years with annual surveillance audits

## 7. The Certification Audit Process

### Stage 1 Audit (Document Review)

The auditor verifies:
- QMS documentation is complete
- Quality policy and objectives are defined
- Processes are identified and described
- Mandatory procedures exist

**Estimated duration**: 0.5 day (for your size)

**Possible outcome**: observations and/or remarks to address before Stage 2

### Stage 2 Audit (On-Site)

The auditor verifies on the ground:
- Effective implementation of the QMS
- Compliance with standard requirements
- Process effectiveness
- Records and evidence

**Estimated duration**: 1-1.5 days (for your size)

**Key points the auditor will examine at DUMMY Sarl:**
- How you manage customer orders
- How you transmit requirements to the Chinese partner
- How you control quality upon receipt
- How you manage nonconformities
- How you measure customer satisfaction
- Your records (orders, inspections, deliveries)
- Management review and internal audit

### Types of Findings

| Finding | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Major nonconformity** | Certification refused, must be corrected and verified |
| **Minor nonconformity** | Action plan to submit, verification at next audit |
| **Observation / Improvement opportunity** | No impact on certification, to be considered |

## 9. FAQ

### Q: How long does it take to get certified?
**A:** For a micro-enterprise like DUMMY Sarl, allow 6 to 12 months. With a consultant, it can go faster (6-8 months).

### Q: Do I need a consultant?
**A:** It is not mandatory, but **strongly recommended** for a first-time certification. A good consultant will save you a lot of time and avoid mistakes. Cost: CHF 3,000-8,000 for a micro-enterprise.

### Q: Does the Chinese partner need to be ISO 9001 certified?
**A:** No, this is not a requirement. But YOU must demonstrate that you control this outsourced process. If the partner is certified, it is a positive element for your supplier evaluation.

### Q: Can I audit myself?
**A:** No. The internal auditor must be independent of the audited process. Being alone, you will need to hire an external internal auditor (consultant, member of a quality network, etc.).

### Q: What happens if I fail the audit?
**A:** If major nonconformities are identified, you generally have 90 days to correct them. A supplementary audit will be conducted. This is not a failure, it is the normal process.

### Q: Is the certification internationally recognized?
**A:** Yes, if the certification body is accredited by an IAF (International Accreditation Forum) member. All the bodies listed in this guide are.

### Q: Do I need documents in English?
**A:** No, documents can be in French. However, if your Chinese partner does not speak French, documents exchanged with them will likely be in English.

### Q: What evidence will the auditor request?
**A:** Concrete records: purchase orders, specifications, inspection results, emails with the supplier, processed complaints, management review minutes, internal audit report, etc.

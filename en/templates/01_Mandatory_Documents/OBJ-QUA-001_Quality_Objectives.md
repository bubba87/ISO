> **Filling Instructions:**
> 1. Objectives must be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time-bound
> 2. Adapt target values to your reality (first year establishes the baseline)
> 3. Monitor indicators regularly (at least quarterly)
> 4. Analyze trends and take action if an indicator is drifting
> 5. Present this dashboard at the management review
> 6. Review and adjust objectives each year

## 1. Company Overview

### General Description

DUMMY Sarl is a Swiss company specializing in the development of molds for plastic part injection. The company bridges the needs of its European customers and the production capacity of its partner in China.

### Value Chain

### Evaluation Grid

**Probability:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3)

**Impact:** Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3) / Very High (4)

**Risk Level = Probability x Impact**
- 1-2: Low (acceptable)
- 3-4: Medium (monitor)
- 6+: High (actions required)

## DUMMY Sarl - Quality Policy

### Our Mission

DUMMY Sarl specializes in the development of molds for plastic part injection and the delivery of injected parts to its customers in Switzerland and Europe. Manufacturing is carried out by our partner in China, under our quality control.

### Our Commitments

The management of DUMMY Sarl is committed to:

1. **Satisfying our customers** by delivering molds and plastic parts that conform to their technical requirements, within agreed deadlines.

2. **Controlling our outsourced processes** by ensuring rigorous control of manufacturing carried out by our Chinese partner, from specifications through to incoming inspection.

3. **Meeting applicable requirements** including legal and regulatory requirements related to our products and activities.

4. **Continuously improving** our Quality Management System by setting and monitoring measurable quality objectives.

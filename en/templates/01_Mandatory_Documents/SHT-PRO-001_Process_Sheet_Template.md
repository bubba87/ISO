# Pre-Filled Examples

## Example: Process Sheet O3 - Purchasing and Subcontracting

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Purchasing and Subcontracting |
| **Code** | O3 |
| **Owner** | Director |
| **Purpose** | Ensure molds and parts manufactured by the Chinese partner conform to specifications |

**Activities:**

| # | Activity | Description | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive internal specifications | Receive specifications from design process | Specifications document |
| 2 | Transmit to Chinese partner | Send specifications, drawings and requirements | Email + acknowledgment of receipt |
| 3 | Manufacturing follow-up | Monitor progress (exchanges, photos, milestone reports) | Emails, supplier reports |
| 4 | Pre-shipment validation | Verify partner inspection reports before shipping | Supplier inspection report |
| 5 | Partner evaluation | Evaluate performance (conformity, delivery times) | FOR-SUE-001 |

**Indicators:**

| Indicator | Target |
|---|---|
| Supplier delivery conformity rate | >= 95% |
| Supplier on-time delivery rate | >= 90% |
| Annual supplier evaluation score | >= [X/10] |

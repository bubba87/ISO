## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for detection, recording, treatment and follow-up of nonconformities (NC) related to products (molds and plastic parts), processes and the QMS of DUMMY Sarl.

## 2. Scope

All nonconformities detected:
- Upon receipt of products from the Chinese partner
- By customers (complaints)
- During internal audits
- During routine operation of the QMS

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Nonconformity (NC)** | Non-fulfilment of a requirement (customer, standard, regulatory, internal) |
| **Major NC** | NC having a significant impact on product quality or customer satisfaction |
| **Minor NC** | One-off NC, limited impact |
| **Correction** | Immediate action to eliminate the detected NC (symptom treatment) |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of the NC and prevent its recurrence (see PRO-ACR-001) |
| **Concession** | Authorization to use a nonconforming product under specified conditions |

## 4. Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Who |
|---|---|
| Detect and report NCs | Manager (and customers, Chinese partner) |
| Record NCs | Manager |
| Decide on treatment | Manager |
| Implement corrections | Manager |
| Initiate corrective actions | Manager |
| Monitor closure | Manager |

## 5. Sources of NC Detection

| Source | Examples |
|---|---|
| Incoming inspection | Parts not conforming to specifications (dimensions, appearance, material) |
| Customer complaint | Customer reports a defect, a delay, an error |
| Internal audit | Audit finding (major or minor NC) |
| Certification audit | Finding by the certification body |
| Feedback from Chinese partner | Problem reported during manufacturing |
| Routine operations | Order error, oversight, missing document |

### 6.2 Step Details

**Step 1 - Record the NC**

Complete the nonconformity report with:
- Unique number (NC-[YEAR]-[NNN], e.g.: NC-2026-001)
- Detection date
- Detection source
- Precise description of the NC
- Product/process concerned
- Order/project reference

**Step 2 - Isolate / Secure**

For product NCs:
- Physically isolate the nonconforming product
- Clearly identify it (label "NONCONFORMING")
- Prevent its unintended use or delivery

**Step 3 - Analyze**

- Determine severity: Major / Minor
- Assess the impact on the customer
- Check whether other products/batches are affected

**Step 4 - Decide on treatment**

### 6.3 Treatment Options for Nonconforming Products

| Option | Description | When to use | Customer approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scrap** | Destroy or return the product | Product unusable | Not required |
| **Rework** | Repair to make conforming | Correctable defect | Not required |
| **Regrade** | Use for another purpose | Product usable otherwise | To be evaluated |
| **Concession** | Accept as-is despite the NC | Minor NC, no impact | **Required** |
| **Return to supplier** | Send back to the Chinese partner | Manufacturing NC | Not required |

**Step 5 - Implement**

- Apply the decision
- Document the actions taken
- If returned to supplier: communicate to the Chinese partner (email, report)

**Step 6 - Verify**

- Confirm that the treatment is effective
- Ensure that the product/process is conforming again

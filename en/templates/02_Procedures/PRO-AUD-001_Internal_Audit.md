## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for planning, conducting and following up on internal audits of the Quality Management System (QMS) of DUMMY Sarl, in accordance with clause 9.2 of ISO 9001:2015.

## 2. Scope

All processes of the DUMMY Sarl QMS.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Who |
|---|---|
| Plan the audit programme | Manager |
| Select and appoint the internal auditor | Manager |
| Conduct the audit | **External internal auditor** (mandatory - independence) |
| Approve the audit report | Manager |
| Implement corrective actions | Manager |

> **IMPORTANT**: The manager cannot audit her own processes.
> An external auditor must be appointed (quality consultant, certified auditor,
> member of an auditor exchange network between SMEs).

## 4. Frequency

- **Minimum 1 internal audit per year**, covering all QMS processes
- The audit must take place **before the management review**
- The audit must take place **before the certification/surveillance audit**

## 6. Audit Process

### 6.1 Preparation (before the audit)

| # | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select the auditor (verify competence and independence) | Manager |
| 2 | Define the audit scope and criteria | Manager + Auditor |
| 3 | Communicate the date and programme to the auditor | Manager |
| 4 | Make the QMS documentation available | Manager |
| 5 | Prepare records and evidence | Manager |

### 6.2 Auditor selection criteria

The internal auditor must:
- [ ] Be independent of the processes being audited (must not be the manager)
- [ ] Have knowledge of the ISO 9001:2015 standard
- [ ] Ideally, have internal auditor training (ISO 19011)
- [ ] Be objective and impartial

**Options for DUMMY Sarl:**
- Appointed quality consultant
- Auditor from a partner company (audit exchange between SMEs)
- Independent certified auditor

### 6.3 Conducting the audit (audit day)

| # | Step | Estimated duration | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening meeting | 15 min | Review of scope, method, schedule |
| 2 | Process audit | 4-5h | Examination of documentation, records, interviews |
| 3 | Auditor synthesis | 30 min | Preparation of findings |
| 4 | Closing meeting | 30 min | Presentation of findings, classification, next steps |

### 6.4 After the audit

| # | Action | Deadline | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The auditor writes the audit report | [1-2 weeks] | Auditor |
| 2 | The manager reviews the report | [1 week] | Manager |
| 3 | For each NC: open a corrective action form | [2 weeks] | Manager |
| 4 | Implement the corrective actions | [Per plan] | Manager |
| 5 | Verify the effectiveness of the actions | [1-3 months after] | Manager / Auditor |
| 6 | Include the results in the management review | [Next review] | Manager |

## 7. Classification of Findings

| Type | Definition | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| **Major nonconformity** | Failure to satisfy a requirement that may affect the capability of the QMS | Immediate corrective action required |
| **Minor nonconformity** | Isolated failure to satisfy a requirement, with no system-level impact | Corrective action within an agreed timeframe |
| **Observation** | Potential weakness that could become a nonconformity | To be considered for improvement |
| **Positive point** | Good practice identified | To be maintained and built upon |

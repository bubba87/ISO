## 1. Purpose

Define the rules for selection, evaluation and control of suppliers and subcontractors, in particular the Chinese partner who manufactures the molds and injected plastic parts.

> **This procedure is CRITICAL for DUMMY Sarl.** Since manufacturing is entirely
> outsourced, controlling this process is essential and will be examined in
> detail by the certification auditor.

## 2. Scope

All purchases and outsourced services that impact the quality of products delivered to customers:
- Mold manufacturing (Chinese partner)
- Plastic part injection (Chinese partner)
- International transport (freight forwarder)
- Any other supplier with a quality impact

## 4. Supplier Classification

| Class | Criteria | Examples | Level of Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A - Critical** | Direct impact on product quality | Chinese partner (molds + injection) | Formal annual evaluation, quality agreement, continuous monitoring |
| **B - Important** | Indirect impact on quality or delivery | Carrier, freight forwarder | Simplified annual evaluation |
| **C - Standard** | Low impact on quality | Office supplies, accounting firm | No formal evaluation |

### 7.2 Specification Content

The specifications transmitted to the Chinese partner must contain at minimum:

| Item | Detail | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|
| Project/order reference | Unique number | YES |
| End customer (if authorized) | Name or anonymous reference | Per confidentiality |
| Technical drawings | Dimensioned drawings in [PDF/DWG/STEP] format | YES |
| Material specifications | Plastic type, grade, color, material supplier | YES |
| Dimensional tolerances | Acceptable tolerances | YES |
| Finish and appearance | Surface finish, color, markings | YES |
| Quantities | Number of parts / number of cavities (mold) | YES |
| Desired lead time | Requested delivery date | YES |
| Acceptance criteria | How the product will be inspected upon receipt | YES |
| Packaging requirements | Type of packaging for transport | YES |
| Regulatory requirements | REACH, RoHS, food-grade, etc. | If applicable |
| Expected documents | Dimensional report, material certificate, etc. | YES |

## 8. Periodic Supplier Evaluation

### 8.1 Frequency

| Class | Evaluation Frequency |
|---|---|
| A - Critical | Annual (formal) + continuous monitoring |
| B - Important | Annual (simplified) |
| C - Standard | No formal evaluation |

### 8.2 Evaluation Criteria (Class A)

See form FOR-SUE-001 for details.

| Criteria | Weight | Calculation Method |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery quality | 40% | Conformity rate (conforming lots / total lots) |
| On-time delivery | 30% | On-time delivery rate |
| Responsiveness / Communication | 15% | Subjective rating (1 to 5) |
| NC management | 15% | Processing time and effectiveness of NC handling |

### 8.3 Actions Based on Results

| Overall Score | Assessment | Action |
|---|---|---|
| >= 80% | Performing supplier | Maintain, possibly increase volumes |
| 60-79% | Acceptable supplier | Request improvement plan |
| < 60% | Underperforming supplier | Urgent improvement plan or search for alternative |

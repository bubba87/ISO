---

> **Filling instructions:**
> 1. Complete a form for EACH receipt of products from the Chinese partner
> 2. Adapt inspections to the type of product (mold vs. injected parts)
> 3. Photograph any defects found
> 4. Keep forms together with the supplier's inspection reports
> 5. Have your instruments calibrated at least once a year
> 6. These forms are key evidence for the auditor

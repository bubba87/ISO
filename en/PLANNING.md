# ISO 9001:2015 Certification Planning - DUMMY Sarl

## Estimated Duration: 9-12 months

---

## Overview

```
Month  1-2  : Phase 1 - Preparation and assessment
Month  2-4  : Phase 2 - QMS construction (documentation)
Month  4-6  : Phase 3 - Operational implementation
Month  6-8  : Phase 4 - Running-in and evidence collection
Month  8-9  : Phase 5 - Internal audit and management review
Month  9-10 : Phase 6 - Corrections and improvements
Month 10-12 : Phase 7 - Certification audit
```
